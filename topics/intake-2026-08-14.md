First intake under v4. Four candidates, researched to the evidence bar in
`queue.md` intake rule 2. **None is promotable yet**, for a reason that is worth
stating before the scores: search demand is 35 of 100 and cannot be scored here.

## The blocker: no volume figure

`scoring.md` is explicit — *"Score 0 without a named target query and a volume
figure. Not scoreable on intuition."* The Ahrefs connector is authenticated but
the plan carries no API access; keywords-explorer and even the free
subscription-usage endpoint both return `Insufficient plan`. So every candidate
below is scored **out of 65**, with the 35 left blank rather than estimated.

Each candidate names its exact target query. Pulling seven numbers from the Ahrefs
web UI unblocks the whole slate.

**Also unverified, and load-bearing:** the "validated patterns" in
`docs/growth-strategy.md` — *"how [AI company] makes money" 45–65K/mo*,
*"[company] business model" 35–50K/mo*, *"how Cursor makes money" 5–9K/mo* — are
what the 30–35 point band rests on, and they come from a research synthesis that
has never been checked against a keyword tool. Worth confirming at the same time,
because if they are soft the top band is soft.

## D. Content management and ghostwriting — the operator writing as someone else

**Target queries:** `linkedin ghostwriter` · `ghostwriting rates` · `how to become a ghostwriter`

Proposed by Manav, who is running one of these engagements right now. That is the
difference between this row and the other three: the POV is not recalled, it is
current, and the rates and the workflow are first-hand rather than researched.

The angle the evidence hands us, and it is the thesis line: **writing is the
smallest part of the job.** The fastest-growing segment is AI-assisted
ghostwriting, where a model trained on the client's posting history drafts and

"install it and stand there until it runs" pattern EP001 is built on, applied to
words instead of workflows.
