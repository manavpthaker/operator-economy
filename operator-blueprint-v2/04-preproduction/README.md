# Step 4: preproduction

Status: **governed by `docs/blueprint-cinema.md` (owner decision, 2026-09-24).** No V2 standard of
its own yet. Until one is approved, Blueprint Cinema's production order, contracts and references
(`blueprint-cinema/references/`) are the authority for this stage. They carry no hash-bound
approval gates of the kind Step 3 has.

In Blueprint Cinema terms this stage runs from sequence treatments, shot boards and scene
directions through the whole-episode directed animatic in HyperFrames.

This stage will turn an approved visual translation into production-ready sequence treatments, persistent-world rules, scene direction, asset requirements, an animatic or equivalent rehearsal, and a production plan.
