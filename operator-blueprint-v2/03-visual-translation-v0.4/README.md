## Step boundary

Step 3 decides what the viewer should look at, why it is there, and who carries language. It does
not choose a lens, angle, generated prompt, acting beat, shot duration, edit point, runtime, or
finishing treatment.

Step 4 expands each approved unit into shots. It owns physical action, face function, scene
geography, master/setup relationships, camera, continuity anchors, initial/final images, and the
shot-level mute-test result.
