## Picture/audio doctrine

`narrated_observation` is the OE default. Other modes are used only when their actual language or
sound carrier exists.

| Picture/audio mode used | Where used | Language carrier | What picture carries | Visible-speech boundary | Mute-test expectation | Step 4 handoff constraint |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Required rulings:

- Narrated reality footage is observation, not silent dialogue.
- Generated narrated plates contain no readable speech or unheard answer beat.
- Every new angle in Step 4 must add action, evidence, tension, consequence, human stakes, or a new
  spatial relationship; visual variety alone is not a job.
- Faces are used because an observable event or task gives them a function, not because the frame
  needs emotion.
- Camera movement must be motivated by an event or information change; cinematic drift is not a
  substitute for action.
- Working Model sequences use the locked rough mark language and one focal change at a time.
- Labels identify components and parameters. They do not duplicate narration or display internal
  source-claim IDs without a viewer-facing purpose.

The episode bible may state these constraints. It may not choose the actual action, face function,
angle, lens, setup, camera move, prompt, or edit point; those are Step 4 decisions.
