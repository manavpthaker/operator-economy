## Step 4 reference boundary

Blueprint Cinema is the eventual Step 4 binding layer. Its shot schema, scene directions, prompts,
runtime, and implementation documents are not Step 3 inputs. Step 3 freezes picture/audio intent;
Step 4 expands it into directable shots without changing its meaning.

Motion and sound implementation evidence remain provisional. The audio-first specimen has no
encoded-media verification, and the locked flattened illustration does not prove a motion-ready
asset. This manifest does not upgrade either condition.
