## Asymmetric selection rule

- Compare only the frozen short provider scores. Long-form/pickup is pass/fail confirmation.
- Retain ElevenLabs when it is eligible, passes confirmation, and its short score is within 5.0
  points of Hume or leads Hume.
- Adopt Hume only when its short score is at least 80, more than 5.0 points above ElevenLabs, and it
  passes long-form continuity and pickup. Eleven needs no long-form test when it was more than 5.0
  points behind and therefore did not advance.
- If neither provider reaches 80, select neither and stop before full capture.
- If the comparison is unavailable or the favored provider fails confirmation, select neither
  rather than inventing an exception.
