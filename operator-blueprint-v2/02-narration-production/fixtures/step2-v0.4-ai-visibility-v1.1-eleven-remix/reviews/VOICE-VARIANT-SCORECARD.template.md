## Specific listens

- Does energy come from intention and contrast rather than louder or faster speech?
- Does the dry observation sound noticed rather than performed?
- Does the narrator recover momentum immediately after a caveat?
- Does instruction sound like one experienced operator helping another?
- Does `BUILD` feel earned rather than announced?
- Does the CTA feel warm and decisive rather than promotional?
- Is there over-enunciation, clipped diction, artificial cheerfulness, or a polished synthetic sheen?
