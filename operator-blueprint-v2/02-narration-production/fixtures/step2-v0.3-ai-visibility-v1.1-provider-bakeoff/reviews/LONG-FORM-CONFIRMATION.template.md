# Long-Form Narrator Confirmation

Status: template; cannot start before a completed blind bakeoff and separate authorization

## Why this gate exists

A provider can perform two short excerpts well and still fail across a full argument. This gate
tests whether the winning voice and direction method retain identity, thought movement, energy,
clarity, and acoustic continuity over a materially longer uninterrupted run.

This confirmation is pass/fail. It does not produce or change a provider's numeric score; the
frozen short-round score remains the only number used in the more-than-5.0-point asymmetric
selection rule.

`pass` may support a later N3/N4B production decision. It is not full-capture authorization,
creative approval of an episode, narration lock, or Step 3 authority.
