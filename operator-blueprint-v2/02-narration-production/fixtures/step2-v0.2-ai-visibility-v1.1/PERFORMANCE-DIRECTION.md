## Listener relationship and promise

- Primary listener: a capable professional, builder, or displaced worker looking for a credible business of one in a market they understand.
- Narrator relationship: an experienced operator working through an investment decision across the table, not lecturing or pitching from a stage.
- Central promise: determine whether AI-assisted discovery creates a responsibility one operator can sell, then reduce it to a bounded first diagnostic and validation test.
- Final landing: conviction in the bounded `BUILD` ruling, paired with a clear understanding that market activity is evidence to test the offer, not proof that a customer will buy it.

## Global target

- Begin alert and specific, not theatrical.
- Keep the investigation curious and conversational while evidence accumulates.
- Let conviction increase only after the market layers and operator responsibility have been established.
- Slow materially around reported figures, modeled arithmetic, qualifications, negations, and stop rules.
- Treat dry observations as underreactions to a broken incentive. Do not perform punchlines.
- Keep the operator blueprint peer-level, practical, and more directive than the market investigation.
- Land the verdict and first action firmly, then give the final CTA as a natural completed thought.

Anti-targets: trailer voice, newsreader recital, corporate presentation, synthetic-demo brightness, motivational cadence, constant sarcasm, uniform intensity, or speed-reading.
