## Category-name boundary

Short public category title: Receivables desk for small firms

Short spoken company name: a receivables desk

One-sentence plain definition: It follows up a small firm's late invoices under that firm's name, and a person handles the ones a machine should not touch.

Internal operating description: thesis, "Company naming lock". Not spoken on first listen.

Category status: proposed editorial label. AccountsReceivable.ai uses "AR desk" for its agent product (CLM-020); that is software, not this company.

Approved claim IDs and locators supporting the definition: CLM-005, CLM-019, CLM-017, CLM-023 (the human job as sold); Canvas sections 4, 6 (this design).

What the name and definition may establish: what this proposed company does.

What they do not establish: that buyers know the phrase, buy this model, or that any one-person version exists at disclosed economics.

Prohibited inference: an established category; "the receivables desk market"; collected money implied by the name.

Allowed first spoken wording: "a receivables desk: it follows up a small firm's late invoices under that firm's name, and a person handles the ones a machine should not touch."

Reason the shorter public name remains accurate: "desk" names a staffed job, and the definition says "follows up", never "collects".

## Horizontal-buyer and example registry

Qualified buyer rule: invoices other businesses on terms, regularly carries balances past 30 days that are large beside the fee, has nobody whose job is follow-up, and will let an outside person write to customers under the firm's name.

Unsuitable buyer rule: bills consumers or is paid up front; has AR staff; so few or such sensitive customers that every account would be locked; wants threats, legal pressure, or accounts past the hand-back worked; is happy with a free tool; operates where the state check fails; a small law firm that fits CollBox.
