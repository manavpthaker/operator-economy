## Verdict

Verdict: pass

Evidence class: adjacent synthesis

Failed checks: none

Required research before reconsideration: not applicable. Open desk items that would strengthen the
package: a multi-client agent price (quote), and any buyer record outside law.

Required Canvas disclosure: QuickBooks figures are one vendor's survey. CollBox, Chaser,
AccountsReceivable.ai, ARPilot, and Biller Genie terms are seller terms, and CollBox's named
customers are seller-chosen. AI tools already sell "the AR desk" for $99 to $299 a month, so the
operator is paid for the human part. Every price, capacity, and contribution figure is a modeled
scenario, not observed performance or an earnings forecast. Client trust, price, and results are
the viewer's first test. Some states (Florida and Washington among those read) appear to treat
this work as collection; the viewer checks their own state before the first client.
