## Category-name boundary

Short public category title: Direct-booking recovery

Short spoken company name: a direct-booking practice

One-sentence plain definition: It helps a small hotel get its returning guests to book direct, instead of paying a booking site to meet them again.

Internal operating description: held in the editorial contract and Canvas. Not spoken.

Category status: **proposed editorial label**. "Direct booking" is an established topic in hotel marketing. "A direct-booking practice" as a solo audit-plus-retainer service is not an observed category.

Approved claim IDs supporting the definition: C022 (no verified operator), C011 (agencies sell adjacent scope).

What the name and definition may establish: what the proposed company does for a buyer.

What they do not establish: that buyers recognise or purchase this model, or that it moves direct share.

Prohibited inference: any implication that the practice is an existing market, that the sites are the enemy, or that the property should leave them.

Allowed first spoken wording: "Today we're building a direct-booking practice. It helps a small hotel get its returning guests to book direct, instead of paying a booking site to meet them again."

Reason the shorter public name remains accurate: the mature company's owned result is the direct booking of a returning guest. The three jobs are how.
