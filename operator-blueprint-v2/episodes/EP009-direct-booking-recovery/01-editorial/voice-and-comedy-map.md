## Report and business plan

### Business context

Normal economics or operating logic: rented discovery at a commission per stay.

Popular story: the commission line is what the hotel loses.

Hidden mechanism: the site keeps the contact, the desk has no time, so the inn pays twice. The recoverable slice, not the line, bounds any service.

Buyer consequence: the same introduction fee for the same guest.

Portable operating principle: price a service under what it can recover for the buyer, from the buyer's own numbers.
