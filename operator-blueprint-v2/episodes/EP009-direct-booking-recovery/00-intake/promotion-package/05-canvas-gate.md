## Verdict

Verdict: **pass**

Evidence class: **adjacent synthesis**

Failed checks: none. Go-to-market passes weakly and economics passes on transparency alone.

Required research before reconsideration: none blocking for the gate. Two items carry to editorial and to any promotion record: (1) **open the Booking.com partner pages** behind CLM-005 and CLM-008 in a browser and record what they say on the day; on 2026-09-03 every attempt returned HTTP 403 and the commission mechanics and guest-data policy are currently secondary; (2) **an independent US source for OTA share and commission profile by property size**, or the audit data from validation step 1, before the band's dependence is stated as anything other than a global vendor figure.

Required Canvas disclosure:

1. This is an **adjacent synthesis**. No verified operator was found selling a recovery-first audit-plus-retainer to 20-40-room properties at disclosed economics.
2. **The retainer is bounded by the property's recoverable commission.** At a modeled 20-room property (20 rooms, $180 ADR, 70% occupancy, 63.4% OTA share, 18-22% all-in commission, 10-point mix shift, 3.5-5% direct cost) the net recoverable is roughly $1,000-1,400 a month. The service must price under that line, and the viewer runs the arithmetic on real inputs rather than accepting these.
3. **Every operator-side figure is modeled** — price, capacity, hours, acquisition, contribution — and the model clears an ordinary imputed cost of time only near the top of the band. `Modeled scenario, not observed performance or an earnings forecast.`
4. **The OTA share, cancellation-by-channel, and programme figures are attributed** — Cloudbeds for the dataset, Booking.com and Expedia for their own rules — every time they are used, and the Booking.com pages are opened before lock.
5. **No RevPAR uplift, commission saved, or direct share gained is stated as an observed result.** The vendors' uplift claims are excluded.
6. **The property should not leave OTAs.** The Canvas describes a mix shift, never an exit, and describes guest-data use only inside OTA terms and consent law.
