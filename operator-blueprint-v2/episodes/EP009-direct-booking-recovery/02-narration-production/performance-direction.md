## Listener relationship

- Primary listener: a capable professional with hospitality judgment, deciding whether a small inn's commission line can fund a service worth their time
- What they already understand: booking a room online, that the sites take a commission, that a small inn is short staffed
- What they may distrust or misunderstand: that the commission line is what the hotel loses (it is not, the recoverable slice is the number), that AI tools are the advantage (access and judgment are), that a modeled figure is an earnings forecast
- Narrator's role in relation to them: an experienced operator across a table who spent two years on the property side of the sites, has never owned a hotel, and has not sold this service. Peer, not presenter
- Central episode promise: take four numbers from a small property and know what it can honestly pay for the job, and whether that is worth the listener's time
- Final belief or action the narration should make possible: run the ceiling on one innkeeper's four numbers and ask whether they would pay, with BUILD understood as bounded and demand understood as unknown

## Global performance target

- Baseline energy: the frozen register's baseline, unchanged. The episode opens as a conversation already underway
- Pace range: the register's moving baseline for setup and connective material. Room, not slowed words, at the two arithmetic passages (S10, S17), at the named rulings, and at the turns below
- Warmth/restraint: warm toward the innkeeper, the desk, the guest, and the listener. Dry recognition reserved for incentives, the vendor's statistic and automation that turns hospitality into a mailing list. Contempt never reaches a person
- Commercial authority: earned by S05 and decisive by S22. The verdict is a recommendation bounded by a test, never a pitch
- Terms to avoid performing as slogans: "the second stay", "the ceiling", "That's the product", "the guest book", "a business of one", "the drafting got cheap"
- Anti-targets: trailer / sales pitch / lecture / synthetic demo / announcer / motivational speaker / a hotel-industry keynote
