## Performance intent

Register: Operator Economy hosted long-form. Warm strategist with investigative wit. Documentary
truth underneath, plainspoken analogy-led reasoning on the surface, dry skepticism aimed at an
incentive rather than a person, a decisive commercial verdict once the case is earned.

Relationship: an experienced operator talking to one smart peer across a table, working through a
real problem. Not presenting. Not selling. He sides with the founder before he judges the system.
