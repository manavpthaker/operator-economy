## Category-name boundary

Short public category title: Helpdesk AI setup

Short spoken company name: **a helpdesk setup practice**

One-sentence plain definition: It makes the AI that came with a small store's help desk actually answer customers, and decides which questions it never should.

Internal operating description: see the Canvas §9.

Category status: **adjacent category, proposed editorial label.** Setup and optimization of the vendor's AI agent is an observed service category (sold by the vendor, CLM-010, and by certified agencies, CLM-009, CLM-012). "A helpdesk setup practice" is this episode's label for the solo version, which was not observed at disclosed economics.

Approved claim IDs and locators supporting the definition: CLM-005 (what the agent needs), CLM-009, CLM-010, CLM-012 (the category is sold), AMD-01 (the buyer boundary).

What the name and definition may establish: That the job exists and is sold; that the solo version is proposed.

What they do not establish: That buyers recognise the label; that any brand has paid a solo operator; any price.

Prohibited market, demand, price, outcome, or category-validation inference: "Brands are looking for helpdesk setup practices"; "the going rate is"; any automation-rate outcome.

Allowed first spoken wording: "Today we're building a helpdesk setup practice. It makes the AI that came with a small store's help desk actually answer customers, and decides which questions it never should."

Allowed later internal-description wording, if needed: "One fixed-fee implementation of the AI agent the store already pays for: audit the queue, write the rules, test, go live, tune for two weeks, report on the vendor's own meter."

Reason the shorter public name remains accurate: the mature company still sets up and keeps set up the bundled agent; nothing in the ladder leaves that job.
