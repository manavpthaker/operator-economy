## Attempts to break the candidate

1. **Is this just "configure Gorgias for money"?** Largely, yes — and the package says so honestly. The distinction it claims (judgment about what never to automate, handover rules, a wrong-answer log) is real in principle but unobserved in practice. Until validation step 1 shows what the wizard leaves undone, the offer's defensibility is an assertion. Re-score if the deliverable proves to be clicks.
2. **Does the enterprise failure rate transfer?** Not as a number. MIT NANDA sampled enterprises with pilots and budgets; a two-agent brand has neither. The package labels this correctly as one hop and refuses to quote 95% as an SMB statistic. That discipline is why the evidence floor still passes. But it means the *costly* in "costly problem" is modeled at this scale, not measured, which is why B scores the factor at 12.
3. **Is the vendor survey being laundered as independent?** No — CLM-004 is labeled vendor-owned and excluded from triangulation. B checked; the triangulation count rests on government, bank and vendor-commercial-behavior signals. B's objection is different: government and bank data both measure adoption, not demand for setup, so they are two measurements of one signal. Triangulation still passes on the vendor's commercial behavior as the second family, but it is closer to two signals than four.
4. **Does the vendor's own service menu prove demand or prove competition?** Both, and the package admits both. What B adds: the vendor's 60-day average launch and 2–5 onboarding sessions describe a **larger** implementation than the proposed 30 hours — either the vendor pads, or the package's hours are optimistic for any brand with undocumented policies. The 45-hour stress case may be the base case.
5. **Can a solo operator get in the door?** Unknown, and the package knows it. The partner-program form lists agency types, not individuals. The go-to-market path is therefore community reach, which every candidate claims and none has shown. B scores GTM at 4 and would fail the Canvas check at "weakly" if this were the only path; it passes because the diagnostic-entry model is transferable and the kill condition is explicit.
6. **Is there audience pull for the operator, not just the merchant?** The merchant-side questions (why isn't my AI agent resolving anything; what does it cost) are evidenced by autocomplete. The operator-side question (can I sell this) is evidenced only by generic "AI agency" attention that the channel exists to critique. The differentiated promise passes precisely because that coverage is bad; the audience for a better version is inferred.
7. **Is the original synthesis actually absent from coverage?** The research lists the coverage reviewed (competitor explainers, vendor tutorials, agency courses) and no reviewed piece makes the outcome-pricing-leaves-the-work framing. B accepts that it is absent from *reviewed* coverage. B does not accept it as a contradicting finding of the kind Step 0.3 held up as the exemplar — it is an angle, not a discovery — and scores POV at 4 rather than 5.
8. **Does the vendor kill the business before the episode airs?** It could. A wizard existed in October 2024; autocomplete shows "gorgias ai agent 3.0" as a live query; the vendor already sells "AI Agent setup." The package carries this as a kill condition and a required disclosure, which is correct. B's view is that this makes the candidate's shelf life shorter than the channel's production cycle unless the episode is framed around the judgment layer rather than the vendor's product — and that framing has not been tested.
9. **Is the legacy material really gone?** Yes. No legacy number appears in the model, the analogy map rejects the agency economics and the Accenture ladder by name, and the packaging territory explicitly excludes "$5.9 billion." This is the package's clearest improvement over EP001.
10. **Is there an episode or a settings screen?** There is an episode if one brand's queue, one wrong refund, and one never-automate decision carry it. The Canvas gate's "re-score if it collapses to clicks" warning applies to the narrative too.

## Shadow gate verdicts

- Evidence and analogy floor: **pass** — six valid transfers, at least two genuinely independent families, one stated hop, legacy material rejected by name
- Operator Canvas feasibility: **pass, narrowly** — go to market is the weakest check and passes only because the diagnostic-entry model transfers and the kill condition is explicit; buyer passes conditionally on CLM-013
- Narrative engine: **pass** — with the tutorial risk noted
- Audience pull: **pass** — consequential interest on transaction data; triangulation on two-to-four families depending on how adoption signals are counted
- POV: **pass, bounded** — real, honest, thin
- Factual and source blockers: **clear for a blueprint that teaches the work and models the fee**; **not clear for any script that states the onboarding threshold (CLM-013) or the Gorgias per-resolution price (CLM-007) before they are opened at source**
- Legal, permission, access, and guest blockers: **manageable** within scoped client-controlled access, written consent, and actions-off-by-default; vendor dependency is a documented constraint, not a blocker

## Decision

Decision: **continue research**

Evidence class: **observed model** for the service category; solo economics modeled — B agrees with the class and insists on the disclosure that no solo operator was observed

Production promotion authorized: no

Reason: Every hard gate passes, and the package is honest about what it has not shown. The score is below 70 because what it has not shown is the market: no small brand paying a solo implementer, no test of what the vendor's wizard leaves undone, no observed fee anywhere, and a buyer boundary resting on a search snippet. Three of those are resolvable in days (open CLM-013 and CLM-007 at source; run the wizard on a sandbox) and one in thirty (validation step 3). B would rescore after those four items and expects the result to move several points in either direction — which is exactly what "continue research" is for.
