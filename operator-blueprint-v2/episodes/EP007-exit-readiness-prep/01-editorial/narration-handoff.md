## Performance intent

Register: Operator Economy hosted long-form. Warm strategist with investigative wit. Documentary
truth underneath, plainspoken analogy-led reasoning on the surface, dry skepticism aimed at systems,
a decisive commercial verdict once the case is earned.

Relationship: an experienced operator talking to one smart peer across a table, working through a
real problem. Not presenting. Not selling.

| Scene range | Intent |
|---|---|
| S00 cold open | Present tense, plain. **The pause is the beat.** Let the narration stop where the owner stops. No lift on the last line |
| S01 | Silent. No narration |
| S02 brand and promise | Brand string flat and unhurried, exactly as written. The promise is a commitment, not a trailer |
| S03 context runway | The most generous passage in the episode. The incumbent case must sound genuinely fair. Any hint of setup-for-a-takedown is a defect |
| S04 to S05 | The question lands quietly. The thesis is an observation about incentives, not an accusation |

| S22 to S26 ending | Resolve rather than repeat. The verdict is a recommendation, not a flourish. The final ask is one sentence, said plainly |

Pace: unhurried baseline, room between ideas rather than inside them. Lift slightly in the cold open
and at the turn. Slow for the economics and the licensing rule.
