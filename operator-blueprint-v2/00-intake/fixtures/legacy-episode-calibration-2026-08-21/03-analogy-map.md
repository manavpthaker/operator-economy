## Synthesis thesis

This business is like a Zapier Solution Partner for the exact hands-on implementation model, an n8n Expert Partner for the specialist agency and integrator model, and a small-business digital-adoption advisor for the buyer's need for personalized guidance. The refinement is a vendor-neutral, fixed-scope reliability sprint whose product is a tested operating state, exception ownership, evidence, and client handoff rather than a tool, generic “AI agency,” or presumed retainer.
