## Attempts to break the candidate

1. **Is “workflow reliability” merely a nicer name for a Zapier freelancer?** Partly. The category is observed. The proposed differentiation survives only if the scope visibly includes baseline, state mapping, exception design, tests, observability, documentation, transfer, and a reject decision. If the delivery becomes “I connect your apps,” it loses the distinction and should be re-scored.
2. **Does KfW's administrative burden prove this buyer problem?** No. It proves a broader class of costly administrative work. The direct partner case and validation interviews must connect that burden to one recurring cross-tool handoff. The episode cannot claim the KfW hours are recoverable by this service.
3. **Do partner programs prove a new solo operator can win?** No. They prove exact businesses and customers exist. Selection, certification, brand, referrals, teams, or incumbency may explain their success. That is why acquisition remains a paid-pilot question and receives limited points.
4. **Does a transparent model make $3,000 plausible?** It makes the consequences calculable, not the price true. The package passes only because the number is labeled as a test and the success condition requires a buyer to pay. A public episode cannot call it a market rate.
5. **Is the retainer being smuggled back in?** No. The base case excludes it. Ongoing monitoring is allowed only when incidents, response duties, and buyer willingness create a real recurring job.
6. **Can a generalist safely touch production systems?** Only within the documented limits. Least privilege, client-controlled accounts, test data, logs, revocation, and high-risk-workflow exclusions are required. A candidate whose first segment cannot satisfy these conditions fails the access or permission gate.
7. **Is audience interest mostly people selling to would-be agency founders?** Possibly. Current YouTube attention is not buyer demand. The gate still passes because direct commercial ecosystems and adoption research provide independent signals, while the episode's differentiated promise critiques the opportunity pitch rather than repeating it.
8. **Is there an episode or just a process diagram?** There is an episode if one transaction, one employee or customer consequence, and one operator decision persist through the build and failure test. A deck of generic boxes would fail the approved narrative despite the written package.
