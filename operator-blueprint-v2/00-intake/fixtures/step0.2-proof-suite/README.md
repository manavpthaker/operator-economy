# Step 0.2 discrimination proof suite

Status: test-only; Step 0.2 remains proposed

This suite tests whether Step 0.2 distinguishes among three materially different candidate states without creating a production candidate, queue row, episode, or Step 1 handoff.

## Controls

1. **Positive regression:** AI visibility diagnostic, a researched adjacent synthesis.
2. **Borderline:** GTM engineering / Clay operator, a coherent opportunity lead with incomplete transfer, operating, and narrative support.
3. **Negative:** OpenAI / Anthropic business-model breakdown, an audience-relevant market story that lacks an operator business.

## What success means

The gate is behaving usefully only if:

- the positive control is `eligible`;
- the borderline control is `continue research`, not automatically passed or prematurely archived;
- the negative control is `archived from the Operator Blueprint format`, even if it could work in another editorial format;
- all production paths remain unchanged.

The suite tests classification and gate behavior. It is not current production research for the borderline or negative idea.

## Files

- `00-TEST-DESIGN.md` records expectations before the two new controls are scored.
- `01-positive-geo-regression.md` checks the completed GEO package against the suite.
- `02-borderline-gtm-engineering.md` reviews the bounded V1 GTM input.
- `03-negative-company-breakdown.md` reviews the pure company-breakdown input.
- `04-RESULTS.md` compares expected and observed decisions and records any rule defect.
