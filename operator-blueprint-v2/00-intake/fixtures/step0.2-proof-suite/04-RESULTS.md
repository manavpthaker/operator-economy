## What the suite demonstrates

### The new model is not exact-precedent dependent

The positive control passes as an `adjacent synthesis` even though paid demand, realized service pricing, delivery time, margin, and client outcome for the exact service remain unobserved. Valid behavioral, adjacent-market, capability, operating, narrative, and audience evidence can support a blueprint when the transfers and assumptions are explicit.

### Novelty and market scale do not create an automatic pass

The borderline control has a fashionable job title, agency-pricing claims, a fast-growing platform, a competition gap, and a plausible POV. It still remains in research because the supplied package does not complete the buyer problem, analogy map, workflow, economics, go-to-market path, narrative mechanism, or demand triangulation.

### Audience interest cannot replace the business

The negative control may work as a company-economics documentary. It fails this pipeline because there is no business for the prospective operator to build. A large company, strong market story, or attractive title cannot substitute for the operator, buyer, offer, workflow, economics, and validation path.

### The hard gates matter more than score noise

The nearest passing review is 76, six points above the 70 threshold. The borderline score is 55, fifteen points below. More importantly, the positive control passes every hard gate while the other controls fail structurally different gates. A small reviewer disagreement cannot flip these fixture decisions.

## Process defect found and repaired

The Step 0 README originally presented seven artifacts without a clear early-exit rule. That could force a full scorecard, Canvas, narrative review, and promotion record for an idea that is obviously out of scope.

The proof added `03-validation/DISPOSITION-RECORD.template.md` and an early-disposition rule:

- archive an out-of-format idea at intake;
- keep a coherent but incomplete idea in research with explicit reopening conditions;
- require all seven artifacts only for eligibility or promotion.

This repair does not weaken a gate. It removes unnecessary work after a decisive failure.

## Limitations

- The positive GEO result was already known and functions as a regression check, not a blind prediction.
- The borderline and negative controls use bounded frozen V1 inputs. They test disposition behavior, not the candidates' maximum potential after new research.
- Both GEO reviews are automated review roles, not independent human reviewers.
- Three controls do not establish universal calibration across every future opportunity category.
- Step 0.2 is still proposed and has not been declared repository-wide canon.
