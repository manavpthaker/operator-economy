# Step 0.2 proof design and predeclared outcomes

Recorded: 2026-08-21

Status: test fixture; no production authority

## Purpose

The GEO retest shows that broader parallel research can rescue a credible novel business. It does not, by itself, prove that the revised system rejects weak or out-of-scope ideas. This suite adds a borderline and negative control.

## Inputs

### Positive regression

- Candidate: AI visibility diagnostic service.
- Source package: `../candidate-test-2026-08-21-geo-agency/step0.2-retest/`.
- Input quality: current, multi-source, full Step 0.2 fixture package.
- Expected result: `eligible`, evidence class `adjacent synthesis`, every hard gate passes.
- Note: This result is already observed and serves as a regression control, not a blind prediction.

### Borderline control

- Candidate: GTM engineering / Clay operator.
- Source package: `../../05-archive/topic-operations/intake-2026-08-14.md`, section A, lines 22-43.
- Input quality: recent frozen V1 lead with agency pricing, company-scale evidence, an initial coverage gap, and partial POV; no Step 0.2 analogy map, complete Canvas, modeled capacity, buyer-problem proof, or narrative review.
- Predeclared expected result: `continue research`.
- Expected reason: The opportunity is coherent enough to investigate but should not pass until the buyer problem, transferable workflow, economics, tool dependency, narrative mechanism, and audience signals are completed under Step 0.2.
- Disallowed outcome: `eligible` based only on Clay's growth, agency-published prices, or the novelty of the job title.

### Negative control

- Candidate: “How OpenAI / Anthropic make money,” preserved as V1 row 19.
- Source package: `../../05-archive/topic-operations/topic-queue-v3-retired.md`, row 19.
- Input quality: a market-story thesis with named companies, public attention, and possible business-documentary value; no prospective operator, buyer, offer, delivery workflow, go-to-market path, operator economics, or validation test.
- Predeclared expected result: `archived from the Operator Blueprint format`.
- Expected reason: Audience interest and narrative potential cannot substitute for an operator business. This may belong in a separate company-breakdown format, but it should not enter this pipeline.
- Disallowed outcome: `eligible` because OpenAI and Anthropic are large, timely, or interesting.

## Decision rules under test

1. A score at or above 70 cannot rescue a failed hard gate.
2. A promising but incomplete business remains in research rather than being forced into pass or reject.
3. A strong media topic without an operator business fails the Canvas and is routed out of the format.
4. Exact business precedent is optional; a real buyer problem and valid transfer structure are not.
5. Market scale, funding, vendor pricing, and tool popularity cannot independently prove a small-operator blueprint.
6. Narrative and audience pull are necessary but cannot replace business plausibility.

## Contamination boundary

The borderline and negative controls are scored only from their frozen V1 source sections and the current Step 0.2 rules. No new market research is added. This intentionally tests how the system handles incomplete intake, not whether either idea could pass after a future full research run.
