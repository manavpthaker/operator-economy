## Proposed Operator Blueprint

- Offer: A bounded visibility-and-accuracy diagnostic before any recurring service.
- Buyer outcome: A baseline, source-gap analysis, prioritized corrections, and a measurement plan.
- Delivery workflow: Define prompts, capture answers, trace sources, audit owned information, propose corrections, implement approved changes, and measure changes.
- Technology and human stack: Answer capture, source tracing, content and technical review, analytics, client context, and human judgment; exact tools are not selected.
- Required skills or credentials: Research, analytics, content strategy, technical SEO familiarity, client communication, and the ability to avoid unsupported causal claims.
- Go-to-market path: Interview a narrow buyer segment and offer a manual diagnostic only after validating the problem.
- Pricing model: Unknown; vendor-published ranges are not usable evidence.
- Delivery costs and margin: Unknown.
- Defensibility: Unproven; a durable service would require trusted measurement, category knowledge, source-quality judgment, and implementation—not simple tool resale.

## Risks and constraints

- Factual uncertainty: Search demand, buyer pain, pricing, outcomes, and market maturity are unresolved.
- Legal or regulatory boundary: Claims about visibility, accuracy, competitive comparison, and regulated categories require future review.
- Permissions, privacy, or guest dependency: Client permission would be required for private data or account access; no guest is required for initial research.
- Platform or vendor dependency: AI-answer behavior and measurement tools can change quickly.
- Ethical failure mode: Manipulative content, fabricated authority, spam, guaranteed placement, or presenting correlation as causation.
- Claims that must not be made: Guaranteed inclusion, guaranteed ranking, guaranteed revenue, proven small-operator economics, or verified demand.

## Thirty-day validation plan

1. Verify query demand and interview five people in one buyer role about the problem, existing spend, and decision process.
2. Run a manual baseline on consenting test brands and define a repeatable accuracy and source-coverage measure.
3. Price a bounded diagnostic, estimate delivery hours, and seek one paid pilot without promising placement or revenue.

Success signal: At least three of five buyers independently confirm a material recurring problem, one accepts a paid bounded diagnostic, and delivery can be measured with documented hours and limitations.

Kill or redesign condition: Buyers view the problem as immaterial, no outcome can be measured responsibly, or a paid diagnostic cannot cover delivery effort without misleading claims.
