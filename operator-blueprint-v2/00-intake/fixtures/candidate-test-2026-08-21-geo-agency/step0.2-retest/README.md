This directory reruns the same GEO-agency idea against the broader but bounded Step 0.2 model. It is isolated from the production candidate and queue paths.

## Question tested

Can a novel AI-business candidate qualify without an exact operating precedent while still being rejected if its buyer problem, parallels, economics, story, or audience interest are weak?

## Result

- Evidence class: `adjacent synthesis`
- Reviewer A: 82/100; all hard gates pass; eligible.
- Reviewer B: 76/100; all hard gates pass; eligible.
- Production status: not promoted; fixtures cannot enter editorial development.

The result changed from the Step 0.1 baseline, where the same broad idea scored 20/100 and 18/100 and failed. The new result is not based on novelty credit. Exact search volume, realized service revenue, paid demand, and an existing GEO-agency precedent receive no assumed points.

It qualifies because the expanded research now establishes:

- a consequential change in consumer discovery through two independent research sources;
- a mature adjacent SEO audit and advisory model;
- current answer, mention, citation, and competitor measurement capabilities;
- five explicit one-hop analogy transfers from five source families;
- a complete but clearly modeled capacity and contribution calculation;
- a bounded first-buyer and validation path;
- a causal operator story with stakes, mechanism, build, tradeoffs, and payoff;
- a differentiated audience promise that does not require an earnings or guaranteed-outcome claim.

## Strict boundaries that still apply

- The buyer problem needs direct or credible adjacent evidence.
- At least three valid parallels from at least two source families are required.
- Buyer/problem, delivery, economics/capacity, and at least one additional pillar must be covered.
- No load-bearing claim may depend on more than one untested inference hop.
- The Canvas, narrative engine, audience pull, POV, truth, and permission gates must all pass.
- An owner override can bypass only the numeric threshold.
- Modeled money is arithmetic, not observed performance or an earnings forecast.

## What still needs validation

Buyer ownership and willingness to pay, delivery hours, actual tool configuration, repeatability, client decision value, acquisition cost, price, and contribution remain unobserved. Those limits do not prevent an adjacent-synthesis episode, but they must be disclosed and built into the episode's validation plan.
