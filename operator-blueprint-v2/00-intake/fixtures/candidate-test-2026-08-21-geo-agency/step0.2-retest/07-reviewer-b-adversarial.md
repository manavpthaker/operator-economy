2. **Are product launches being mistaken for demand?** No. Product sources support measurement capability only. Consequential interest comes from Gartner and McKinsey; adjacent spend comes from Clutch.
3. **Does changed consumer behavior prove a costly brand problem?** It proves strategic relevance, not a quantified loss. The Canvas passes only because the public deliverable is a diagnostic and the buyer consequence and purchase trigger remain explicit validation items.
4. **Does SEO pricing prove a $3,000 diagnostic?** No. It bounds a neighboring expert-service rate. The fixture labels the service price, hours, and contribution as a model and includes a paid-pilot kill condition.
5. **Can the operator promise improvement after remediation?** No. The allowable promise is evidence, prioritized controllable actions, and repeated measurement. Placement, traffic, leads, and revenue are prohibited claims.
6. **Is there a story or just a pitch deck?** There is a causal story: changed discovery exposes a blind spot, the operator maps its mechanism, builds a diagnostic, confronts what cannot be controlled, and reaches a build / test / reject decision.
7. **Would the new rules let any three vaguely related businesses pass?** No. This package has direct behavioral evidence, five bounded transfers from five source families, all six pillars covered, a one-hop limit, complete economics, a passing narrative, and explicit failure conditions. A label-only “X plus Y plus AI” idea would fail those checks.
