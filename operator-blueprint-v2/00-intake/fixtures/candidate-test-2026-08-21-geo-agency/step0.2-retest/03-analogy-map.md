## Synthesis thesis

This business is like an SEO audit for the buyer, diagnostic entry, pricing structure, and remediation handoff; like a brand or competitive monitoring service for repeated observation and reporting; and like current AI-visibility software for prompt, mention, citation, and competitor measurement. The new combination is a small-operator service that interprets those signals, separates controllable from uncontrollable gaps, and sells a decision-grade diagnostic without guaranteeing placement or revenue.
