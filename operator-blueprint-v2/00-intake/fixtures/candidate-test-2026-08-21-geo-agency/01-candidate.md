## Opportunity in one sentence

An experienced marketing or content-operations professional could build a service that helps brands understand and improve how accurately they appear in AI-generated answers, if buyer demand, measurable outcomes, delivery costs, and responsible optimization methods can be independently established.

## Viewer outcome

- Viewer promise: Understand what a GEO service might sell and which facts must be proven before treating it as a real business opportunity.
- Operator decision: Decide whether to investigate a bounded diagnostic service rather than immediately selling recurring optimization.
- Practical capability: Outline a buyer interview, visibility baseline, measurement method, and thirty-day validation test.
- Expected Operator Canvas: Buyer, end customer, problem, offer, outcome, delivery, stack, acquisition path, economics, risks, and validation plan.

## People

- Viewer/operator: An experienced marketing, SEO, content, analytics, or product-operations professional who can conduct research and explain uncertainty to a buyer.
- Buyer: Hypothesized marketing or communications leader responsible for brand discovery and reputation; not yet validated.
- End customer or beneficiary: The buyer's prospective customer using an AI answer engine to research a category or brand.
- Guest or outside participant required: no for research; buyer participation would be required to validate willingness to pay.

## Problem

- Costly problem: Brands may be absent, inaccurately described, or poorly represented in AI-generated answers, but the financial cost and recurrence are not yet independently established.
- Why it matters: Discovery and reputation may shift toward AI answers, but the link between visibility changes and commercial outcomes remains a hypothesis.
- Why now: AI-answer products and a funded measurement category exist, based only on frozen V1 research that has not been refreshed for this fixture.
- Existing alternatives: SEO agencies, content agencies, public relations firms, brand-monitoring tools, and emerging GEO vendors.

## Proposed business

- Offer: Begin with a bounded AI-answer visibility and accuracy diagnostic; recurring implementation remains unproven.
- Customer outcome: A documented baseline, prioritized accuracy and source gaps, and an ethical test plan—not a guaranteed ranking or revenue result.
- Delivery hypothesis: Define representative prompts, capture answers, trace cited sources, identify correctable owned-content gaps, implement approved changes, and measure changes without claiming causation prematurely.
- Revenue hypothesis: A paid diagnostic followed by optional implementation; no usable price, delivery-cost, or margin evidence is available in this fixture.
