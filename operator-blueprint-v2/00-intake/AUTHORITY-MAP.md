## Approved local Step 0 authority

The following files are canonical for new Operator Blueprint V2 opportunity intake. This approval does not make later V2 stages authoritative.

1. `STEP0.2-APPROVAL.md` — owner approval, locked scope, authority hashes, acceptance evidence, and change-control boundary.
2. `README.md` — lifecycle, scope, evidence classes, calibration zone, and promotion boundary.
3. `03-validation/EVIDENCE-STANDARD.md` — direct, adjacent, component, transfer, and modeled-economics rules.
4. `03-validation/ANALOGY-MAP.template.md` — required transfer map and minimum evidence floor.
5. `03-validation/CANVAS-FEASIBILITY-GATE.md` — complete business-model feasibility gate.
6. `03-validation/EDITORIAL-POTENTIAL-GATE.md` — narrative-engine and audience-pull gates.
7. `03-validation/SCORECARD.template.md` — Step 0.2 opportunity-readiness score plus hard-gate and calibration-zone record.
8. `03-validation/DISPOSITION-RECORD.template.md` — auditable early-exit record that prevents unnecessary full-package work.
9. `01-candidates/CANDIDATE.template.md`, `02-research/RESEARCH-BRIEF.template.md`, and `04-queue/PROMOTION-RECORD.template.md` — required intake, research, and handoff contracts.
10. `04-queue/QUEUE.md` — active V2 queue state.
11. `fixtures/ACCEPTANCE-SET.md` — frozen behavioral acceptance set for future Step 0 versions.
