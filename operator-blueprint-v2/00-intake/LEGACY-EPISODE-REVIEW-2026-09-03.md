## Method

Each of the six published V1 episodes (EP001 to EP006) was entered as a real candidate under the Step 0.2 artifact-path contract, with the POV factor scored under Step 0.3. Each premise was first bounded into one testable operator business (one buyer, one job, one deliverable, one 30-day test) without changing the underlying business, then researched against current sources with a 2026-09-03 cutoff. Every package received a Reviewer A scorecard and an independent adversarial Reviewer B pass. Prior publication received no credit; every legacy number was re-opened and either re-verified or marked not usable.

The 2026-08-21 legacy screen (`fixtures/legacy-episode-calibration-2026-08-21/00-legacy-screen.md`) and the EP003 calibration fixture remain frozen test evidence and were used only as leads.

## What the gate found, in one line each

- **EP001:** the vendor sells the same setup itself and ships a wizard; no observed price exists.
- **EP002:** the platforms absorbed the product between July and September 2026, and the statistic that carried the episode is a 2016 agency post.
- **EP003:** the category is observed but no independent price exists; the fixture's price bands were agency-authored.
- **EP004:** the offer is verified, the solo economics are not, and three statistics that aired are not on the pages they were cited from.
- **EP005:** not a business; a thesis.
- **EP006:** gates pass and the story is strong, but the recoverable commission at a 20-room property caps the retainer near the operator's own labor cost.
