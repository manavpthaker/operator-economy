# Owner decision 2026-09-28: Step 0 is a coaching foundation, not proof

Status: approved owner decision. Recorded 2026-09-28 from the owner's instruction in chat.

Decided by: Manav Thaker (owner)

Owner's words: "im not talking to buyers or asking or paid pilot. these episodes are not proof of
anything we need to have enough research to provide a foundation for the audience to go and do the
work. remember we're coaches."

Scope: how Step 0 research, dispositions, re-entry conditions, and bench run records treat field
validation. This decision does **not** edit any locked Step 0.2 file or the Step 0.3 amendment.

## Why no locked file changes

The locked standard already assigns the first test to the viewer. The Canvas feasibility gate asks
whether "a prospective operator can run a bounded thirty-day test with a success signal and a kill or
redesign condition" (`03-validation/CANVAS-FEASIBILITY-GATE.md`). The evidence standard says an
uncovered pillar "can be tested through the bounded validation plan before substantial investment"
(`03-validation/EVIDENCE-STANDARD.md`), which describes a viewer's plan, not an owner's pre-requisite.

The drift was in how dispositions and bench runs from 2026-09-03 to 2026-09-28 wrote re-entry
conditions: they required the owner to run buyer interviews, paid-pilot asks, outreach tests, or
delivery rehearsals before a candidate could move. This decision corrects that reading.

## Rules

1. **No owner field work.** Buyer interviews, paid-pilot asks, outreach tests, and owner delivery
   rehearsals are never a Step 0 re-entry condition, blocker, or "next owner input". The owner will
   not run them.
2. **Field-only unknowns become the viewer's first test.** When a question can be answered only by
   talking to buyers or selling, record it in the research brief's validation plan as what the
   viewer must test, with a success signal and a kill condition. It is a labelled unknown the episode
   teaches, not a gate.
3. **Desk research must carry the foundation.** Research is sufficient when a viewer could act on it:
   a sourced buyer problem, observed current spend or alternatives, a clear delivery boundary, the
   strongest substitute, published or observed price anchors where they exist, and honest labels on
   what is fact, model, and unknown. Missing desk evidence (a document not yet opened, a source not
   yet searched) is still a valid reason to continue research.
4. **Episodes are not proof.** No episode claims the business works, that buyers will pay, or any
   earnings outcome. The existing public-framing, claim-status, and earnings-language rules remain in
   force unchanged. A synthetic rehearsal on screen is showable teaching material, not evidence.
5. **Nothing else relaxes.** Hard gates, the evidence floor, the analogy transfer test, scoring,
   the calibration zone, and owner promotion are unchanged. A score may not be raised because field
   work was removed; field-only gaps stay scored as gaps. Owner promotion is still required.
6. **Owner-submitted leads.** The owner may admit a held pool lead directly. The pool entry records
   the owner admission decision, and the bench then develops it like any other candidate.
