# Parked topics — guest-dependent, waiting on capacity or traction

Opened 2026-08-10 with the v3 queue reset. These are not dropped and not scored. They are episodes
whose value depends on a named person appearing, and a guest is expensive in the two currencies the
operation is short of right now: production efficiency and audience.

**The unblock condition, stated once for all of them:** the pipeline runs an episode without
hand-tuning per stage, *and* the channel has enough reach that appearing on it is worth a guest's
time. Concretely, browse CTR clearing roughly 4% and an episode reliably clearing three figures of
views. Until both hold, asking someone to give up an afternoon and clear their real revenue numbers
is asking them to spend more than the episode returns to them.

There is a second reason to wait that matters more than scheduling. A guest who says real numbers
on camera is the channel's scarcest asset and it is spendable once. Spending Joanie or Mike on an
episode that gets 3 views burns the relationship and the evidence together.

Reviewed monthly, or immediately when either condition flips.
