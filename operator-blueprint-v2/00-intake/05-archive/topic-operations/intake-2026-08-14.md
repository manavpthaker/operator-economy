## D. Content management and ghostwriting — the operator writing as someone else

**Target queries:** `linkedin ghostwriter` · `ghostwriting rates` · `how to become a ghostwriter`

**HELD 2026-08-14, same day it was promoted.** Its entire advantage was
first-party POV — Manav's own rates, hours and where the seams show. Those
figures are not to hand, and without them the row keeps the weak
agency-self-published low end that every other candidate has while losing the one
thing that made it better than them. Not guest-dependent and not parked: the
unblock is Manav's own numbers, nobody else's schedule.

Proposed by Manav, who is running one of these engagements right now. The POV is
present tense rather than recalled — which is why it is worth returning to.

The angle the evidence hands us, and it is the thesis line: **writing is the
smallest part of the job.** The fastest-growing segment is AI-assisted
ghostwriting, where a model trained on the client's posting history drafts and
the operator does strategy, voice and client management — which is precisely the
"install it and stand there until it runs" pattern EP001 is built on, applied to
words instead of workflows.
