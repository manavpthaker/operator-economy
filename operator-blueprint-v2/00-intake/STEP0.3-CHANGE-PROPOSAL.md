Proposal date: 2026-09-01

Production authorization: none

## Why v0.3 is required

Step 0.2 was exercised on real material for the first time on 2026-09-01
(`candidate-2026-09-01-ai-visibility-diagnostic`). The rubric worked: it caught seller-published
pricing masquerading as market evidence and correctly declined to promote a business nobody has
shown to be sellable.

It also exposed a structural defect that will compound.

The Operator Economy POV factor is worth 7 points and reads, in full:

> Record direct experience, earned judgment, useful synthesis, and limits.

Four sources are named and no guidance is given on weighing them. In practice the scorer collapsed
them to the first: the AI visibility candidate scored **4/7**, deducted three points because "the
experience is being lived, not completed." Nothing in the rubric required that reading, and nothing
prevented it.

The consequence appeared immediately. The net-new candidate search that followed produced a slate
dominated by hospitality, because hospitality is where the owner's direct operating experience
exists. Under the collapsed reading, **the channel can only cover sectors its owner has personally
operated in** — hotels, grocery, hospitality — and every candidate outside that set carries an
automatic three-point penalty regardless of the quality of its research.

That is not what the factor is for. It also contradicts the hard gate, which already permits
originality:

> Operator Economy POV is real and bounded. Synthesis may be original; experience claims may not be
> invented.

The gate governs **honesty**. The scoring factor was being used to govern **sector adjacency**.
Those are different tests and only one of them belongs.

## What the current wording collapses

Three distinct things are being scored as one:

1. **Sector experience** — the owner has operated this specific kind of business. Rare, highest
   value, and does not scale across a topic queue.
2. **Transferable earned judgment** — the owner has been the buyer of this service, has built the
   kind of system it depends on, or has run a business with the same structural constraint. Common,
   genuinely differentiating, and currently unscored.
3. **Original synthesis** — the research produced a defensible finding that is not present in
   existing coverage. Available on any topic to anyone willing to do the work, and currently
   unscored despite being explicitly permitted by the gate.

The AI visibility package is the worked example. Its research established, against the independent
2023–2026 literature, that the intervention-efficacy transfer the entire commercial field rests on
does not survive scrutiny — and that the "40% improvement" figure in general circulation describes a
simulator result where the document was already retrieved. No published source says this. It is a
genuine, defensible, channel-specific point of view. It scored zero, because it was not sector
experience.

## Proposed change

Replace the POV factor's rationale with an explicit source taxonomy at parity, and raise the
evidentiary bar for each source in exchange for the added breadth.

### Proposed factor wording

> **Operator Economy POV — 7 points.** Score whether the channel can say something about this
> opportunity that other coverage cannot, and whether that claim is honestly bounded. Credit any of:
> direct operating experience in the sector; transferable earned judgment (buyer-side experience,
> builder experience, or a business run under the same structural constraint); or original synthesis
> demonstrated by a specific finding the research produced that is absent from existing coverage.
> Each source must be **demonstrated, not asserted** — name the experience, the artifact, or the
> finding, and state its limit. Sector adjacency is not itself a scoring criterion.

### The exchange

Breadth is bought with demonstrability. Under v0.2 a scorer could award POV points for a general
claim of relevant background. Under v0.3:

- **Direct or transferable experience** must name the specific experience or artifact and state what
  it does not license the channel to claim.
- **Original synthesis** must point to a **specific finding, with its source, that is absent from
  the strongest existing coverage.** "We researched it carefully" is not synthesis. "The independent
  survey of this literature contradicts the industry's central claim, here is the citation, and no
  published source says so" is.
- A candidate with none of the three scores **0–1**, not 4. The floor drops as the ceiling widens.

### What does not change

- The hard gate is untouched. POV must still be **real and bounded**, and experience claims may
  still never be invented. Loosening the scoring factor does not loosen the honesty gate, and this
  proposal must not be read as permitting a manufactured point of view.
- The factor remains 7 points. This is a redistribution of how those points are earned, not an
  inflation of their weight.
- The YouTube inauthentic-content posture is unchanged and arguably strengthened: a demonstrated
  original finding is stronger evidence of genuine human editorial work than an unevidenced claim of
  background familiarity.

## Risk this proposal accepts

Broadening the sources raises the risk that POV points are earned by research that is merely
thorough rather than genuinely original, which is the failure mode the factor exists to prevent.
The demonstrability requirement is the mitigation, and it is a real bar: the finding must be
specific, sourced, and shown to be absent from the strongest existing coverage identified in the
research brief's competition review.

If that bar proves unenforceable in practice — if scorers begin awarding synthesis points for
ordinary summarisation — the correct response is to tighten the demonstration requirement, not to
return to sector adjacency.

## Rescoring implications

- **No completed package is retroactively rescored.** Step 0.2 governed the AI visibility package
  and its 73/100 stands as the decision of record.
- Under v0.3 that candidate's POV would plausibly score 5–6 rather than 4, on demonstrated original
  synthesis plus buyer-side experience. That is a 1–2 point movement. **It would not have changed
  the outcome**: the park was driven by economics at 5/10, and 74–75 remains inside the calibration
  zone. This proposal is not a route to reopening it.
- Re-entry for any parked candidate still requires a new reviewed package under whatever rubric is
  current at that time.

## Required evidence before approval

- The proposed factor wording is applied to at least two net-new candidates in different sectors,
  at least one of which has no direct operating experience available.
- At least one of those candidates scores its POV on demonstrated original synthesis, with the
  specific finding and its absence from existing coverage recorded.
- A worked negative control: a candidate whose POV correctly scores 0–1 because none of the three
  sources is demonstrable, confirming the lowered floor functions.
