## Why parked

A permission-and-business-fit failure with a specific, checkable cause, not a timing decision.

The de minimis suspension is real, primary-sourced, current, and highly showable, and nothing like
it exists anywhere in the repository. The business does not follow from it. Classification and
valuation performed for another party for compensation are customs business requiring a broker's
license under 19 CFR 111.1, the definition also reaches activities relating to the preparation of
documents intended to be filed with CBP, the compliance carve-out is limited to a related business
entity, and the § 111.2(a)(2) exceptions do not include a paid third-party adviser. Remove every
regulated task and what is left is the landed-cost calculation, which Zonos publishes at $2 per
order plus 10% of duties and taxes. No accessed source shows any buyer paying an independent
operator for the residual decision layer.

## Unblock condition

Both are required.

1. **A qualified written review** by a customs attorney or licensed customs broker establishing that
   a lawful unlicensed scope exists, and stating whether taking classification as a broker-supplied
   input keeps the operator outside "activities relating to the preparation" of a CBP filing.
2. **One paid engagement** from a target importer for that lawful scope alone, with the regulated
   tasks visibly removed from the statement of work.

A negative finding on condition 1 is a reject, not a re-park.

## Review trigger
