## Why parked

A business-fit failure with a specific, checkable cause: the AI step is already a coded field and
an existing product.

The old way is real and primary-sourced: a Washington firm bought 10,555 state-patrol reports and
had a staffer read each for fault before mailing, then paid $950,000 to settle. The candidate bet
that AI could replace that reading. In Ohio, an open state, the report already codes each person's
injury level and the at-fault unit, and a vendor already sells Ohio crash data to law firms scored
by injury and fault within hours. Missouri's state patrol filters injury crashes by severity in a
free public search. What remains for an operator is data entry and mail, which vendors already run.

## Unblock condition

Either one reopens research (desk work; no owner field work required):

1. An open state where injury and fault are not coded fields on the report a firm receives, and no
   vendor already sells that state's reports filtered by injury or fault.
2. Primary or independent evidence that law firms pay an outside party for the compliance layer
   alone (exclusion list, lawful-day timing, suppression log), separate from data and mail.

## Review trigger

Either unblock condition being met, otherwise **2026-12-31** (state access law and pending bills),
or on issue of the Georgia Supreme Court ruling on crash-victim solicitation. Re-entry requires a new
reviewed package with a current cutoff and the full ethics refusal list carried forward.
