## Reason for stopping

- Failed or incomplete requirement: evidence and go-to-market
- Plain-English finding: The bounded premise (a fixed-fee implementation of the AI agent already bundled in a small Shopify brand's Gorgias or Zendesk helpdesk, measured on the vendor's own verified-resolution meter) is a real service category: the vendor and its partner directory both sell it. Both reviewers recommend holding until three cheap verifications and one paid engagement exist.

  - The legacy EP001 market-size ladder (the $5.9B figure, Accenture bookings) as evidence a solo operator can sell implementation.
  - The MIT NANDA '95% of pilots fail' finding as a small-brand statistic; it is enterprise evidence.
  - Any fee, hour, payback, or margin figure as typical or observed; all are modeled.
  - The Gorgias onboarding threshold (CLM-013) and per-resolution price (CLM-007) in any script until opened and recorded at source.
