## Verdict

Verdict: **pass**

Evidence class: **observed model**

Failed checks: none. Three checks pass narrowly or weakly and are named: costly problem (unquantified consequence), go to market (unproven channel), and viewer/operator (disqualifier for non-designers).

Required research before reconsideration: none blocking for the Canvas as a *test blueprint*. The following would be required before any public statement beyond the modeled scenario: (1) a first-party paid month at a predeclared price (validation step 2); (2) logged hours per request (step 3); (3) a day-30 renewal answer (step 3). Without these the Canvas teaches how to test the business, not that it works.

Required Canvas disclosure:

1. This is an **observed model whose offer is verified and whose solo economics are not.** The reference operator's published contract is real; its revenue and client count are self-reported at five different levels and two different counts and are **excluded from this package**.
2. **Every operator-side figure is modeled** — price point, hours per request, capacity, acquisition cost and contribution — from published category prices and government wage data. At the modeled point ($3,000 × 5 clients) the business is a well-paid job for one designer. `Modeled scenario, not observed performance or an earnings forecast.`
3. **Retention is a hypothesis.** No usable churn data exists for this category. Agency-churn benchmarks in circulation publish no methodology and may not be borrowed.
4. **The buyer has the same tools.** Figma Make adoption is verified from Figma's own filings. Whether a seed-stage buyer keeps paying a designer once drafts are free is what the 30-day test exists to find out.
5. **This business requires design ability.** A non-designer cannot run it on AI tools; Figma's own survey has two-thirds of users unable to rely on the output. The Canvas must say who this is not for.
6. **Acquisition is unproven.** The reference operator's growth is attributed to an audience on X; the Canvas offers demo-first outreach as a testable process with one first-party data point (6 emails, 1 callback, 0 clients) and no proven conversion rate.
7. **Do not state**: "70% of Figma's active users use AI" (not in primary), "5.8% reply rate for lists under 50" (not found at source), any Superside layoff percentage (aggregator-only), any income range as typical.
