## Adversarial editorial finding

Performed by the same agent as the evidence pass; not an independent review.

- Strongest alternative explanation: this is e-discovery consulting, an existing trade with
  established firms and certified staff. AI helps those firms as much as a newcomer, and small
  agencies will either hire a specialist (Methacton) or buy software for the clerk they already have.
  The solo-operator angle may be the weakest version of a real market.
- Strongest causal weakness: "AI made asking cheap" is supported only by 2024 FOIA Buddy reports and
  officials' suspicion; the 2026 surges cite investigations, litigation, video, and complexity at
  least as often.
- Hidden labor: procurement responses, insurance, security onboarding into agency systems, and full
  human verification of every AI mark.
- Recourse and liability: a wrongful release harms a third party; agency approval does not remove
  the processor's exposure.
- If the most attractive claim (AI floods) is removed: the episode still stands on a verified,
  showable, paid job with a clean decision line — stronger than either earlier bench candidate.
- Duplication: none found in the repository.
