## Recommendation

Decision: **eligible**

Evidence class: **adjacent synthesis**

Required disclosure: The behaviour change is **observed** — independent national survey evidence that AI-assisted information seeking is now mainstream. Category investment is **observed**. The service format is **transferred** from SEO audits and brand monitoring, which share the shape of the sale but not its established reason. Every price, hour, capacity and margin figure is **modeled**, and the price inputs come from sellers publishing their own asking rates rather than from any observed transaction. The claim that optimisation reliably improves organic discoverability is **not supported and is rejected** — the independent survey of the 2023–2026 literature finds no stable cross-platform causal effect, and the widely quoted "40% improvement" is a benchmark gain measured where the document was already retrieved. Whether the honest version of this service can be delivered at a sellable price is **hypothetical and untested**, and is the first thing the operator should measure.

Rationale: This scores 73 and is eligible, but the number understates how unusual the package is. Its strongest asset is what the research removed rather than what it found: the intervention-efficacy transfer that the entire commercial field is built on was tested against independent evidence and rejected, leaving a narrower business that sells measurement rather than remedy. That narrowing is what makes the episode honest and differentiated — page-one coverage is written exclusively by sellers, and no neutral account exists of whether the business works.

The two deductions that matter are real and are not arguable away. The economics rest on seller-published prices with no observed transaction, and the dominant cost driver is unmeasured — with the independent evidence implying roughly seven times the intuitive delivery hours against fees anchored in the low thousands. It is genuinely possible that the honest service does not clear. That risk is carried explicitly as the kill condition in the validation plan rather than assumed away, and an episode that reaches "do not build this, and here is exactly why" is a legitimate and useful outcome for the viewer.

Because the score falls inside the 70-75 calibration zone, promotion requires explicit owner review recorded in the promotion record.
