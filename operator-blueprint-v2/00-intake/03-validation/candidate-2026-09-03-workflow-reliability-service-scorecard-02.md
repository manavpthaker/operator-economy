## Recommendation

Decision: **continue research** (not eligible; override not recommended; not park while desk items 1–3 above are open)

Evidence class: **observed model** at category level (fixes and builds are observed being offered and budgeted); the reliability retainer, which the viable solo case depends on, is a **hypothesis**

Required disclosure: Automation fixes and builds for small firms are **observed** — sellers ask $40–$695 for a fix, directory partners set $100–$1,000 minimums, and buyers post $750–$3,000 budgets for builds that include reliability requirements; these are asking prices and posted budgets, **not accepted prices or market rates**. How platforms stop workflows is **observed** in vendor documentation; how often it happens to a small firm and what it costs is **unknown**. The audit-and-fix price is **transferred** from build budgets. The monitoring retainer at $300–$500 a month is **modeled with no buyer price**, and it must beat a $29 monitoring tool plus a $150 fix. Without it the modeled business is small-build freelancing at roughly $40–$66 per hour worked before owner pay and tax. Every figure: `modeled scenario, not observed performance or an earnings forecast`. The $3,500 sprint is retired as a base case.

Rationale: The total holds at 69 but the composition changed. Desk work improved the buyer evidence and anchored most prices, and those anchors are adverse: the stand-alone reliability sprint does not survive observed buyer budgets, and the business only clears the imputed labour line if an unpriced monthly retainer sells against a cheaper substitute. Economics gains a point for input support, not attractiveness, and is capped because the decisive input is unsupported. Audience and analogy each lose a point on the same evidence. No factor was raised because owner field work was removed; retainer uptake and incident frequency stay scored as gaps and move to the viewer's first test. The candidate can teach an honest, smaller lesson, but it is not ready for owner promotion review, and the three validation artifacts must be reissued before any further score is meaningful. If a subsequent re-score falls below 65, the disposition's stated next step is park.
