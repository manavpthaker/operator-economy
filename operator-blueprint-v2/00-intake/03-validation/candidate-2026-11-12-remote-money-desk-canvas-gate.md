## Verdict

Verdict: pass

Evidence class: observed model

Failed checks: none

Required research before reconsideration: none for the gate. Open desk items that would strengthen
it: the payment mechanism a remote seller uses; two more banks' view-only pages; E&O and bond
quotes; a referral program that sends families to remote bill-pay services.

Required Canvas disclosure: Parent Bill Pay and Paytrust sell this remotely at published starting
prices of $250 and $500 a month; no record of families paying them was found. The one-operator
version, its price, household count, hours, and contribution are a modeled scenario, not observed
performance or an earnings forecast. Agent reading of household mail is untested. The three hard
limits are a non-lawyer's reading of federal rules and two or three states, not legal advice.
