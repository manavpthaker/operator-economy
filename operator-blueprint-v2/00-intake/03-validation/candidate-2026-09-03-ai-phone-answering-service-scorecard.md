## Recommendation

Decision: **parked**

Evidence class: **adjacent synthesis**

Required disclosure: Platform prices, infrastructure per-minute costs, receptionist wages, human-answering prices and the legal rules are **observed**. Home-services answer rates are **observed from two vendor datasets with conflicting definitions**, at 14% missed to 48% not reaching a person; the "62% unanswered" figure aired in EP002 is **excluded** as a 2016 SEO-agency post about 85 businesses. The managed-layer offer is **transferred** from managed-service models and is weak. Every operator-side figure is **modeled** and the base case barely clears imputed labor. Whether any contractor pays for the residual layer is **hypothetical** and untested. Nothing about disclosure, recording consent or the TCPA is legal advice.

Rationale: This scores 59, below the threshold and below the override band, and every hard gate passes. That combination is the correct output for a premise that is coherent, well-evidenced on the buyer's side, editorially strong, and commercially undercut. The package is better sourced than the one that produced EP002 — primary pricing pages, government wage data and primary law where the old brief had vendor blogs — and that better sourcing is exactly what shows the business is not there: the component the old episode sold is now a $29 line item in the buyer's own software, the build is a marketplace gig, and the only thing left to sell is a layer nobody has been shown to buy.

The decision is **parked**, not archived and not continue-research. It is not archived because there is a real operator business with a real buyer, a testable offer and a strong narrative. It is not continue-research because desk research cannot resolve the remaining question; only a first-party test can. Re-entry requires one of two things: a completed thirty-day test in which at least one contractor pays for the managed layer at a fee that clears imputed labor, or a buyer segment in which the vertical software has not absorbed the receptionist — which must be shown with current evidence, not assumed. If a re-entry package is filed, the legacy figures remain excluded and the 2026-12-03 refresh date applies to every platform claim.

One sourcing standard carries to any future editorial use of this material: CLM-020, CLM-023 and CLM-024 are secondary legal summaries. A script may say that disclosure and recording-consent rules exist and vary by state; it may not name a state and tell a viewer what they may do there without primary verification.
