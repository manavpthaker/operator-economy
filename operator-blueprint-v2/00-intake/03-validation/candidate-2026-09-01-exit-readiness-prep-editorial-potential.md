## Overall editorial verdict

Narrative engine: **pass**

Audience pull: **pass**

Overall editorial potential: **pass**

Story spine: `an owner has run a profitable business for 25 years and plans to sell "in a couple of years"` -> `a buyer runs diligence, finds the business is the owner, and reprices or walks` -> `value only transfers if the business runs without the person selling it, and roughly half of exits arrive with no warning at all` -> `a fixed-fee readiness practice that documents the operation and removes the owner from the critical path` -> `get paid years before the payoff, and stay on the right side of a licensing line that moves by state` -> `the viewer knows whether they can build it, what they may legally sell, and the condition under which it fails`

Working audience promise: The people who help owners sell only get paid when the deal closes. Here is the business that gets paid before it — and the legal line that decides whether you can run it.
