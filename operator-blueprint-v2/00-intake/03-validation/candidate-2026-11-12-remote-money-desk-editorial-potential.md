Narrative verdict: pass

Caveat (adversarial): the operating shape (agents read, one person owns the exceptions and the
record) is the same as EP010's receivables desk. A second "desk" episode right after EP010 risks
feeling like a repeat. What differs is the buyer (a family, not a business) and the legal limits,
which must carry the episode.
