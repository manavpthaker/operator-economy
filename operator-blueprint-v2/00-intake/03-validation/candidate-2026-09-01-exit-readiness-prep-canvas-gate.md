## Verdict

Verdict: **pass**

Evidence class: **adjacent synthesis**

Failed checks: none

Required research before reconsideration: none blocking. One item is escalated rather than resolved: **the brokerage perimeter requires written legal review before any script lock**, because it determines whether the business as designed is legal in the operator's state and the channel must not appear to rule on it.

Required Canvas disclosure:

1. This is an **adjacent synthesis**. No verified operator was found running a standalone fixed-fee readiness practice at disclosed economics.
2. **The revenue model is a legal consequence, not a preference.** The fee is fixed rather than success-based because success fees on a transaction trigger brokerage licensing in 16 states plus Illinois registration. California requires a broker or law licence to advise on a transaction at all.
3. **Nothing in the Canvas is legal advice.** The perimeter varies by state and the operator must verify locally.
4. **No multiple uplift is attributed to preparation.** The circulating prepared-versus-unprepared figures are published by firms selling preparation services and are excluded from the evidence base.
5. **The urgency is probabilistic, not scheduled.** The businesses this is modelled on sell against a dated audit. This one sells against the finding that roughly half of exits are involuntary and most listings never sell. That is a genuine argument and a weaker one, and the Canvas must present it as such.
6. **Every operator-side economic figure is modeled.** No observed readiness-engagement fee or hour count was found.
