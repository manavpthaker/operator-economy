### Search-volume exception

Exact-query volume is recorded as **not measurable** (CLM-008). A current measurement attempt was made on 2026-09-01 and failed for a documented tool limitation: no exact-volume tool is available to this operation, and the V1 intake of 2026-08-14 records that the Ahrefs plan carries no API access. Vendor-published growth percentages were found and are **not used** — they are marketing claims, not measurement.

The exception is properly invoked: consequential interest passes on CLM-002/CLM-003, and demand triangulation passes on three independent source families. "The market is new" is not offered as evidence anywhere in this package. Unknown volume is recorded as unknown, not as absence of demand.
