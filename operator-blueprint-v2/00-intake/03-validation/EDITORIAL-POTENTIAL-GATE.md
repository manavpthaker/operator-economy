This gate asks whether the opportunity can become a compelling Operator Economy episode. It is separate from business plausibility. A useful business idea can still fail editorially if it produces only a tool list, generic explainer, or static pitch deck.

## Narrative engine

The operator is the narrative center. A guest or conventional character arc is optional. The story may follow a person, business system, transaction, customer journey, or operating decision, but it must change causally over time.

Failure rule: A chronology, list of tools, market overview, or ten-slide taxonomy is not a narrative engine unless the elements form a causal operating journey.

## Audience pull

Search volume is one signal, not the definition of interest. An emerging topic can pass without measurable exact-query volume when the research documents current proxy demand.

| Check | Pass condition | Result | Evidence | Failure or caveat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Named audience job | The intended viewer recognizes a decision or problem they may plausibly face. | pass / fail | [artifact] | [limit] |
| Consequential interest | At least one usable direct or adjacent source shows problem behavior, spending, adoption, risk, or active buyer attention. | pass / fail | [claim IDs] | [limit] |
| Demand triangulation | At least two independent signals support interest. Signals may include exact or adjacent search demand, buyer behavior, transactions, adoption, recurring questions, community demand, or credible category research. | pass / fail | [claim IDs] | [limit] |
| Timely tension | The topic contains a current change, contradiction, threat, or opening rather than generic evergreen advice. | pass / fail | [claim IDs] | [limit] |
| Differentiated promise | Existing coverage leaves room for an Operator Economy synthesis, decision tool, or blueprint. | pass / fail | [competition review] | [limit] |
| Honest packaging | A compelling title-and-thumbnail premise can be stated without an unsupported outcome, fear claim, or earnings promise. | pass / fail | [working premise, not final packaging] | [limit] |

Audience-pull verdict: pass / fail

### Search-volume exception

Exact-query volume may be marked `not measurable` only when the package records a current measurement attempt and explains the tool or vocabulary limitation. The candidate must still pass consequential interest and demand triangulation. “The market is new” is not itself evidence of audience demand.
