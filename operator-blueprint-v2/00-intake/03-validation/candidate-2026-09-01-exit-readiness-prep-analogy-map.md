## Synthesis thesis

This business is like **due-diligence preparation** for assembling a defensible package against a scrutinising counterparty, like **SOC 2 / ISO readiness consulting** for fixed-scope remediation ahead of an external examination, and like a **technical audit** for the diagnostic sold before implementation. The new combination is readiness discipline applied to owner transition by a generalist, priced as a fixed fee **because** success fees trigger brokerage licensing.

## Evidence-floor verdict

- Usable direct or adjacent buyer/problem evidence: **pass** — ANA-001 is a direct relationship, and CLM-001/CLM-002 quantify it
- Valid analogy count: **five valid** (ANA-001, 003, 004, 005, 007-context), one weak (ANA-002), one rejected (ANA-006). Minimum 3 — pass
- Independent source families: **three** — professional-association closed-transaction data (CLM-003), a credentialing institute publishing national survey research (CLM-001, CLM-002), and state regulatory/legal sources (CLM-004). Minimum 2 — pass
- Required pillars covered: **pass**
- More than one untested inference hop in a load-bearing claim: **no.** The chain runs: owners are unprepared (CLM-002) → most listings do not sell and half of exits are unplanned (CLM-001) → buyers run competitive diligence (CLM-003) → preparation is work someone can be paid for. Each link has its own source. The hop that *would* have been speculative — that preparation causes a specific multiple uplift — is excluded as CLM-007 rather than assumed
- Uncovered pillars resolvable through the bounded validation plan: **yes**

Verdict: **pass**

Reason: The floor passes on stronger footing than the parked AI-visibility package. The buyer relationship is **direct** rather than adjacent, the consequence is quantified from two independent directions, and the pricing environment is anchored by closed-transaction data rather than seller asking prices. Two transfers were removed rather than stretched: ANA-006, because regulation prohibits it, and CLM-007, because it is a seller-published claim about the value of the service being sold — the precise source class that killed the previous candidate.

The concentrated weakness is ANA-002. The compliance-readiness parallel supplies the offer's structure but not its urgency, because SOC 2 and ISO have scheduled examinations and an exit does not. The partial mitigation is real and evidenced — roughly half of exits are involuntary, so the event is certain and its timing is not the owner's to choose — but it converts a deadline into a probability, which is a materially weaker sale. Whether owners act on it is the first thing the validation plan tests.

## Disclosure carried forward

The episode must describe an **adjacent synthesis**. No verified operator was found running a standalone fixed-fee readiness practice at disclosed economics.

The most important assumption the viewer must hear: **this business asks an owner to pay now for a payoff at an event with no date.** The businesses it is modelled on — SOC 2 and ISO readiness — sell against a scheduled audit. This one does not. The honest counter is that about half of exits are involuntary and most listed businesses never sell, so the preparation cannot safely wait for a date the owner picks. That is a genuine argument and it is weaker than a deadline, and the episode must present it as such rather than manufacturing urgency.

Two things must not be said. **No multiple uplift may be attributed to preparation** — the figures circulating to that effect are published by firms selling preparation and are excluded from this package. And **nothing about licensing may be presented as legal advice**; the brokerage perimeter varies by state, determines whether the practice is legal as designed, and belongs to the viewer's own counsel.
