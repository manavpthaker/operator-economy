## Recommendation

Decision: parked

Evidence class: observed model

Required disclosure: Observed: families describe the job in their own words; Parent Bill Pay and
Paytrust sell it remotely from $250 and $500 a month (seller copy); monitoring software sells the
watching for $7.49 to $26.99. Transferred: the local daily money manager's scope and insurance
norm to a remote desk. Modeled: every price, household count, hour, and contribution figure.
Hypothetical: that families will pay a remote desk more than the software, that agents cut the
reading time, and that referrers will send work to a remote desk. The three hard limits are a
non-lawyer's reading, not legal advice.

Rationale: Every hard gate passes, but the score is 56, well below 65. The points it lacks sit in
buyer payment, go to market, economics, and POV. The first three are questions only the field can
answer (they belong in the viewer's first test, not in more desk passes), and this run showed the
POV is narrower than thought: the record and separation rules are the CFPB's own advice. Two or
three more desk items (payment mechanism, more bank pages, insurance quotes) could add perhaps two
to four points, not nine. With EP010 just finished on the same operating shape, the honest call is
to park and free the bench for the next shortlisted lead. Reopening conditions are in the
disposition record; none of them is owner field work.
