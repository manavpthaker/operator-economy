## Reason for stopping

- Failed or incomplete requirement: business fit and evidence
- Plain-English finding: The business EP002 described no longer exists for an independent operator. Jobber ships the AI receptionist inside its plan at $29 a month (general availability 2025-08-18), Housecall Pro and ServiceTitan sell it as an add-on, and the build itself lists on Fiverr and Upwork for tens of dollars. The only residual layer (emergency-escalation design, state-correct AI disclosure and recording consent, a monthly answer-rate report) has zero evidence that any contractor pays for it, and the legal surface forces the buyer to own the account, which removes the operator's margin. The '62% of calls go unanswered' figure that aired in EP002 traces to a January 2016 SEO-agency post about 85 businesses and is excluded.
- Claims or assumptions that remain unusable:
  - The '62% unanswered' and 'only 38% answered' statistics in any form.
  - Any missed-call, per-minute margin, fee, or income figure from the legacy package.
  - Any statement of state consent, chatbot-disclosure, or TCPA rules presented as legal advice or naming a state without primary verification.
  - Vendor survey results as demand evidence; every demand signal found is an interested party.
