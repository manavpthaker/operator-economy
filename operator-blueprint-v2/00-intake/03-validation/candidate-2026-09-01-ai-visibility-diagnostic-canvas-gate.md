## Verdict

Verdict: **pass**

Evidence class: **adjacent synthesis**

Failed checks: none

Required research before reconsideration: none blocking. Two questions are deliberately routed to the operator's own validation rather than resolved here — real delivery hours at a defensible repetition count, and real run-to-run variance. Both are measurable in the thirty-day plan and neither can honestly be settled from public sources.

Required Canvas disclosure:

1. This is an **adjacent synthesis**. No verified operator was found running this service at disclosed economics. The Canvas presents a business to test, not one observed working.
2. **Every economic figure is modeled.** Price ranges come from seller-published pricing pages that verify only what those firms ask, not what any buyer paid. Capacity, hours and contribution are arithmetic on assumptions.
3. **The intervention claim is rejected, not merely hedged.** Independent survey of the 2023–2026 literature finds no stable, cross-platform causal effect on organic discoverability. The Canvas must not present optimisation as a reliable remedy, and must not repeat the "40% improvement" figure as a client-achievable result.
4. **The measurement is unstable and that shapes the product.** A defensible baseline needs roughly seven to eight captures per question. This is the dominant cost driver and the reason the honest service may not clear at the price the market appears to anchor.
5. **The cost of the problem is unknown.** Absence from AI answers has no published revenue consequence. The Canvas must state this rather than imply a recoverable loss.
