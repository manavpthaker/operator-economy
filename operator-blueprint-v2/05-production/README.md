# Step 5: production

Status: **governed by `docs/blueprint-cinema.md` (owner decision, 2026-09-24).** No V2 standard of
its own yet. Until one is approved, Blueprint Cinema's production order, contracts and references
(`blueprint-cinema/references/`) are the authority for this stage. They carry no hash-bound
approval gates of the kind Step 3 has.

In Blueprint Cinema terms this stage runs from asset tickets, candidates and selects through
HyperFrames graphic and scene plates, including the presenter look lock and
`blueprint-cinema/references/PRESENTER-RECIPE.md`.

This stage will source, license, capture, generate, design, and assemble the approved footage, archival material, documents, graphics, interfaces, and synthetic plates required by the production plan.
