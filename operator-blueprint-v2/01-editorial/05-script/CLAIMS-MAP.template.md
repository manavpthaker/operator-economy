## Coverage rule

Inventory every script statement that introduces or changes:

- A number, date, population, prevalence, magnitude, or comparison.
- Market size, demand, willingness to pay, price typicality, or acquisition pace.
- A named company, product, institution, study, law, or regulation.
- Causality, outcome, safety, legal, or performance meaning.
- A modeled economics input or interpretation.
- A public category title, spoken company name, internal operating description, or statement that could imply an established market category.
- A company-level BUILD verdict when its surrounding language could be mistaken for proof of demand, price, results, or sustainability.

Plausibility, cautious tone, or words such as `can`, `may`, `many`, `most`, `around`, `up to`, `conservative`, `reasonable`, `realistic`, `achievable`, and `should` do not replace evidence.
