## Owner corrections

The v1.2 controls were materially better in conviction, analogy, and point of view, but their openings still sounded like written arguments. They entered the thesis before a new listener understood the normal system, provided no distinct cold open or recurring show identity, and allowed dense polished sentences to pass as natural speech.

The owner also corrected the proposed show identity. Operator Economy is not primarily a show about discovering overlooked business opportunities. It exists to help a capable professional build, own, and operate a sustainable business of one using AI.

The core audience includes:

- an employed professional exploring a path to independence;
- a capable professional between roles who needs a credible route to income; and
- a builder who already has an idea or practice but needs operating guidance.

The shared thesis is:

> You do not need a huge share of the market. You need a reachable share of a problem large and costly enough to support one operator.

This is a business-design standard, not an earnings promise. Every episode must show the assumptions and real-world tests required before the model could responsibly be called sustainable.

## Proposed fixed identity

Use exactly once after the cold open and silent identity sting:

> This is The Operator Economy, where we show you how to build, own, and operate a sustainable business of one using AI.

The following sentence is episode-specific. It identifies the business being examined, the role of one operator, and the useful result the viewer will receive.

## Proposed structural correction

Add an approved episode beat sheet before the outline and require this opening grammar:

```text
concrete cold open
→ silent identity sting
→ fixed brand string
→ episode-specific promise
→ context runway
→ central question
→ earned thesis
```

The body remains episode-agnostic but must perform these functions in the causal order the subject requires:

```text
reported problem and fair context
→ hidden mechanism or reveal
→ operator opportunity
→ buyer, offer, and result
→ delivery and stack
→ go-to-market and economics
→ boundaries and failure modes
→ first validation test
→ opening callback and verdict
```

The Operator Canvas tests business completeness. The episode beat sheet controls what a listener needs to understand next. Neither becomes a mandatory slide order.

## Proposed business-of-one test

Every episode must make four things concrete:

1. What AI makes cheaper, faster, or newly possible.
2. Which judgment, accountability, relationship, and consequence remain with the operator.
3. What customer count, transaction volume, price, delivery load, tools, acquisition cost, support burden, and risk would have to reconcile for one owner.
4. Why a small reachable share of the approved problem could be enough, and which first test could disprove that assumption.

“Business of one” means one accountable owner can sell and deliver the initial model with AI, software, and bounded specialist help. It does not forbid contractors or later growth. It forbids treating a model that requires a large team, outside capital, or broad market capture before it can work as a proven one-person entry model.

## Proposed voice and first-listen correction

The craft calibration becomes:

> 60 Minutes reporting bones, a plainspoken and mildly bemused human surface, Modern MBA business context, restrained Last Week Tonight reveals, and Manav's operator judgment.

The owner's shorthand, “Nate Bargatze doing 60 Minutes,” defines structure and temperature, not imitation.

V1.3 therefore:

- begins with an ordinary person, transaction, workflow, or consequence rather than an abstract thesis;
- explains the normal system and fair case before the strongest ruling;
- limits the first-listen load to one new abstraction at a time;
- treats analogy as causal explanation rather than decoration;
- uses dry understatement after comprehension rather than jokes during explanation;
- fails a succession of mic-drop fragments even when sentence-length metrics look varied;
- requires the ending to resolve or reinterpret the opening; and
- keeps the narrator curious at the start and decisive by the end.

`SCRIPT-BEAT-RESEARCH.md` records the source sample and limits. Named creators and programs contribute high-level craft functions only. Their recognizable wording, jokes, routines, personas, and vocal mannerisms are prohibited.
