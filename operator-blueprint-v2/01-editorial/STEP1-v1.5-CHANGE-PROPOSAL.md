## Why this proposal exists

The AI Visibility v0.2 fixture passed its mechanical checks and seven reviewer lanes, but it failed the owner's actual read. The script still sounded like a report in several places, weakened its own company premise, made the travel example sound like market advice, repeated conversational markers, and moved between scenes without enough causal inheritance. The later horizontal rewrite also exposed that the fixture Canvas still contained the retired travel denominator, so the proposal now requires a pitch-deck coverage map across VO, visuals, and the downloadable Canvas.

That failure exposed a control problem. The approved v1.4 process could produce clean artifacts and positive reviews without proving that the complete script sounded natural to the owner. It also did not separate conversational attribution from the exact citation material needed on screen and in show notes.

The v0.7 revision repaired those issues and passed its independent reviews. The owner still returned `REVISE` because the cold open did not reveal the operator or business payoff before the silent sting, the fixed show string did not express AI as the tool in the approved order, and the ending did not fully resolve the opening or earn a like-and-subscribe request.

This proposal therefore governs both argument quality and episode framing. It keeps evidence precise, makes the opening promise worth staying for, fixes the show identity, and requires the ending to deliver the promised operator action before asking anything from the viewer.

A later read-only audit found one more control gap. The seven v0.8 reviewer lanes did not establish that the complete lexical surface sounded like Manav. They established lane-specific safety, clarity, structure, and predicted performability, then routed the script to the owner. The labels made those findings look more conclusive than their evidence. This proposal now separates first-listen clarity, lexical performability, and positive hosted-voice identity. It does not retroactively relabel the frozen fixture files.

The first voice-only conform, v0.9, proved the new control could reject its own overcorrection. V1.0 repaired the spoken surface, but the owner identified two upstream failures that a voice-only conform could not solve: the voice functions were not distilled into an original Manav-hosted authority, and the episode explained a service without pitching the market opportunity. Travel also carried too much of the company definition, prospect logic, and economics.

V1.1 therefore reopens the research amendment, claims map, Investment Thesis, narrative, beats, outline, voice map, and script. It preserves the macro episode movement while rebuilding the opportunity pitch and making the company horizontal.

6. A scene transition must inherit and advance the prior scene's question, noun, decision, image, or consequence. A heading or connective phrase alone is not a transition.

8. The cold open must create a concrete problem, reveal the specific operator or business payoff before the silent sting, and leave the mechanism, proof, and final thesis to be earned after the sting.

20. Do not derive a prospect pool or market-share claim from an unobserved conversion rate.

## Proposed controls

### Three evidence-delivery layers

Every material external fact receives:

1. Conversational and audio-honest voiceover with every limitation that changes the conclusion.
2. An on-screen source receipt with exact source, artifact, date, population or sample when relevant, figure, locator, and claim ID.
3. A full show-note citation with direct link, publication and access details, locator, method note, known limitation, and Step 0 provenance.

The voiceover cannot rely on the picture to become honest. The source receipt cannot force the narrator to read bibliographic metadata that does not change the spoken conclusion. Money, forecasts, constructed cases, and outcome claims still require an audible plain-English boundary.

### Operator-market fit

Before using the episode's example market, the script teaches the viewer to begin where at least one earned advantage overlaps with a reachable buyer:

- relevant professional experience
- adjacent client or operating work
- a trusted relationship or real network
- enough buyer-language fluency to understand the decision
- decision-makers the operator can credibly contact.

Experience and access improve judgment and entry. They do not prove demand.

### Causal scene seams

Every adjacent narrated sequence is tested by reading the outgoing sequence's final two spoken lines directly into the incoming sequence's first two spoken lines without headings. The incoming sequence must inherit and advance something specific. It fails if it restarts the subject, redefines the company, behaves like a deck heading, or could follow almost any prior sequence.

The silent identity sting and brand reset is the one routine exception and must be recorded rather than silently counted as a pass.

### Spoken-language integrity

The same discourse marker may not open multiple scenes. Report-style source summaries, approval-rubric language, overloaded economics, and phrases a professional understands but would not say to a colleague are word-level failures. Step 2 may direct performance, but it may not repair them by changing the locked words.

### Proposed hosted-long-form routing

For this proposed Step 1 application, Content OS documentary rigor governs evidence, attribution, uncertainty, fairness, and release hygiene. The observed Manav speech profile governs the lexical surface: viewer relationship, working-through language, analogy, reaction, commercial judgment, and practical instruction. The approved contract, investment thesis, narrative, beats, outline, and claims map continue to govern meaning.

The Content OS authority candidate now explicitly routes YouTube VO to an original Manav-hosted Operator Economy register. Documentary rigor governs truth. The hosted register governs viewer relationship, investigative movement, analogy-led reasoning, dry systemic skepticism, and commercial guidance. Named creators, ratios, personas, routines, and recognizable mannerisms are prohibited from production prompts.

### Opportunity-scale argument

Every Episode Investment Thesis must connect:

1. the underlying costly behavior or responsibility;
2. an established adjacent budget or accountable function;
3. at least two observed operating layers;
4. the proposed business-of-one synthesis;
5. the analogy breaks and unknowns; and
6. bottom-up engagement, capacity, and validation requirements.

The evidence pattern may use the same job across scales, budget migration, a fragmented buyer pool, or a documented combination. Funding, valuation, total company revenue, vendor marketing, or one top-down estimate cannot pass the gate alone.

### Horizontal buyer and example boundary

A horizontal company must state qualified and unsuitable buyer rules without an industry name. It may use up to three examples to clarify distinct buying decisions. The company must remain understandable after every example is removed.

No example industry may control the cold open, company definition, total market, prospect denominator, or economics unless the approved thesis selects it as the recommended market.

### Opportunity-to-blueprint balance

The spoken script targets 55 percent opportunity pitch and 45 percent operator blueprint. An opportunity share from 52 to 58 percent passes. The detailed offer begins only after the behavior shift, economic consequence, adjacent budget, comparable-business ladder, missing responsibility, company thesis, and operator opening are earned.

### Positive hosted-voice evidence

Voice review now records three separate findings:

1. First-listen clarity.
2. Lexical performability of the exact words.
3. Positive hosted-voice identity.

The third finding requires exact script evidence across the opening, opportunity case, operator build, economics, and ending. Each passage must perform a natural observed Manav function and survive a neutral-documentary substitution challenge. Reviewers may return `revise`, `no lane-specific blocker`, or `clear for owner voice test`. They may not issue an owner-level voice pass from negative hygiene.

The five functions are not quotas for catchphrases, contractions, self-repairs, jokes, sentence lengths, or borrowed mannerisms. The owner completes the test by reading the entire clean hash and answering: “Does this sound like me talking, not merely like a good Operator Economy script?” Only `yes` can establish positive hosted-voice identity.

### Pre-sting operator payoff

The cold open must establish a recognizable problem and make the operator or business destination specific before the silent identity sting. It may name the responsibility, company shape, useful decision, or proposed first product the viewer will learn to build.

The cold open is still not the complete thesis. It teases the destination while withholding the mechanism, evidence, economics, validation results, and final editorial ruling that the episode must earn. The final spoken line before silence must complete the viewer contract and hand naturally into the sting.

### Fixed show identity

The exact fixed string is:

> This is The Operator Economy, where we show you how to use AI to build, own, and operate a sustainable business of one.

`Business of one` is the fixed phrase. `On your own` is not a substitute because the operating model may use bounded specialists or contractors. `Use AI to build` keeps the business as the object and AI as the enabling tool.

The fixed string follows the silent sting. The episode-specific promise follows the fixed string and may not repeat the complete cold open.

### Earned ending and final CTA

The ending must proceed in this order:

1. Return to the opening image, contradiction, or question.
2. State the company or operator payoff the episode has now earned.
3. Land BUILD without converting a model into proof of demand, results, sustainability, or income.
4. State the first construction or validation action.
5. End with one natural like-and-subscribe sentence tied to the value of returning.

The final CTA may not introduce a new business claim, stack a download or comment request, or tease an unlocked next episode. It is part of the locked script, not an edit-room addition.

### Step 1 and Step 2 lexical boundary

Step 1 locks the words of the cold-open payoff, fixed show string, closing resolution, BUILD instruction, first action, and final CTA. Step 2 may shape pace, emphasis, energy, and silence around those words. It may not invent, rewrite, reorder, replace, or improvise any of them.

### Decisive owner cold read

Before E6, the owner reads the complete clean script continuously and without annotations. The record captures:

- the exact script and read-through hashes
- any line instinctively rewritten
- any seam where the argument was lost
- any report-like, dense, repetitive, or inauthentic passage
- the owner's pass or revise decision.

Any word change creates a new hash, regenerates the read-through, and reruns affected reviews before another complete owner cold read.
