# Step 1 v1.2 Change Proposal: Conviction, Comedy, Analogy, and Cadence

Status: regression complete; owner found material improvement but requested structural revision; superseded by v1.3; never canonical

Opened: 2026-08-22

Requested by: Manav Thaker

Previous owner-approved version: `operator-blueprint-v2-step1-v1.0`

Rejected intermediate proposal: `operator-blueprint-v2-step1-v1.1`

Proposed version: `operator-blueprint-v2-step1-v1.2`

## Owner correction

The v1.1 scripts sounded like cautious explainers. Repeated “hypothetical,” “does not prove,” and “this is not real” constructions protected the evidence boundary by draining authority from the narrator. The scripts did not sound like an opinionated operator, did not use the intended craft-reference blend with enough nuance, and underused Manav's analogy-heavy rhythmic cadence.

Required direction:

- The base persona is a seasoned operator, career advisor, and strategist giving away the blueprint.
- The Gawdat/Mulaney/Maher craft functions sit on top of that base.
- A Last Week Tonight-style engine makes the work informative and entertaining through research, systemic critique, escalation, earned contempt, sarcasm, and dry wit.
- Manav's personal contribution is analogy as explanation and cadence with rhythm, not rhyme.
- The narrator has a view. Confidence and conviction are required.
- Evidence classification remains rigorous, but qualifications appear once at the point of risk and lead to a decision instead of becoming a running apology.

## Proposed correction

- Add a layered V2 long-form voice architecture rather than a flat adjective list.
- Apply the existing 39/41/20 craft blend to V2 OE long-form as an overlay on the operator-advisor base.
- Treat the Oliver layer as an episode engine, not another impression or percentage.
- Add explicit stance, verdict, contempt-target, comedic-decode, analogy-family, callback, and cadence planning before script drafting.
- Replace “visible hypothetical” as a blanket spoken requirement with an evidence-efficient scenario rule.
- Add a disclaimer and hedge audit that fails defensive repetition.
- Require each major act to land a position, not merely summarize evidence.
- Require evidence that humor clarifies the mechanism and that contempt targets systems, incentives, hype, or lazy conventions rather than people.
- Rewrite both v1.1 fixture scripts from v0.3 to v0.4 and rerun claim, voice, conviction, comedy, analogy, cadence, and non-caricature reviews.

## Authority precedence during the proposal

- Content OS universal prohibitions, truth hygiene, punctuation rules, and evidence boundaries remain live authority.
- `EDITORIAL-VOICE-STANDARD.md` and `VOICE-ARCHITECTURE.md` propose the V2 OE long-form register-specific layer requested by the owner.
- Where the current `content-os/voice.md` scopes 39/41/20 to personal surfaces, this proposal records an explicit V2 long-form exception. It does not change other OE surfaces or the sibling repository.
- Step 2 still cannot rewrite locked words to add personality, jokes, analogies, stance, or connective language.

## Approval boundary

Step 1 v1.0 remains the last owner-approved authority. V1.1 is rejected. V1.2 may become canonical only after the revised documents and fixtures pass, final hashes are frozen, and the owner explicitly approves the voice.

Regression result: both v0.4 controls received claims and E5V pass recommendations. See `fixtures/STEP1-v1.2-VOICE-TEST-RESULTS.md`. The owner did not approve them. The scripts still opened without enough context, lacked a distinct cold open and recurring show identity, sounded dense in places, and allowed polished written sentences to pass as speech. V1.3 supersedes this proposal without rewriting its frozen fixture evidence.
