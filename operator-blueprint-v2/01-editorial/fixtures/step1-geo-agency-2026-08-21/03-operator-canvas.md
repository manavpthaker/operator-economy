# Operator Canvas: AI visibility diagnostic

Status: locked for dry-run testing only

Episode: unassigned fixture

Overall evidence class: adjacent synthesis

## Operator

An experienced SEO, content, digital-strategy, or competitive-intelligence practitioner who can design a research sample, validate sources, communicate uncertainty, and talk with marketing buyers. `PARALLEL`: Step 0 candidate and ANA-002 through ANA-004.

The operator does not need to train models or promise algorithmic control. A beginner reselling a dashboard without research judgment is not a fit.

## Buyer and first test segment

Economic buyer: A marketing, content, or organic-growth leader responsible for digital discovery at a consumer-facing brand. `OBSERVED/PARALLEL`: CLM-001 through CLM-007.

First test segment: Consumer travel brands with an existing SEO or content responsibility. `MODELED`: selected to create a bounded, discovery-dependent test; Step 0 does not prove this segment is superior.

Beneficiary: The consumer using AI-assisted research to compare options.

Purchase trigger: A planning cycle, brand-positioning concern, unexplained discrepancy between conventional search performance and AI-answer presence, or executive request for a baseline. `MODELED`.

## Costly problem

Conventional search reporting can look healthy while AI-assisted answers describe, omit, or compare the brand using a different mix of owned and third-party sources. The buyer lacks a repeatable baseline for important questions, answer accuracy, cited-source patterns, and controllable gaps. `OBSERVED/PARALLEL`: CLM-001 through CLM-005.

The financial consequence is unknown. The service cannot claim a recoverable revenue loss.

## Offer

A fixed-scope AI discovery diagnostic for one brand, one market, and a buyer-approved set of ten high-value questions across two answer surfaces.

Included: scope, question design, captured answer sample, mention and description log, citation and source map, factual accuracy review against approved brand documents, competitor context, controllability classification, prioritized actions, and a repeat-measurement plan.

Excluded: guaranteed placement, content production, digital PR execution, recurring monitoring, and implementation unless separately scoped after the diagnostic.

Evidence status: `PARALLEL/MODELED`, supported by ANA-002 through ANA-004.

## Buyer result

The buyer receives a decision-grade baseline and knows:

1. Where the brand appears in the selected sample.
2. How it is described.
3. Which sources are cited.
4. Which important facts are inaccurate or unsupported.
5. Which gaps appear controllable, influenceable, or outside the buyer's control.
6. Which actions deserve a test.
7. How and when the sample will be repeated.

This is an evidence and prioritization result, not a ranking, traffic, lead, or revenue result.

## Delivery system

1. Confirm buyer, market, brand facts, permissions, and decision question.
2. Agree on ten category, comparison, and brand-specific questions.
3. Capture answers across two selected surfaces at a recorded time and configuration.
4. Log presence, description, competitors, citations, variance, and uncertainty.
5. Trace important cited sources and compare claims with approved brand source-of-truth documents.
6. Classify gaps as controllable, influenceable, or not currently controllable.
7. Recommend a small set of factual, owned-source, distribution, or monitoring actions without promising outcome.
8. Repeat the sample after an agreed interval and report differences with the same limits.

Human judgment owns question design, fact validation, source interpretation, controllability, comparative claims, and client communication.

## Go-to-market path

1. Choose one discovery-dependent segment.
2. Identify 25 brands already investing in SEO or content.
3. Prepare a small public-information screen using three questions, clearly labeled as a sample rather than a diagnosis.
4. Ask the marketing owner whether the discrepancy affects a real planning or content decision.
5. Sell the complete fixed-scope diagnostic only when the buyer can name the decision it should inform.
6. Test agency and specialist referrals as a secondary channel.

The first channel and conversion rate are `MODELED/UNKNOWN`.

## Economics

The validation scenario uses one $2,000 diagnostic, 16 delivery hours, a $60-per-hour labor allowance, $99 of measurement software, and $300 of acquisition and listed overhead. The arithmetic leaves $641 before tax and unlisted costs.

The working scenario uses two $3,000 diagnostics, 48 delivery hours total, $198 of software, and $600 of acquisition and listed overhead. The arithmetic leaves $2,322 after the labor allowance and before tax and unlisted costs.

Every figure is a scenario except the adjacent SEO hourly evidence and displayed vendor price. Buyer acceptance, actual hours, utilization, additional software, refunds, insurance, legal review, and client results are unknown. This is a modeled scenario, not observed performance or an earnings forecast.

Most sensitive assumption: A buyer will pay enough for expert interpretation and decision value without requiring a guaranteed placement or revenue result.

## Risks and kill conditions

- Buyers see no decision value beyond existing SEO reporting.
- Measurements vary too much to support a responsible baseline.
- Existing agencies absorb the work at no separate budget.
- The service becomes dashboard resale with no differentiated judgment.
- A buyer requires guaranteed placement, traffic, leads, or revenue.
- Tool cost, coverage, terms, or client complexity breaks the hour model.
- Comparative or factual claims require specialist legal or regulated-category review.

## First 30-day test

Interview five marketing or organic-growth leaders in the selected segment. Run one consenting manual ten-question baseline across two surfaces. Track all delivery time. Offer the completed scope at a fixed validation price.

Continue only if at least three buyers independently describe a consequential decision, at least one accepts a paid diagnostic or gives a specific budget-backed purchase condition, and the evidence can be repeated within 24 hours of work.

Redesign or stop if buyers see no material decision value, the sample is not responsibly repeatable, the service requires outcome guarantees to sell, or a realistic price cannot cover delivery and acquisition effort.
