# Operator review: GEO script v0.1

Status: completed against immutable `07-script-v0.1.md`

Verdict: revise

## Findings

### GEO-OP-01 — Buyer decision arrives too late

Severity: high

The script defines the deliverable before making one buyer decision concrete. “Planning, content, reputation, or measurement” is still a menu. The revision should carry one example decision through the episode, such as deciding which factual source gap deserves a correction test.

### GEO-OP-02 — Segment choice is honest but strategically unsupported

Severity: medium

Consumer travel is correctly labeled a test choice. The script should explain that the operator selects a segment based on reach and discovery dependence, not imply the vertical emerged from the evidence.

### GEO-OP-03 — Delivery ownership is strong

Severity: positive

The script distinguishes tool collection from human research design, source judgment, permissions, and client communication. Preserve this.

### GEO-OP-04 — Economics support a decision rather than an earnings pitch

Severity: positive

The script says the commercially important inputs are unobserved and uses the model to expose failure sensitivity. Preserve the disclosure and do not add a higher upside case.

## Recommendation

Revise S01, S04, and S05 around one recurring buyer decision. Keep the rest of the operating model.
