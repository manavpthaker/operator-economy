# Episode beat sheet v0.5: AI visibility diagnostic

Status: approved for v1.3 fixture regression only; production use prohibited

Episode: unassigned fixture

Working promise: show how to test a bounded service that helps a company understand its appearance in AI answers and decide what deserves action without promising control of the answer.

Central question: Can an operator turn the gap between ordinary search reporting and AI answers into a useful diagnostic without selling ranking theater?

Central thesis to earn later: the first business is evidence plus a decision, not control over AI recommendations.

Recurring carrier: one travel marketing lead looking at a green search dashboard while a customer sees a missing or stale AI answer.

## First-listen controls

- “GEO” is not required in the cold open.
- The first evidence block appears only after the ordinary search-report chain is clear.
- The committee analogy follows the literal explanation of multiple answer sources.
- The offer, buyer, economics, and kill test occupy separate beats.
- Each economics scenario receives a plain restatement before the next figure.
- No beat combines a new technical term, several numbers, a new analogy, and a joke.

## Identity

Fixed brand string:

> This is The Operator Economy, where we show you how to build, own, and operate a sustainable business of one using AI.

Episode-specific promise: show what one operator would sell to help companies understand how they appear in AI answers and what would have to be true for the work to support a business of one.

AI role: collect and compare answer samples and source patterns; the operator retains research design, fact-checking, permissions, source judgment, and the final recommendation.

Reachable-share logic: the operator does not need the entire travel market. The test asks whether a reachable set of brands has enough decision value to support a small number of responsibly delivered diagnostics. The fixture does not yet prove that it does.

## Ending loop

Opening question: Which view should the marketing lead trust?

Resolution: the search report may be accurate but incomplete; the diagnostic records the second doorway and turns it into a bounded decision.

Final action: run the diagnostic manually before building a recurring service.
