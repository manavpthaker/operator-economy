# Claims review: GEO script v0.1

Status: completed against immutable `07-script-v0.1.md`

Verdict: revise

## Findings

### GEO-CL-01 — McKinsey sample and year need precise treatment

Severity: medium

The sentence is supportable through CLM-003, but the review copy should say “in its August 2025 survey of 1,927 U.S. consumers” or use a less precise paraphrase. Do not imply half of all consumers.

### GEO-CL-02 — “Cannot show” is too absolute

Severity: high

The opening says the conventional dashboard “cannot answer” what happens in AI discovery. The package supports that conventional reporting does not fully reveal the answer-and-source state. Use “does not fully show,” because some tools or teams may already include AI reporting.

### GEO-CL-03 — Illustrative travel brand must remain visibly hypothetical

Severity: high

No real brand case exists. The revision may use a recurring illustrative brand only if it is introduced as a hypothetical and never receives invented metrics or outcomes.

### GEO-CL-04 — Economics qualifiers pass

Severity: positive

The script identifies the model inputs, missing costs, and non-forecast status. Every number maps to CLM-011. Publication approval is still absent.

### GEO-CL-05 — Search-volume claim is unnecessary

Severity: positive

The script does not turn missing keyword data into a demand claim. Keep C009 out of the spoken body unless a limit needs explanation.
