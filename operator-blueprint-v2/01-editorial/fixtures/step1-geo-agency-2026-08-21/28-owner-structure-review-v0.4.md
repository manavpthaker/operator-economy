## What improved

- More conviction and a clearer point of view.
- Less defensive qualification.
- A useful committee-and-minutes analogy.
- A practical diagnostic, delivery model, economics test, and stop rules.

## What still failed

1. The script jumped directly into the argument without enough background for a new viewer.
2. “A travel brand can look healthy in search and still show up wrong in an AI answer” functioned as a compressed thesis rather than a real cold open.
3. There was no distinct silent identity break, recurring Operator Economy brand string, or episode-specific show string.
4. The main ruling arrived before the normal search system, the changed discovery path, and the buyer's confusion had been patiently explained.
5. Several passages remained dense and written rather than spoken.
6. “This is not a contradiction. It is a measurement failure” is the clearest failure. It is polished on paper, but it is not how a colleague would naturally explain the problem.
7. The gate rewarded short declaratives and quotable landings without detecting a punch-line metronome.

## Required recovery

- Open on the marketing lead and two conflicting experiences.
- Leave a practical question unresolved before the show identity.
- Add a silent identity sting.
- Use the fixed brand string and one episode-specific promise.
- Explain the familiar search-reporting system and the new AI-answer path before stating the offer thesis.
- Replace abstract compression with ordinary explanation.
- Let the committee analogy enter after the literal mechanism is understandable.
- Resolve the green dashboard and second doorway at the end.
