# Performance review: GEO script v0.1

Status: completed against immutable `07-script-v0.1.md`

Verdict: revise before lock

## Findings

### GEO-PF-01 — Dense taxonomy in S04

Severity: high

“Date, context, answer, brand presence, competitors, citations, and visible variation” is hard to retain aloud. Break the work into three spoken passes: capture the answer, trace the sources, then classify the gap.

### GEO-PF-02 — Too many abstract nouns

Severity: medium

“Decision-grade visibility and source intelligence” works in the Canvas but feels like report language in narration. Prefer “evidence the buyer can use to decide what to fix.”

### GEO-PF-03 — Economics need breath groups

Severity: medium

The modeled numbers are transparent but arrive as two long accounting sentences. Separate price, labor, direct costs, and what remains. Slow down the disclaimer rather than treating it as parenthetical language.

### GEO-PF-04 — Pronunciation register

Severity: low

Record preferred pronunciation for GEO if the acronym is spoken. The current draft avoids it in narration; preserve that unless Step 2 receives direction.

## Recommendation

Revise for spoken grouping without reducing the evidence qualifiers.
