Status: completed against immutable `07-script-v0.1.md`

Verdict: revise

## Findings

### GEO-ST-01 — The middle becomes a competent workshop

Severity: high

S03 through S06 explain the service clearly but lose the opening brand. Keep one hypothetical brand and one question present through the baseline, source discovery, classification, and buyer decision. This remains an illustrative thread, not a fabricated case study.

### GEO-ST-02 — The central tension is the control boundary

Severity: positive

The strongest line of movement is not “AI search is growing.” It is the operator learning what can be observed, influenced, or not controlled. Make that three-state distinction the spine.

### GEO-ST-03 — List rhythm flattens S07

Severity: medium

“First, second, third, fourth” reads as a risk slide. Tie each failure to the central decision: no budget, no repeatability, no judgment value, or a required guarantee.

### GEO-ST-04 — Ending earns the test

Severity: positive

“The first product is evidence about whether a useful business exists” resolves the episode honestly. Preserve it.
