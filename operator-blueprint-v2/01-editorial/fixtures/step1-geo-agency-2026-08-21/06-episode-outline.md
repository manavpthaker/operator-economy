# Episode outline: AI visibility diagnostic

Status: approved for dry-run script drafting only

Working promise: Show how to assemble and test an AI visibility diagnostic without promising rankings or revenue.

Target test-script duration: 6-8 minutes

## Sequence map

| ID | Narrative job | Viewer question | Canvas coverage | Claims | Turn |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S01 | Establish the blind spot and promise | What can the old dashboard not tell us? | Problem, why now | C001-C003 | Healthy search performance does not answer the AI-discovery question. |
| S02 | Explain the mechanism | Why does the blind spot exist? | Problem, measurement | C002, C005, C006 | The source mix varies; the operator needs a sample, not a certainty claim. |
| S03 | Define the business | What is actually being sold? | Offer, buyer result | C004, C007 | The product is decision-grade evidence, not rankings. |
| S04 | Walk the delivery loop | How would the operator produce it? | Delivery, stack, controls | C005-C007 | Tools collect; the operator judges. |
| S05 | Define buyer and acquisition | Who might pay and how do we test that? | Buyer, go to market | C004, C007 | A decision must exist before the diagnostic has value. |
| S06 | Expose economics | What would have to be true? | Price, capacity, contribution | C008 | Transparent math reveals the fragility of the offer. |
| S07 | Stress the model | What could break? | Risks, objections | C002, C008, C009 | Existing SEO, platform variance, or weak attribution may kill it. |
| S08 | Resolve with validation | What should the viewer do first? | 30-day test | C007-C009 | Test buyer value before building a GEO agency. |

## Opening contract

Establish that a conventional dashboard cannot show the complete answer-and-source state. Promise a business blueprint and a falsifiable test, not a ranking tactic.

## Ending

The viewer should leave knowing that the first product is not optimization. It is evidence about whether a useful, repeatable buyer decision exists at all.
