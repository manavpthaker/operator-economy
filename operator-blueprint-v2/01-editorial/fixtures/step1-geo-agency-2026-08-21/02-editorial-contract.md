# Editorial contract: AI visibility diagnostic

Status: approved for dry-run testing only

Episode: unassigned fixture

## Viewer

Primary viewer: An experienced search, content, or digital-strategy professional evaluating whether AI-assisted discovery creates a responsible specialist service they could test.

Starting state: The viewer sees growing interest in GEO but cannot tell whether there is a real buyer problem, a sellable offer, or merely a new dashboard category.

Ending capability: The viewer can define a narrow buyer, explain the measurement boundary, assemble a diagnostic workflow, model the labor and costs, identify the weakest assumptions, and design a paid validation test without promising rankings or revenue.

## Episode job

Working promise: Show how to assemble and test an AI visibility diagnostic for consumer brands without pretending the business or its results are already proven.

Central argument: The responsible product is not “rank higher in ChatGPT.” It is a bounded evidence service that maps important questions, observed answers, cited sources, factual gaps, controllable actions, and repeat measurement so a buyer can decide what is worth changing.

Narrative question: Can an experienced search or content operator turn a real discovery blind spot into a useful paid diagnostic when neither placement nor business impact is under their control?

Why now: Independent consumer studies report material use of AI-assisted research, while current products expose answer and citation patterns that conventional search reporting does not fully represent.

First responsible action: Choose one discovery-dependent segment and interview five marketing buyers before building a generalized GEO service.

## Point of view

Common coverage mistake: Treating AI visibility as a new ranking system and selling control the operator does not possess.

Operator Economy view: Sell diagnosis, source judgment, prioritization, and honest repeated measurement. Do not sell guaranteed placement or causal revenue claims.

What would change the recommendation: If buyers cannot name a decision the evidence would change, measurements cannot be repeated responsibly, or a realistic price cannot cover expert delivery and acquisition, the standalone service should be folded into existing SEO work or rejected.

## Scope

Must answer: buyer, problem, offer, workflow, measurement boundary, human judgment, stack by capability, first-buyer path, modeled economics, risks, and 30-day test.

Out of scope: final platform tactics, prompt-manipulation recipes, a directory of GEO tools, guaranteed optimization methods, final title and thumbnail, and production visuals.

## Truth contract

Observed facts and neighboring models may establish relevance and feasibility. The episode must repeatedly make clear that the combined service and every commercially important result remain unobserved.
