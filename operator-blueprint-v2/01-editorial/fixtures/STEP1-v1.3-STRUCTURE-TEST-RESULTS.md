# Step 1 v1.3 structure-and-voice regression results

Status: two-fixture regression complete; pass recommendation; owner review pending; v1.3 not locked

Date: 2026-08-22

## Owner-directed failure

V1.2 was materially better than v1.1. It restored conviction, analogy, and a point of view. It still allowed both controls to open like confident written arguments.

The missed failures were:

- no true cold open;
- no separate show identity;
- no recurring brand and episode string;
- insufficient background for a new viewer;
- main rulings delivered before the normal system was clear;
- dense, epigrammatic sentences passing as speech; and
- cadence checks rewarding many short landings without detecting the cumulative metronome.

The owner's example, “This is not a contradiction. It is a measurement failure,” is now a negative wording control.

During the v1.3 revision, the owner also corrected the proposed brand string. The show is not primarily about overlooked problems. Its promise is helping a capable professional build, own, and operate a sustainable business of one using AI, with a small reachable share of a large costly problem rather than venture-scale market share.

## Research correction

`SCRIPT-BEAT-RESEARCH.md` reviews representative official material from Modern MBA, 60 Minutes, Nate Bargatze appearances, and Last Week Tonight.

The primary calibration becomes:

> 60 Minutes reporting bones, a plainspoken and mildly bemused human surface, Modern MBA business context, restrained Last Week Tonight reveals, and Manav's operator judgment.

In the owner's shorthand: “Nate Bargatze doing 60 Minutes.” This defines structure and temperature, never imitation.

## V1.3 rule change

1. Add a required episode beat sheet before the outline and voice map.
2. Separate the cold open, silent identity sting, fixed brand string, episode promise, context runway, central question, and earned thesis.
3. Fail any cold open that is an abstract thesis, definition, evidence dump, or agenda.
4. Require the normal system, change, affected person, and stakes before the main ruling.
5. Add a first-listen load map and a written-not-spoken challenge.
6. Add a countercheck for short-sentence and mic-drop metronome.
7. Make humor follow comprehension and default to mild bemusement.
8. Require the ending to resolve or reinterpret the opening.
9. Replace the personal-register percentage stack with the Operator Economy documentary register and high-level reference functions.
10. Correct the fixed show identity to the business-of-one promise.
11. Require every episode to explain what AI enables, what judgment one operator retains, what sustainability would require, and why a small reachable share of a large costly problem could be enough.

## What the test proves

- The v1.3 controls reject both v0.4 openings despite their accurate claims and stronger conviction.
- A real cold open can add context without inventing a documented case.
- The show identity, episode promise, and context runway perform different jobs.
- The thesis becomes clearer when it arrives later.
- The business-of-one promise can be explicit without turning the scenario into an income forecast.
- AI's role can be named without letting tools inherit human judgment or accountability.
- A reachable-share argument can remain conditional on actual buyer and delivery tests.
- Both v0.5 recoveries preserve the approved claims and modeled economics.
- The ending can close the opening case while still giving the Operator Blueprint and practical first action.

## What the test does not prove

- It does not prove either business will support an operator.
- It does not prove demand, pricing, acquisition, or market share.
- It does not approve the fixed brand string as final brand canon.
- It does not prove the owner's authentic voice until he reads and approves the exact scripts.
- It does not authorize an episode, narration, visual translation, production, or publication.
- It does not make v1.3 canonical.
