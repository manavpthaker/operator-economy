# Step 1 inverse edge test: story cannot substitute for a business

Result: `E3_FAIL_THEN_FIXTURE_PASS_WITH_STORY_QUARANTINED`

The premature story was compelling: one client handoff exposed a larger opportunity to build an AI-enabled operations-reliability company. The first Canvas could not explain who paid first, what they bought, how delivery remained safe, how the buyer accepted the result, or whether the revenue survived labor and support.

Gate E3 stopped the workflow. Gate E4 was not reached.

The repair preserved the ambition while defining one buyer, one paid diagnostic, a separate build decision, safe delivery and ownership, an acceptance measure, a bounded first-customer test, modeled economics with capacity, and evidence required before expansion.

The repaired Canvas passed E3 in fixture mode. The early story remains quarantined exploration and must still be reconciled before any future E4 review.

Episode number, approved narrative, script, narration, or production authority created: no.
