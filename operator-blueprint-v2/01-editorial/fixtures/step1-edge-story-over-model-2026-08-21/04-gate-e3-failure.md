# Gate E3 decision: incomplete AI operating-layer Canvas

Status: completed negative-path decision

Canvas under review: `03-incomplete-canvas.md`

Premature story: `02-premature-story.md`

Decision: fail Gate E3; return to Canvas; Gate E4 not reached

## Why it fails

| E3 requirement | Result | Blocking reason |
|---|---|---|
| Identifiable first buyer | fail | “Small and midsize businesses” spans incompatible buyers, triggers, risks, and procurement paths. |
| Costly first problem | fail | No workflow, frequency, consequence, baseline, or current alternative is selected. |
| Bounded first offer | fail | Diagnostic, implementation, subscription, monitoring, governance, and software are one undefined package. |
| Measurable buyer result | fail | “Faster” and “fewer errors” have no primary measure, baseline, acceptance event, or influence boundary. |
| Deliverable operating system | fail | The delivery plan omits process observation, build/no-build criteria, testing, human judgment, recovery, documentation, and handoff. |
| Safe access and ownership | fail | Operator-owned infrastructure and an AI decision-maker are proposed without least privilege, client ownership, revocation, or human review. |
| Credible first-customer path | fail | Five generic channels replace a reachable segment, prospect list, paid test, and rejection condition. |
| Reconciled economics | fail | Revenue is calculated without labor, support, infrastructure, security, acquisition, refunds, incident cost, or capacity. |
| Conditional expansion | fail | Service work is treated as proof of recurring demand and a platform without intermediate buyer evidence. |
| Safe 30-day test | fail | Building a demo and website precedes buyer, workflow, measure, and permission decisions. |

## Story-appeal check

The story has a strong human opening, recurring transaction, conceptual turn, causal expansion idea, and aspirational ending. Those are E4 strengths.

They have no authority to resolve the E3 failures above.

The story is preserved as exploratory material because it may help after the model is repaired. It is quarantined from approval: it may not define claims, fill Canvas fields, authorize an outline, or justify scriptwriting.

## What is not being rejected

- The operations-reliability company remains a valid modeled aspiration.
- An AI-enabled control layer remains a possible future capability.
- The signed-client handoff remains a promising illustrative entry point.
- The story concept does not need to become less ambitious.

The failure is operational: the viewer could be inspired by the company and still be unable to explain what is sold first, how it is delivered safely, how the buyer accepts it, or whether the economics can work.

## Gate consequences

Gate E3: fail

New episode or production authority: prohibited
