# Step 1 v1.2 voice-architecture regression results

Status: required two-fixture regression complete; mechanical pass recommendation; owner requested structural revision; v1.2 not locked and superseded by v1.3

Date: 2026-08-22

## Owner-directed failure

V1.1 correctly rejected report language, but its v0.3 recovery scripts still sounded cautious. They repeatedly told the viewer what the archetypal scenario was not, repeated limitations after the audience already understood them, and delivered evidence without enough conviction, analogy, dry wit, or rhythmic personal voice.

The owner rejected v1.1. The v0.3 scripts are now negative controls.

## Rule change tested

V1.2 adds five stacked requirements:

1. A seasoned operator or career-strategist base relationship.
2. Distinct high-level functions for first-principles reasoning, observational specificity and escalation, and an opinionated spine.
3. A research-and-comedy episode engine that moves from evidence through fair explanation and absurdity to systemic reveal, verdict, and action.
4. A Manav layer built from governing analogies and rhythmic conversational cadence, not repeated catchphrases.
5. Evidence-efficient confidence: record every evidence class, disclose where a misunderstanding is likely or material, then continue without a running apology.

Named reference creators remain internal craft references. The rule prohibits copying exact wording, jokes, mannerisms, vocal patterns, or recognizable routines.

## What the test proves

- The gate now fails the v0.3 cautious-explainer pattern even though it is accurate and conversational.
- The new voice-and-comedy map forces thesis, counterargument, ruling, analogy mapping, callbacks, contempt target, act verdicts, cadence, and evidence treatment before the full script.
- Both recoveries become more confident without adding a new fact, market claim, outcome, price-typicality claim, or income promise.
- Archetypal scenes can remain truthful through metadata and fabrication prohibitions without a spoken “this is hypothetical” interruption.
- One economics boundary can protect the model without repeating modeled-scenario language after every calculation.
- Humor can clarify the operating mechanism and the bad incentive instead of becoming a separate punch-up layer.
- Opinion lines can be direct while uncertain evidence remains properly bounded.
- Step 2 remains unable to add or rewrite any thesis, joke, analogy, stance, cadence word, or conversational connector.

## What the test does not prove

- It does not prove that the owner personally hears his voice in every passage.
- It does not approve exact imitation of any named craft reference.
- It does not produce a real episode, narration, or production lock.
- It does not reopen or change the Step 0.2 evidence package.
- It does not make the v1.2 working documents canonical.
