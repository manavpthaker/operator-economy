# Gate E4 decision: broad narrative v0.1

Status: completed negative-path decision

Narrative under review: `03-narrative-v0.1-flat.md`

Decision: fail Gate E4; return to narrative; scriptwriting prohibited

## Why it fails

| E4 requirement | Result | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Protagonist with a concrete goal | fail | “An operator” and “small businesses” are categories, not a person facing a decision. |
| Recurring human situation or transaction | fail | No buyer, employee, client, or operating event survives from one sequence to the next. |
| Specific constraint | fail | Disconnected systems, administration, security, support, and commoditization are all introduced without one binding constraint. |
| Causal mechanism | fail | The offer portfolio is a menu; the viewer never sees one failure become one diagnostic, build decision, accepted result, and possible expansion. |
| Proof changes understanding | fail | Category evidence is grouped in a market section rather than used where it changes an operating decision. |
| Necessary decision or tradeoff | fail | The viewer is asked whether to build an entire company before learning which first transaction deserves intervention. |
| Narrative rather than Canvas order | fail | The ten sequences are a complete pitch-deck tour. Removing the sequence headings would not produce a story. |
| Honest relationship between entry and destination | fail | Diagnostics, projects, retainers, governance, and software appear as one simultaneous offer instead of conditional stages. |
| Responsible resolution | fail | The company roadmap is presented before the first buyer proof has been earned. |

## What is not being rejected

- The broader operations-reliability ambition is not too large.
- Diagnostics, implementation, managed reliability, governance, and productization may all remain in the modeled business.
- The first workflow does not have to become the permanent service boundary.
- The episode may end with the larger company vision.

The failure is the route through the material: the draft treats the complete business as ten equally weighted subjects instead of showing how the first proof could earn the next scope.

## Bounded revision request

Preserve the scope model and its aspirational destination. Revise only the narrative:

1. Begin with one human consequence inside the signed-proposal-to-kickoff handoff.
2. Make the diagnostic-to-build decision the central turn.
3. Show the first accepted result and the evidence it creates.
4. Introduce each broader scope only as a conditional next step.
5. Use the final sequence to reveal the operations-reliability company the entry wedge could become.
