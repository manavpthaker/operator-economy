- Two fixed sprints per month.
- $3,000 per sprint.
- Twenty-four owner delivery hours per sprint.
- $60 per owner hour as a planning allowance.

Model:

```text
2 x $3,000 = $6,000 modeled gross revenue

$5,200 - (48 hours x $60) = $2,320 contribution after the owner-labor allowance
```

Capacity consequence: Six sprints require 144 delivery hours before sales, administration, monitoring, support, and recovery. The declared safe entry capacity is two sprints, or forty-eight delivery hours, with the remaining owner time reserved for those other duties.
