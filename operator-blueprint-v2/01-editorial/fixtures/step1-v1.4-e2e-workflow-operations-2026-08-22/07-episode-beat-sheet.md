Working promise: Show why the work between business tools is a company-level opportunity and how one operator begins with one paid responsibility.

Central question: What responsibility remains valuable enough to build a company around after the tools are connected?

Central thesis to earn: A Workflow Operations company owns whether important client work arrives, not merely whether an application ran.

Opportunity thesis: An observed automation-service category leaves a larger end-to-end ownership gap.

Operator thesis: Experience defines the process, exceptions, permissions, and consequence; AI and software reduce repeatable work.

Entry-wedge thesis: Paid diagnosis and one conditional sprint are the first responsibility a buyer can inspect.

Company verdict: `BUILD`

Recurring carrier: One accepted agency proposal moving toward acknowledged kickoff ownership.

## Opening ladder

### B00: cold open

Target: twenty to forty seconds

Situation: Client signs an agency proposal. Sales records it. Intake, finance, and project ownership remain uncoordinated. The client asks what happens next.

What looks normal: Every application completed its local task.

What becomes strange: The business outcome is still missing.

Question: If the software worked, what failed?

Prohibited: Real company identity, result, metric, or thesis.

### B01: silent identity sting

No narration.

### B02: brand and episode promise

Target: twenty to thirty seconds

Use the fixed brand string exactly. Name Workflow Operations and the plain definition. Do not use `Today, we're`, a chapter list, or the internal description.

### B03: context runway

Target: roughly ninety seconds

Explain: Small firms buy tools one job at a time; staff carries state between them; native connections and checklists can be enough; software and AI can now do more stable execution and documentation.

First evidence: Broad burden and adoption context, qualified once.

### B04: central question

Ask only after the normal system and direct service category are understood: What responsibility remains after the tools are connected?

### B05: earned thesis

Ruling: The missing company owns the handoff, not the application event. Name the mature Workflow Operations company.

## Ending loop

Return to the signed proposal. This time, the event produces visible state, missing information is exposed, one person acknowledges kickoff, and a failed handoff has a recovery route.

New understanding: Local software success matters only when the business responsibility arrives.

Verdict: Build the Workflow Operations company. Begin with one funded, inspectable responsibility. Count owner labor. Keep judgment human. Expand only after the first duty survives real operation.

Remaining uncertainty: Demand, price, true capacity, acquisition, repeat work, support load, and reachable share.
