# Workflow Operations opportunity-scale regression

Status: BLOCKED AT E3I

Purpose: test the proposed Step 1 v1.5 opportunity standard without repairing or rewriting the frozen Workflow Operations fixture

## Existing result preserved

The prior audited fixture remains blocked for undefined first-transaction economics, category-language upgrades, unsupported prevalence language, and word-level performance problems. This regression adds a separate upstream question: does the evidence make a persuasive opportunity case before the blueprint begins?

## Adaptive scale-pattern test

Candidate patterns considered:

1. Same job across enterprise, specialist, and solo scales.
2. Existing budget migrating into workflow reliability.
3. Large fragmented buyer pool narrowed through bottom-up capture.
4. Combination.

## Evidence present

- Zapier operates a program for hands-on automation providers.
- n8n operates a selected partner pilot for established agencies and integrators.
- A vendor-published case describes implementation, procedures, training, and support.
- Marketplace profiles show a possible route to workflow specialists.
- Current tools can execute flows and expose logs, alerts, retries, and errors.

This establishes an automation-service category, possible delivery shapes, and possible acquisition routes.

## Evidence missing

- No approved enterprise or institutional spend, revenue, or operating-responsibility evidence for the proposed Workflow Operations company.
- No approved established budget migration from automation implementation into continuing handoff responsibility.
- No approved specialist-company scale, customer count, recurring duty, or price evidence.
- No approved buyer-universe count or observed reachability evidence.
- No accepted price, delivery labor, support load, or conversion evidence for the paid diagnostic.
- The modeled 200-agency set and four-client, 2-percent share remain constructed inputs, not a market or reachability finding.

## Gate ruling

The candidate cannot use vendor programs, one vendor case, marketplace profiles, and a modeled buyer list to claim that the mature opportunity is commercially scaled. Those sources support adjacency, not the full pitch.

The adaptive standard therefore blocks rather than forcing an Accenture-style analogy.

Decision: **BLOCK AT E3I AND REQUEST A BOUNDED STEP 0 AMENDMENT.**

The amendment should seek one of these evidence paths:

- observed businesses paid to own continuing workflow reliability or managed automation operations;
- a credible established budget moving from project implementation into continuing operational responsibility; or
- a defensible buyer pool plus observed paid diagnostic behavior and bottom-up capture evidence.

If none can be established, the candidate may still support an automation implementation episode, but it cannot pitch the broader Workflow Operations company under the proposed standard.

## What this regression proves

- Opportunity scale is required.
- The evidence route is adaptive.
- A new AI business may use valid parallels.
- The process does not manufacture parallels or promote every plausible business idea.
- Strong build mechanics cannot repair a weak opportunity case downstream.

Production status: BLOCKED
