## Sequence detail

### S00: the client is waiting

Enter through an ordinary agency transaction. No statistic or company label. End on the question.

### S03: why the tools are separate

Explain that one-purpose purchases are rational. Introduce staff as the hidden handoff. Steelman a native connection, checklist, or internal owner.

### S04: problem and category

Use the institutional setting once, then direct vendor-program and marketplace evidence. Separate what exists from what remains unproven. Do not narrate a source list.

### S05: the company in the gap

Explain local task completion versus end-to-end responsibility. Introduce the relay only after the literal proposal path is visible. Name Workflow Operations and the complete mature responsibility.

### S06: opportunity-to-build turn

Ask what the tools still cannot own. Move from market evidence to operator judgment: process meaning, permission, exception rules, acceptance, recovery, and customer communication.

### S07 and S08: company before wedge

Describe the focused outsourced function first. Then explain that the buyer's first purchase is a paid diagnosis. Implementation follows only when the process deserves it. Preserve no-build as a useful buyer result.

### S09: delivery

Follow one record. Observe, map, define acceptance, build the stable path, test failures, expose state, name recovery, document, transfer, and review. AI assists with notes, tests, comparison, and runbook drafting. It does not choose safety or consequence.

### S10: first customer

Use a bounded agency set and an actual process walk-through. The partner programs show the category, not customer acquisition.

### S11: economics and required share

First expose why the entry model cannot meet its declared owner target. Then present the conditional mature scenario with four funded duties, fifty-six service hours, a 200-buyer planning set, and a 2-percent implied share. Make clear that the math passes internally while demand and reach remain unproven. Do not hide the stress case.

### S12: expansion and hard part

No monthly fee without a monthly job. Name trust, access, unstable rules, high-impact judgment, exceptions, vendor change, distribution, and support load.

### S13: construction and callback

Begin construction through a buyer-facing diagnostic artifact, bounded prospect motion, paid ask, and funded work record. Return to the proposal and land the BUILD verdict without promising success.
