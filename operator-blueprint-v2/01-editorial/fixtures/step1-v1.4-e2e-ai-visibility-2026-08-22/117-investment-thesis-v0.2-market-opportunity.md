# Episode Investment Thesis v0.2: AI Visibility

Status: approved for fixture narrative development; production blocked

## Show thesis

Experienced professionals can use AI to compress collection and coordination, then build a business of one around judgment, accountability, and a costly customer decision. The opportunity does not require a large market share. It requires a narrow buyer, a paid responsibility, repeatable delivery, and evidence that the economics survive real work.

## Company

Spoken name: AI Visibility company

Definition:

> A business that helps companies understand how they appear when customers use AI to discover, compare, shortlist, or verify providers, then helps the responsible buyer decide what deserves action.

## Qualified buyer

A strong first buyer sells a meaningful, high-consideration purchase; already owns search, content, brand, reputation, public relations, or acquisition work; can name the customer questions that matter; and has an accountable person who could act on the findings.

An unsuitable buyer relies mainly on impulse or foot traffic, cannot name a decision the evidence might change, has no accountable owner, or expects guaranteed placement, leads, or revenue.

## Opportunity thesis

AI has added another doorway to customer research. Existing teams and dashboards can do their assigned jobs and still miss how a company appears inside that doorway. A new product and service category is forming around measurement and optimization, but the commercially useful responsibility is not merely collecting answers. It is deciding what the sample means, what can be changed, what should be watched, and when the evidence is too weak to justify action.

## Scale argument

The opportunity is supported by a combination pattern:

- established search, content, brand, and acquisition responsibilities already have buyers;
- Accenture is staffing related client work;
- Semrush reports paid AI-product activity inside a much larger visibility platform;
- Profound and AirOps report category investment and organizational adoption;
- specialist agencies package and deliver related work; and
- OE proposes a narrower operator-owned decision service.

This proves category activity and adjacency. It does not prove demand for the OE diagnostic, accepted price, representative outcomes, or livelihood.

## Three explanatory cases

1. B2B software clarifies shortlist and comparison.
2. Professional services clarifies trust and qualification.
3. Travel and hospitality clarifies recommendation and comparison.

No case defines the company or market. The company must remain understandable without all three.

## Operator thesis

One experienced search, content, brand, strategy, public-relations, research, or intelligence operator may have an advantage because the hard part is asking the right questions, judging sources, understanding the buyer's decision, narrowing the conclusion, and refusing false certainty. AI can help collect and compare the material. It does not remove the operator's accountability for the sample or recommendation.

## Mature company and entry wedge

The mature company maintains a decision record for a focused portfolio and earns recurring work only when another look supports another buyer decision.

The entry wedge is one paid diagnostic for one qualified business, one market, ten buyer-approved questions, and two answer surfaces. It records what appeared, checks important claims against client-approved facts, and gives a bounded act, investigate, monitor, or leave-alone recommendation.

## Economics and capture

The working model requires 19 three-thousand-dollar diagnostics per year to clear a 48-thousand-dollar owner labor and support target before tax and unlisted costs. This is arithmetic, not a forecast.

The model does not state a required prospect pool. The first five qualified conversations measure whether a consequential decision exists and whether anyone will pay. Only observed acceptance behavior may later support a reach calculation.

## Narrative contract

Opportunity pitch target: 55 percent, allowed range 52 to 58 percent.

Operator blueprint target: 45 percent.

The detailed offer begins only after the behavior shift, economic context, comparable-business ladder, missing responsibility, company thesis, and one-operator opening are earned.

## Verdict

**BUILD.** Build the AI Visibility company as a bounded test. Start with one high-consideration market you understand and can reach, sell one paid diagnostic, record the real delivery time and buyer decision, and expand only when repeated evidence creates repeated responsibility.

BUILD does not establish demand, price, outcomes, recurring revenue, sustainable economics, or income.
