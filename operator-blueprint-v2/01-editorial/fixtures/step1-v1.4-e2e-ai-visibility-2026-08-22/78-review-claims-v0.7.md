- `changed from one domain to another` preserves material domain variation.
- `can't predict what will happen for a client` preserves the no-client-result limit.
- The wording makes no current-engine guarantee and does not infer a commercial workflow, organic discoverability, traffic, buyer value, demand, or client outcome.
