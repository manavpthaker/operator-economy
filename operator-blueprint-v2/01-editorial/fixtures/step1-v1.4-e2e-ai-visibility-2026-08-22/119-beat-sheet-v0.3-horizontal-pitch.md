# Beat sheet v0.3: AI Visibility horizontal opportunity

Status: approved for fixture outline

## Fixed opening

### S00

Start with a customer asking AI which company to trust, shortlist, or choose. The company is missing or described through sources it does not control. Tease the paid responsibility: one operator can map the answer, check the evidence, and help the buyer decide what to do. Withhold the market proof, delivery model, economics, and BUILD verdict.

### S01

Silent identity sting, 3 to 6 seconds.

### S02

Use the exact brand string. Name the AI Visibility company and promise to establish who needs it, whether the market is real enough, what one operator owns, and what the first paid version must prove.

## Opportunity pitch

### S03: the second doorway

Explain the familiar search trail and the AI-assisted research shift. Preserve the survey boundary and source variability. End on economic consequence, not a category definition.

### S04: three buying decisions

Use B2B software, professional services, and travel as brief archetypes. Explain shortlist, qualification, and recommendation. Then define the qualified buyer and unsuitable buyer without any industry name.

### S05: follow the money and responsibility

Move from established search and brand work to Accenture staffing, Semrush's emerging AI portfolio, specialist-platform investment, and agency delivery. Keep every boundary audible. The conclusion is category activity, not exact-offer demand.

### S06: measurement is not the company

Explain what tools collect and what a responsible operator still decides. Name the mature AI Visibility company only after the gap is clear.

### S07: why one operator

Show how AI compresses collection and comparison while the operator owns questions, source judgment, client facts, exceptions, and recommendations. Teach the viewer to begin from experience and access.

## Operator blueprint

### S08: first paid diagnostic

One qualified business, one market, ten approved questions, two answer surfaces. Deliver a decision record, not a placement promise. Use five qualified conversations as validation, not expected conversion.

### S09: economics without a false market share

Retain the two-thousand-dollar validation case, three-thousand-dollar working case, 19 annual diagnostics, and 960-hour capacity model. Remove 250 travel brands and 7.6 percent. Explain that observed acceptance must precede any reach calculation.

### S10: failure modes

Buyer sees ordinary SEO, sample instability, tools absorb the decision, scope expands, delivery exceeds capacity, or buyer demands guaranteed outcomes.

### S11: construction

First 30 days create the template, preview, record, scope, and five qualified conversations. Continue only when the existing three-part gate passes. Next 60 days are funded work only.

### S12: verdict

Return to the second doorway. Land BUILD as bounded construction, tell the viewer to choose one qualified market they understand and can reach, sell one diagnostic, measure the work, then earn expansion. End with one like-and-subscribe sentence.
