# Narrative spine v0.3: AI Visibility horizontal opportunity

Status: approved for fixture beats

## Story in one sentence

A buyer asks AI whom to trust, a company discovers that its familiar reports are watching a different doorway, and an experienced operator follows the responsibility from enterprise consultancies and platforms down to one paid decision diagnostic.

## Viewer movement

Starting belief: AI Visibility is another dashboard, SEO tactic, or travel-specific example.

Ending understanding: it is a horizontal decision service for qualified high-consideration businesses, supported by category activity but still requiring paid validation at operator scale.

## Governing analogy

The customer journey has gained another doorway. Existing reports can remain accurate while watching the old entrance. The proposed company does not promise to control who walks through the new doorway. It helps the buyer see what happened there and decide what deserves action.

## Opportunity act

1. Show the broader customer decision before naming an industry.
2. Explain the second doorway.
3. Use three mini-cases to show shortlist, qualification, and recommendation.
4. Follow the responsibility across existing budgets and four operating scales.
5. Show what measurement still leaves to a person.
6. Name the AI Visibility company and why one operator may be able to own it.

## Build act

1. Choose a qualified market through experience and access.
2. Sell one bounded diagnostic.
3. Model price, labor, capacity, and annual requirements.
4. Refuse false outcome promises and uncontrolled scope.
5. Use the first five conversations and first paid work to replace assumptions.
6. Return to the second doorway and land BUILD.
