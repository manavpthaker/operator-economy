## Opportunity act

S00 through S05 establish the opportunity. The episode must not reduce the company to the diagnostic before S05 names the mature responsibility.

Evidence rhythm in S03 and S04:

1. State the ordinary system.
2. Give one conversational finding.
3. Let the evidence receipt carry precision.
4. Explain the business consequence in ordinary language.
5. Move to the next source only when it answers a new question.

Do not stack year, organization, report title, sample, number, interpretation, analogy, and joke into one spoken paragraph.

Act I turn: tools can collect the sample, but someone still has to decide whether the sample deserves action. That responsibility is the company.

## Build act

S06 through S12 establish that the viewer can construct and test the company.

Operator-to-market logic:

```text
earned experience or access
-> understood buyer decision
-> narrow reachable industry
-> credibility screen
-> buyer conversation
-> paid diagnostic ask
```

Travel fills the `narrow reachable industry` slot for this illustration and arithmetic. The narration must explicitly invite the viewer to replace it with a field they understand and can reach.

Company-to-wedge transition: the company owns the continuing decision function, but the first buyer purchases one bounded diagnostic they can inspect.

Why the first month is construction: it creates buyer-facing assets, reaches prospects, runs a delivery capability, and makes a paid ask.

## Spoken-transition controls

- A new scene may begin with `So`, `But`, `And`, or another familiar connector only when the sentence names the causal inheritance from the preceding scene.
- `Okay`, `right`, `here's the thing`, and similar personal markers are never section labels or quotas.
- Permit at most one purposeful `Okay` in the full script. Zero is acceptable.
- Do not announce chapters with `Now let's move to`, `Next`, or `The question is` when the preceding consequence can pull the listener forward.
- The final two lines of each scene and first two of the next must pass the seam test in `38-narrative-spine-v0.2.md`.
