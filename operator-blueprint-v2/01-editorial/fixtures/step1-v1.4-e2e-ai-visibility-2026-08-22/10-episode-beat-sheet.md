Working promise: show why an AI visibility company may deserve to exist, what one operator owns, and how to begin building it without promising control over an AI answer.

Central question: can one experienced operator turn the decision gap into a useful company when placement, demand, and impact remain outside their control?

Central thesis: the company does not control AI answers. It owns the buyer's decision about what those answers deserve.

Company verdict: BUILD, bounded to construction and paid proof.

Recurring thread: archetypal travel-marketing lead, green search report, and unmeasured AI-answer doorway.

Spoken name: AI visibility company

Plain definition: Helps brands understand how they appear in AI answers and decide what deserves action.
