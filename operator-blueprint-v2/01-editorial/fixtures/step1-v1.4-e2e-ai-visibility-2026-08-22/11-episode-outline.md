## Sequence detail

### S00: the report is green

Viewer enters through one marketing lead. Search traffic is steady, then the buyer sees an AI answer that omits the company or relies on stale information. The report remains green. The narrator asks which view is missing and withholds the thesis.

### S01: show and company promise

After the silent identity break, use the exact brand string. Name the AI visibility company and explain its job in one plain sentence. Promise an ownership and first-proof decision, not a chapter list.

### S02: the normal doorway

Explain query, page, click, analytics. Defend its usefulness. A green report can be right about the doorway it measures.

### S03: the added doorway

Attribute McKinsey's August 2025 survey and sample. State that this does not mean everybody abandoned conventional search. Explain source variation. Use the doorway analogy only after the literal system is clear. Ask who owns the resulting decision.

### S04: components are not the company

Show what vendors can collect, what a benchmark can measure, and what neighboring SEO services establish. State each transfer limit once. Land the ruling that collection capability and a familiar audit form do not prove this buyer or service.

### S05: name the complete company

Identify the missing responsibility: question design, evidence record, fact and source review, controllability, and buyer recommendation. Name the AI visibility company. Explain the mature portfolio function before any diagnostic scope.

### S06: why this operator

Ask what software cannot decide. Answer with buyer context, source trust, factual authority, refusal, and action. Connect the advantage to experienced search, content, or intelligence judgment.

### S07: mature operating model

Walk through the question-to-decision loop. Divide work among AI, software, buyer, operator, and specialist. Explain that one owner stays accountable and that the model breaks when every engagement needs a hidden team.

### S08: first paid wedge and delivery

Bring the company down to one brand, one market, ten questions, and two surfaces. Define the evidence registry, classifications, buyer acceptance, completion, and valid no-action result. Make it clear that no-action can prove the company sells judgment rather than optimization.

### S09: first customer

Choose the modeled consumer-travel test bench. Build the bounded prospect list and three-question public screen. Ask what decision the evidence changes. Offer the complete diagnostic only after the buyer answers.

### S10: make the model carry its own weight

Introduce the fixture model once. Explain the validation case and working case without calling either typical or viable. State that the current arithmetic covers a delivery-labor allowance but not a livelihood. Give the required-share formula and withhold any customer-count claim until paid evidence exists.

### S11: what breaks

Cover buyer substitution, unstable samples, outcome pressure, specialist dependency, and capacity drift. Tie every risk to a refusal, narrowing, or stop decision. State that monitoring is not earned by the existence of another month.

### S12: construct and test

First 30 days build the evidence registry, question template, delivery boundary, screen, and prospect set. Conduct conversations, run one consenting baseline, track the work, and make a paid ask. Days 31 through 90 deliver funded work, replace assumptions, and repeat only for another decision.

### S13: green report callback

Return to the original buyer. The report can remain useful while another doorway remains unmeasured. Land BUILD as construction, not proof. Start with one paid diagnostic and earn every larger responsibility.

First purchase: paid fixed-scope diagnostic.

Client-level no-action result: preserved.

Expansion: conditional on buyer use, repeatability, support, capacity, and economics evidence.

First construction step: build the buyer-facing evidence and delivery assets, reach the bounded prospect set, and make the paid ask.

Exact final verdict direction: Build the AI visibility company. Start with the diagnostic. Earn everything after it.
