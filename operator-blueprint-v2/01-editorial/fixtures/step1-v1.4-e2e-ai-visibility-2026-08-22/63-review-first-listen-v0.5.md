## Targeted rewrite retest

The listener hears one complete thought:

> McKinsey found that roughly half the people it surveyed were deliberately using AI-assisted search. Regular search didn't disappear. For those customers, AI had become another doorway into the same research.

`The people it surveyed` carries the evidence boundary. `For those customers` keeps the next statement tied to that group without repeating survey language. The passage returns immediately to the governing analogy. Result: **pass**.

### S04 C006 and company turn

### S07 completion and no-action result

The listener can now repeat the delivery contract:

- Each important gap receives the evidence record plus one recommendation.
- The four possible recommendations are act, investigate, monitor, or leave it alone.
- The job ends when the record is clear enough for the buyer to use.
- `Leave it alone` must be a genuine answer rather than an automatic upsell.

The clipboard joke arrives only after this distinction is understood. Result: **pass**.

### S11 three-part continuation gate

The sequence is now easy to reconstruct:

1. Use the preview to earn the conversation.
2. Ask for payment before delivering the full diagnostic.
3. If the buyer does not accept, the alternate commercial signal requires a named budget and a stated approval condition.
4. Continue only when all three gate conditions hold:
   - At least three of five buyers independently call the problem consequential.
   - At least one pays or names the budget and approval condition.
   - Manual delivery produces repeatable evidence within 24 hours of work.

The three conditions separately test problem importance, commercial seriousness, and delivery repeatability. Result: **pass**.
