# Voice map v0.4: Manav-hosted AI Visibility

Status: approved for fixture script

## Original hosted register

The host is a warm strategist making an investment case with the viewer. Investigative wit supplies movement and tension. Dry skepticism targets dashboards, category hype, fake certainty, and activity without responsibility. The viewer, buyer, worker, and operator remain protected.

Named creators and programs are prohibited from production instructions. No ratio, impression, catchphrase, or borrowed mannerism applies.

## Cadence and identity checks

- Vary sentence and paragraph length.
- Use first-person judgment only where the host is genuinely advising.
- Let reactions emerge from episode facts rather than a joke quota.
- Do not open more than one scene with the same discourse marker.
- Avoid polished negate-and-replace chains.
- The market case must feel like a pitch, not a report bibliography.
- The company must remain understandable after removing B2B software, professional services, and travel.
