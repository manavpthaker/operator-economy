## Three-change review

### 1. C006 ordinary wording

Map 50 requires all three meanings to remain audible:

1. The evidence came from a benchmark measuring the surface.
2. Results varied by domain.
3. The benchmark does not predict a client's result.

v0.6 says:

> Researchers have shown that AI visibility can be measured. The results changed from one domain to another. So you can study the pattern. You still can't predict what will happen for a client.

Finding: **REVISE**. The second and third meanings pass. The first does not. `Researchers have shown` generalizes the source class and removes `benchmark`, even though map 50 says to keep benchmark scope audible. The screen receipt cannot repair an audio-scope omission.

The minimal claim-safe repair is to restore the source class in ordinary language without returning to the polished antithesis. For example:

> Researchers measured AI visibility in a benchmark. The results changed from one domain to another. So you can study the pattern. You still can't predict what will happen for a client.

No source title, date, sample, method recital, or locator needs to enter narration.

### 2. No-action sentence deletion

v0.6 retains:

> Leave it alone has to be a real recommendation.

It also retains the following systemic explanation:

> If every report recommends more work from its author, that isn't a decision service. It's a sales pitch with a clipboard.

Finding: **PASS**. Deleting `Otherwise the buyer paid for a disguised sale, not judgment` removes duplicate wording without removing the actual no-action recommendation, the buyer-decision standard, or the incentive-conflict warning. C007 and the proposed service boundary remain intact.

### 3. Spoken 24-diagnostic and 576-hour removal

The deleted sentence did not control the governing annual decision, which remains 19 diagnostics. v0.6 still says:

- 19 paid diagnostics are required by the model.
- 19 diagnostics at 24 hours require 456 delivery hours.
- The 960-hour model therefore leaves 504 hours for all non-delivery work.
- The calendar works only if everything else fits inside those 504 hours, which remains unknown.

The exact unchanged receipt still says:

> MODELED CAPACITY · 20 hours × 48 weeks = 960 owner hours · 19 × 24 hours = 456 delivery hours · 24-diagnostic ceiling × 24 hours = 576 delivery hours · 960 - 456 = 504 hours for all non-delivery work · E008

Finding: **PASS**. Map 50 requires the displayed ceiling to remain modeled and qualified; it does not require every displayed model calculation to be read aloud. The scene's audible model qualifier precedes the capacity block, the receipt retains `MODELED`, and no buyer outcome or business conclusion depends on presenting 24 or 576 in voiceover.
