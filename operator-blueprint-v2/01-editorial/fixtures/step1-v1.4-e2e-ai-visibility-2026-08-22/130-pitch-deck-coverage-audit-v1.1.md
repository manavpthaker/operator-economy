## Verdict

The v1.1 episode and refreshed Canvas answer the complete operator pitch without forcing the narration to sound like a slide presentation.

- VO owns the story, causal argument, company, first offer, economics boundary, risks, and first action.
- Visuals own spatial explanation, evidence receipts, comparison detail, secondary arithmetic, and persistent business-model orientation.
- The downloadable Canvas owns the complete operating specification, formulas, exclusions, unknowns, and conditional expansion rules.

No pitch point is delegated only to visuals. No visual is authorized to turn adjacent evidence into exact-offer demand, hide an unknown, or present the mature company as if every capability is sold on day one.
