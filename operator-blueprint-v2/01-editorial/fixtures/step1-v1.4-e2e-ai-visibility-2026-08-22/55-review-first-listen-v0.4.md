## Targeted first-listen retest

### S04 to S05: one reveal, two jobs

After S04, the listener can say: the tools collect evidence, but a person must decide what it deserves.

After S05, the listener can say: the company repeatedly records the agreed sample, checks facts and sources, and gives the buyer a recommendation.

Those are consecutive steps rather than duplicate definitions. The phrase `That responsibility` provides a literal handoff. Result: **pass**.

### S09: number hierarchy

The scene now supplies an audible map before the arithmetic:

| Case | Figures a listener is asked to retain | Immediate meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Validation | Two grand, 16 hours, and 641 dollars left before tax and unlisted costs | If the job does not fit, scope or price must change. |
| Working | Three grand, 24 hours, and 1,161 dollars left before tax and unlisted costs | This is the case used for the annual decision, and it fails if price or delivery time is wrong. |
| Annual | 48,000-dollar owner labor-and-support target, 2,601 dollars per job, and 19 diagnostics | Nineteen is the modeled annual sales requirement, not a forecast. |
| Capacity | 456 delivery hours inside 960 annual hours, leaving 504; ceiling of 24 jobs and 576 delivery hours | The calendar fits only if every non-delivery obligation fits in the remainder. |
| Reach | 19 different buyers from an example list of 250, or 7.6 percent | This is a sales hurdle for the example list, not market size or a conversion forecast. |

The exact amounts remain demanding, but they are no longer presented as parallel cases of equal importance. Plain interpretation follows each decision layer, and the two-project aggregate has moved out of the spoken words. A listener may not remember every cost component, but can retain which case governs the annual requirement and what must be tested. Result: **pass with watch for the owner cold read**, not a word-level blocker in this lane.

### S11: transaction chronology

The spoken order is now explicit:

1. Build the three-question preview and prospect list.
2. Use the preview to earn a conversation.
3. Ask the buyer to pay for the full diagnostic.
4. Deliver the full diagnostic only after the ask.
5. Evaluate paid acceptance or a specific budget-backed condition together with the three-of-five problem signal and repeatable manual evidence within 24 hours.
6. Move into funded work and record what actually happened.

The listener no longer has to infer whether a full diagnostic was given away and then sold retroactively. Result: **pass**.

### S12: resolution

The ending answers the opening in concrete terms. The report can remain green because it was watching one doorway. The diagnostic is named as the first sale, while the company is named as the repeatable decision work. The exact BUILD line then gives one bounded action. Result: **pass**.
