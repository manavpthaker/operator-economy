## Required semantic checks

### C006 conversational benchmark language

Approved map meaning:

- AI visibility was measured in a benchmark.
- Effects varied by domain.
- The benchmark does not predict a client's result.

v0.5 wording:

> Researchers have shown that AI visibility can be measured. But their benchmark produced different results in different domains. Useful, yes. A prediction of what will happen for a client, no.

Finding: **PASS**. `Can be measured` remains tied to `their benchmark`; `different results in different domains` preserves domain variation; the final sentence explicitly blocks client prediction. The passage does not claim a current-engine guarantee, commercial workflow, organic-discoverability result, traffic result, or client outcome.

### E006 budget and approval-condition paraphrase

Approved map meaning:

- At least one buyer accepts a paid diagnostic, or
- At least one gives a specific budget-backed purchase condition.

v0.5 wording:

- `one buyer names a budget and tells you what would have to be true for approval`
- `at least one accepts a paid diagnostic, or names the budget and what would have to be true for approval`

Finding: **PASS**. A named budget preserves the specific budget component; what must be true for approval preserves the purchase-condition component. Both elements remain conjunctive, and the script immediately states that this is not paid demand. Mere interest, a vague budget reference, or an approval condition without a named budget would not satisfy this map, but the frozen wording does.

### Two-answer-surfaces repair

The narration now says `two AI answer surfaces` in S07 and S11. The model receipts and E006 registry say `two answer surfaces`.

Finding: **PASS**. `AI` describes the surfaces without changing the sampling unit. The former `two AI answer tools` mismatch is gone.
