## C006 internal-turn regression

The only v0.7 spoken change is inside S04:

> One research benchmark showed that AI visibility can be measured. The results changed from one domain to another. So you can study the pattern. You still can't predict what will happen for a client.

The story function remains intact and is now more precisely bounded:

1. `One research benchmark` identifies a single piece of evidence rather than suggesting a broad research consensus.
2. `The results changed from one domain to another` prevents the measurement from becoming a universal result.
3. `So you can study the pattern` establishes the limited capability needed for the proposed service.
4. `You still can't predict what will happen for a client` prevents the capability from becoming a client-outcome promise.
5. `So yes, you can collect a sample` advances from bounded measurement to collection.
6. `A new measurement doesn't appoint anyone to decide what it means` advances from collection to the unowned buyer decision.

The added source noun does not create a detour, chapter reset, or second company definition. S04 still ends at the responsibility the company would own, and S05 still defines the full company once. Decision: **PASS**.
