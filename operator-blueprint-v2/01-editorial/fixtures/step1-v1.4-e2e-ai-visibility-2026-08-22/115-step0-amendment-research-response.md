## Research finding

The evidence supports a broad category opening, not a measured market for the exact Operator Economy offer.

AI-assisted discovery is creating a new visibility and interpretation responsibility beside established search, content, brand, public-relations, reputation, and customer-acquisition work. Enterprise consultancies are staffing the responsibility. Public software companies and funded platforms are building products around it. Specialist agencies are packaging and delivering related services.

That is enough to support an editorial recommendation to test a bounded operator service. It does not prove demand for the proposed diagnostic, its price, its delivery time, recurring purchase, representative outcomes, or sustainable livelihood.

### M007: proposed operator synthesis

## Qualified buyer rule

The strongest initial buyer is a business where:

- customers research and compare before a meaningful purchase;
- omission, misclassification, or inaccurate representation could affect a shortlist or trust decision;
- the business already assigns responsibility for search, content, brand, public relations, reputation, or customer acquisition;
- one customer or contract is valuable enough for the buyer to investigate the issue; and
- the operator can identify specific questions customers ask before buying.

The weakest initial buyer relies mainly on impulse purchases or foot traffic, has no accountable owner for discoverability, cannot name a customer decision that might change, or expects guaranteed placement, leads, or revenue.

## Three explanatory cases

| Market | Customer decision | Illustration | Transferable meaning | Non-transferable meaning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| B2B software | Shortlist and comparison | A buyer asks AI to compare platforms and receives outdated positioning or omits a provider. | AI answers can influence which vendors enter consideration. | No claim about prevalence, lost pipeline, or B2B demand for the OE offer. |
| Professional services | Trust and qualification | A prospective client asks AI to compare providers and receives incomplete expertise or location information. | Accuracy and third-party sources can matter during qualification. | No claim about legal, financial, medical, or other regulated advice; no outcome claim. |
| Travel and hospitality | Recommendation and comparison | A traveler asks where to stay and receives an omission or inaccurate description. | AI can become another discovery doorway. | Travel is not the prescribed market or economic denominator. |

All three are archetypal illustrations unless a later Step 0 artifact approves a documented case.

## Scale-proof decision

Pattern: combination of budget migration and the same responsibility appearing across enterprise, platform, and agency layers.

Observed operating layers: enterprise consultancy, public platform, emerging specialist platforms, specialist agencies.

Decision: PASS for a fixture opportunity argument with explicit boundaries.

## Bottom-up boundary

The model may state annual engagements, delivery hours, owner-time allowance, listed costs, and validation conversations. It may not state a required reachable prospect pool, conversion rate, or market share until the test observes acceptance behavior.

The preserved 250-travel-brand denominator and 7.6 percent share are retired from the revised fixture. They remain historical evidence in the frozen v1.0 package.
