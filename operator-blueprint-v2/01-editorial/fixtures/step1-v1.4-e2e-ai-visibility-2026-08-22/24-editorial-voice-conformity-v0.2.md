## Challenges

### Generic-language challenge

The line `That judgment is the advantage` could appear in another professional-services explainer by itself. It survives because the preceding sentences specify the actual judgment: buyer question, client factual authority, and evidence threshold. Removing that context would fail the challenge.

### Written-not-spoken challenge

The most formal surviving block is the annual economics sequence. It now uses explicit listener resets: `Now ask the annual question`, `Next, ask whether one operator can deliver them`, and `Now ask whether the buyers are reachable`. No sentence prompted an instinctive word-changing rewrite in the fixture rerun. The owner must still perform the final read aloud.

### Weakest stance challenge

Weakest tempting stance: `This is the company we would test.` It would be too cautious as the ending. The final script instead says BUILD under C010, then immediately names what the first paid diagnostic must prove.

### Removable-humor challenge

The slide/client-work joke could be removed without losing arithmetic, but it earns its place by marking the switch from cooperative modeled revenue to scarce owner capacity. The clipboard and one-invoice lines are more essential because each decodes a failure mechanism.

### Verdict-before-case challenge

The business verdict does not appear in the cold open. The viewer hears the normal system, changed discovery path, evidence limits, missing owner, mature company, and operator advantage before the build act and final BUILD instruction.

### Cadence diagnostic

The narration contains 204 sentences, 16 sentences of four words or fewer, three sentences of 30 words or more, and no cluster of three consecutive sentences with six words or fewer. A feed-style sentence-length coefficient-of-variation diagnostic is approximately 0.50, below the legacy feed heuristic. Step 1 v1.4 treats cadence metrics as smoke alarms rather than approval. No official long-form VO cadence tool is wired into this fixture, so the metric cannot be called an automated pass. The varied transitions, long explanatory stretches, short rulings, and exact clean-read result support an owner read-through rather than a lock.
