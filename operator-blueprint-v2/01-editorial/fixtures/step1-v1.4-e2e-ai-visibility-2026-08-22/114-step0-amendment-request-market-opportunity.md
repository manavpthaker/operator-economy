# Bounded Step 0 amendment request: AI Visibility opportunity scale

Status: submitted for fixture research response

Episode: unassigned fixture

Candidate: AI Visibility company

## Why the amendment is required

The preserved v1.0 script explains the problem and the first diagnostic, but it does not establish a persuasive opportunity case. Travel carries the cold open, the example prospect universe, and the reach calculation. That makes a horizontal company sound like a hotel-specific service.

Step 1 cannot repair this with stronger prose. It needs approved evidence for the category context, comparable operating businesses, qualified buyers, and the boundary between observed scale and the proposed business-of-one model.

## Narrow research questions

1. Is AI visibility already a recognized operating responsibility across enterprise consultancies, software platforms, and specialist agencies?
2. Which existing budgets or functions are adjacent to the work?
3. Which businesses are most likely to care because customers use AI to discover, compare, shortlist, or verify a meaningful purchase?
4. What can these parallels establish about an operator opportunity, and what remains unproven?

## Required evidence layers

- Customer behavior and discovery shift.
- Established search, content, brand, public-relations, reputation, or customer-acquisition responsibility.
- Enterprise or institutional operation.
- Platform or product-category operation.
- Specialist or agency operation.
- Reachable operator synthesis.

## Proposed explanatory cases

- B2B software: shortlist and comparison.
- Professional services: trust and qualification.
- Travel and hospitality: recommendation and comparison.

The cases may clarify different customer decisions. They may not define the company, total market, prospect denominator, economics, demand, price, or outcome.

## Prohibited upgrades

- Funding is not customer demand.
- Valuation is not market size.
- Total company revenue is not AI Visibility revenue.
- A job posting proves an operating capability is being staffed, not client revenue or project volume.
- A vendor case establishes only the reported case and cannot establish representative outcomes.
- No conversion rate, reachable-market share, or required prospect pool may be stated before observed validation.

## Allowed outcomes

- Approve a bounded scale argument with explicit limits.
- Approve narrower qualified-buyer language.
- Block the opportunity case if fewer than two observed operating layers are supported.
- Preserve all exact-offer demand, price, repeat-purchase, outcome, and livelihood questions as unknown.
