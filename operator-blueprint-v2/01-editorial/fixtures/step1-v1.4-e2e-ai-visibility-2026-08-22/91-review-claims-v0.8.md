## S12 payoff, BUILD, commercial, and CTA review

### Opening resolution

`The search report can stay green. It wasn't wrong. It was watching one doorway while the customer used another.` resolves the illustrative opening. Within that story, the report tracks the regular-search doorway described in S03. It does not claim that every real report is accurate or that every customer uses both pathways.

`This company isn't another dashboard` is a positioning distinction for the proposed company. `A clear call on what deserves action and what doesn't` is the designed service deliverable: a recommendation. It is not a claim that the recommendation will improve placement, traffic, leads, revenue, or any other client outcome.

### BUILD and first action

`Start with one paid diagnostic in a market you understand and can reach` preserves C010 as a bounded construction recommendation and preserves the experience-and-access-led market-selection rule. It does not prescribe travel as the viewer's market or claim that the first buyer will purchase.

`Prove the work helps the buyer make a real decision` is a validation condition. It requires evidence before expansion. It does not state that the work has already helped a buyer or that a buyer result is assured.

`Then earn every larger responsibility after it` makes expansion conditional on proved usefulness and paid work. Monitoring, implementation, and a larger scope remain unearned until the prior responsibility works.

### Commercial and livelihood boundary

`One costly problem`, `buyers willing to pay`, and `enough repeatable work` are prerequisites connected by `need`. They are not presented as observed conditions in this fixture.

`Build a sustainable business of one` is therefore conditional and aspirational. It does not convert the $48,000 fixture planning target into expected income, promise a livelihood, or erase the tax, unlisted-cost, demand, capacity, support, and reach uncertainties made audible in S09.

### Final call to action

`If you want another business worth testing` describes the benefit of future channel content. `Like the video and subscribe` is one combined, natural audience request. It introduces no next-episode claim, fabricated result, factual assertion, commercial guarantee, or additional viewer obligation.
