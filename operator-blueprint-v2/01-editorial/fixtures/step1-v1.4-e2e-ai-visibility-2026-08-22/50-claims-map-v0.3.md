## Evidence-presentation contract

Claim eligibility and citation delivery are separate decisions. The narrator carries the finding and any limit that changes its meaning. The screen carries exact provenance. The show notes carry the independently reviewable record.

| Layer | Job | Required treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Voiceover | Keep the argument understandable and honest without a picture | Use ordinary language, a conversational source or population reference when scope requires it, and the material limit once. Do not read report titles, dates, sample sizes, methods, or locators unless one changes the conclusion. |
| On-screen evidence receipt | Carry precise provenance at the moment the claim appears | Use the exact approved copy in this map. Include source, artifact, date, population or sample and figure when applicable, locator, and claim ID. |
| Description or show notes | Preserve the complete citation | Use the approved citation copy in this map, including direct link, publication and access details, locator, methodology note, known limitation, and Step 0 provenance. |
| Claims registry | Control what may be inferred | Preserve evidence class, transfer limit, confidence, and prohibited inference even when those details are not all spoken. |

An on-screen receipt or show-note link cannot repair misleading audio. Money, forecasts, constructed cases, and outcome claims require an audible plain-English qualifier. Every displayed model number must also carry `MODELED` and its applicable exclusion on the same screen.

Allowed spoken wording:

> Continue only if at least three of the five buyers independently describe the problem as consequential, at least one accepts a paid diagnostic or gives a specific budget-backed purchase condition, and the manual delivery produces repeatable evidence within 24 hours of work.
