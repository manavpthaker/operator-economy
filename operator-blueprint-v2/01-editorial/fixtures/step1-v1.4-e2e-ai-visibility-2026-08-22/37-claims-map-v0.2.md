## Evidence-presentation contract

The evidence must remain rigorous without making the narrator read a bibliography aloud.

| Layer | Job | Required treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Voiceover | Keep the argument understandable for a listener who cannot see the screen | Use a conversational source name and the material limit once. Round a non-money figure when precision does not change the conclusion. Do not lead with the publication year, report title, sample count, or methodology. |
| On-screen evidence receipt | Carry precise provenance at the moment the claim appears | Show source, report or artifact title, publication date, population and sample when relevant, exact figure, locator, and claim ID. Keep it legible long enough to inspect. |
| Description or show notes | Preserve the complete citation | Include the direct source link, title, date, page or section, methodology note, access date, and the limitation recorded in the Step 0 registry. |
| Claims registry | Control what may be inferred | Preserve classification, transfer limit, confidence, and prohibited inference even when those details are not all spoken. |

Evidence receipt and show-note text are editorial requirements, not optional production decoration. If a visual receipt cannot be guaranteed, the script must carry enough extra context for the claim to remain bounded in audio alone.

Money, forecasts, constructed cases, and outcome claims still require an audible plain-English qualifier. A screen label cannot rescue an unqualified spoken earnings claim.
