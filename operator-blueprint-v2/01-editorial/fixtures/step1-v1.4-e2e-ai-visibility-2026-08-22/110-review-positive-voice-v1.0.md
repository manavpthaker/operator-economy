Counts do not prove identity. The functional difference does:

- V0.8 reports that the dashboard is green. V1.0 places the viewer at the dashboard and reacts fairly to what it can and cannot see.

Replacing those passages with v0.8 wording would preserve the information but materially weaken the narrator-viewer relationship. V1.0 therefore passes the neutral-documentary substitution challenge at the reviewer level.

### Operator build

The narrator treats the viewer as an experienced peer. The language connects prior work to source judgment and buyer access, then tells the viewer what to sell, refuse, test, and stop. ### Economics

`Two grand`, the owner's week, the first decision, and the paid-delivery instruction make the math operational rather than academic. The narrator does not promise income or call the scenario typical. He rules clearly that the arithmetic fitting is not the same as the buyer, price, delivery, support, or reach being proven.

### Ending

The green-report callback resolves the opening without declaring conventional search wrong. The first-person hypothetical is evidence-safe, gives the BUILD action with conviction, and introduces no biography. The business-of-one principle follows, and the exact CTA is the final spoken beat.
