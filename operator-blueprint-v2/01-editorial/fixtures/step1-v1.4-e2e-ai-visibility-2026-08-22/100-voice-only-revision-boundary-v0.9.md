## Exact structural invariants

### Thesis and company

The following decisions may not move or change meaning:

- Show thesis: an experienced professional uses AI to compress collection and coordination, applies judgment to a costly business problem, and begins building a company one accountable owner can sell and deliver. The goal is a reachable buyer set and an operating model that can eventually cover the work, not a large market share.
- Spoken company name: `AI visibility company`.
- Plain definition: `Helps brands understand how they appear in AI answers and decide what deserves action.`
- Opportunity thesis: the added answer doorway is observable, while the decision between the sample and buyer action remains unowned.
- Operator thesis: experience, judgment, context, permission, access, and accountability matter more than tool access.
- Mature company: agreed questions, recorded samples, source and fact review, controllability judgment, a recommendation, and repetition only when another look serves another buyer decision.
- Entry wedge: one fixed-scope paid diagnostic for one brand, one market, ten buyer-approved questions, and two answer surfaces. It returns an evidence record and an `act`, `investigate`, `monitor`, or `leave it alone` decision.
- BUILD verdict: construct and test the company through one paid diagnostic; expand only when buyer use, repeatability, responsible capacity, and a real next decision are observed.
- Unproven matters: demand, accepted price, acquisition, delivery burden, repeat value, owner support, sustainability, income, and client outcomes.
- Travel: story carrier and arithmetic example only. The reusable instruction is to begin in a market the operator understands and can credibly reach.
- Systemic target: software activity presented as a business result.
- People protected: buyer, worker, customer, and operator.
