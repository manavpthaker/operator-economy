# Episode beat sheet v0.5: workflow-reliability service

Status: approved for v1.3 fixture regression only; production use prohibited

Episode: unassigned fixture

Working promise: show how one costly handoff can become a responsible workflow-reliability service, including when the operator should refuse to automate it.

Central question: If every tool reports success while the client is still waiting, what exactly should an operator fix and sell?

Central thesis to earn later: the business is not connecting apps; it is making one consequential handoff visible, measurable, recoverable, and ownable.

Recurring carrier: a newly signed agency client waiting for the first clear next step.

## First-listen controls

- “Workflow reliability” appears only after the ordinary tool-by-tool growth pattern is clear.
- KfW and UK evidence describe the environment, not demand for the offer.
- The group-project analogy follows the literal handoff explanation.
- Service-category evidence, delivery, economics, retainer logic, and first test occupy separate beats.
- The modeled economics receive one boundary and a plain explanation of why labor changes the result.
- No beat combines a new abstraction, several figures, an analogy, and a joke.

## Identity

Fixed brand string:

> This is The Operator Economy, where we show you how to build, own, and operate a sustainable business of one using AI.

Episode-specific promise: show how one operator could turn a broken handoff into a workflow-reliability service, what AI and automation should handle, and how to tell when the workflow should not be automated.

AI role: help organize discovery, draft test cases and documentation, and reduce repetitive analysis; the operator and client retain permissions, exception judgment, recovery ownership, and acceptance.

Reachable-share logic: the operator needs a small number of buyers with recurring consequential handoffs, not broad adoption across all small businesses. The first test asks whether one reachable service segment contains enough of them.

## Ending loop

Opening question: Is the problem the software or ownership between systems?

Resolution: a reliable handoff creates visible state, a named owner, and a recovery path when the process stops.

Final action: sell one diagnostic before architecture or a retainer.
