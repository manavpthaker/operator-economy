# Story review: workflow-reliability script v0.1

Status: completed against immutable `07-script-v0.1.md`

## Findings

### WFR-ST-01 — The signed client disappears in the process section

Severity: high

The opening creates a human situation, but S03 through S07 become a strong process lecture. Bring the same hypothetical signed client back when testing missing intake, duplicate project creation, billing failure, and kickoff ownership.

### WFR-ST-02 — Category proof interrupts the causal story

Severity: medium

Partner programs are useful evidence, but two paragraphs of ecosystem description pause the transaction. Compress them and use them only to answer “is this a real service category?”

### WFR-ST-03 — The diagnostic-to-build decision is the dramatic turn

Severity: high

The episode becomes more than a checklist when the operator discovers that the workflow might not deserve automation. Make that decision the hinge between discovery and build.

### WFR-ST-04 — Closing resolves the correct product

Severity: positive

The first deliverable being evidence—not automation—lands the revised premise. Preserve it.
