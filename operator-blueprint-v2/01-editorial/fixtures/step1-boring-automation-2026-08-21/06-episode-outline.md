# Episode outline: workflow-reliability service

Status: approved for dry-run script drafting only

Working promise: Show how to turn one broken handoff into a tested workflow service and how to know when not to sell it.

Target test-script duration: 6-8 minutes

## Sequence map

| ID | Narrative job | Viewer question | Canvas coverage | Claims | Turn |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S01 | Make the failure human | What happens after the client signs? | Problem, stakes | C001, C002 | The client exists, but no system or person owns the next state. |
| S02 | Explain the mechanism | Why do more tools create hidden work? | Problem, why now | C001, C002, C007 | People become the integration and exceptions disappear. |
| S03 | Establish the category honestly | Do businesses already buy this work? | Proof, alternatives | C003-C006 | Exact services exist; new-operator performance does not follow. |
| S04 | Define the offer | What is the product? | Offer, buyer result | C008 | The product is a tested operating state, not the connection. |
| S05 | Walk delivery | How does the operator create reliability? | Workflow, stack, human control | C005, C007, C008 | Failure tests and ownership distinguish production work from a demo. |
| S06 | Expose economics | What must be true commercially? | Price, capacity, cost | C009 | Transparent labor makes the attractive model fragile. |
| S07 | Bound support and risk | What should remain human or be rejected? | Access, maintenance, risks | C007-C009 | Ongoing revenue exists only if an ongoing job exists. |
| S08 | Resolve with validation | What should the viewer test first? | Go to market, 30-day test | C003-C009 | Sell discovery before architecture; let evidence stop the business. |

## Opening contract

Start with the signed client and missing next action. Promise the operating blueprint and rejection test before discussing tools or partner programs.

## Ending

The viewer should leave believing the first deliverable is a workflow map and buyer-funded diagnostic—not an automation, retainer, or agency website.

## Boundary

No scene, visual, tool implementation, title, thumbnail, audio, or production direction is authorized here.
