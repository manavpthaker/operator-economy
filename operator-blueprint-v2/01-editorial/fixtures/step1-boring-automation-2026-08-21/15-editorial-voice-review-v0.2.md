## Why it fails

### WFR-VO-01 — The voice disappears after the opening

The opening sounds closer to the intended speaker, but much of the middle returns to formal process language: “Map the inputs, states, decisions, systems, credentials, exceptions, and consequences.” The script explains the model correctly without consistently talking one operator through it.

### WFR-VO-02 — Formal transitions flatten the advisor relationship

Transitions such as “Then the economic view,” “The responsible version asks whether an ongoing job exists,” and “The full test matrix belongs in the Canvas” read like a structured report. They need conversational questions, reactions, and direct guidance in the exact script.

### WFR-VO-03 — One prohibited spoken construction remains

S01 contains an em dash in “and—only if it should—building.” The thought boundary should be authored as speech before lock rather than delegated to performance.

### WFR-VO-04 — The strongest Manav patterns are isolated

The draft uses stacked declaratives and useful negation, but direct address, immediate question-and-answer, natural pivots, and objection handling are inconsistent across the full episode.
