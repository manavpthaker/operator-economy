# Narrative spine: workflow-reliability service

Status: approved for dry-run testing only

## Story in one sentence

A small agency celebrates a signed client, but the agreement dies between its systems; an operations-minded operator follows that client through every missing state, rebuilds only the stable path, forces the workflow through failures, and decides whether the result is reliable enough to sell and hand back.

## Viewer movement

Starting belief: The service is connecting apps and charging for automation.

Ending understanding: The service is a measured operating handoff with exception ownership, observability, documentation, and a reject path.

## Causal spine

- Protagonist: Operations-minded independent considering a client service.
- Human consequence: Agency staff and a newly signed client do not know who owns the next action.
- Persistent transaction: One modeled signed-client onboarding record.
- Constraint: State is split across systems; happy-path automation hides missing data, duplicates, access failures, and ambiguous decisions.
- Opening: Smaller firms use more productivity tools while exact service ecosystems formalize hands-on implementation.
- Mechanism: Observe -> map states -> keep judgment human -> build minimum stable path -> test failures -> add logs and ownership -> document -> monitor -> measure.
- Proof: Institutional adoption and burden research, two partner ecosystems, a delivery case, marketplace procurement, and current component capabilities.
- Decision: Whether one buyer will fund the complete reliability job and whether it fits the modeled capacity.
- Resolution: Paid diagnostic and sprint test with clear rejection conditions.

## Fairness

Simple native integrations, internal staff, or a quick automation may solve some workflows. The proposed service earns a fee only where the buyer needs process discovery, exception design, testing, observability, documentation, and accountable handoff.

## Sequence arc

1. A client signs; the next state disappears.
2. Humans are the hidden integration between tools.
3. Existing partner ecosystems prove the service category, not the new operator's success.
4. Select one bounded workflow and reject bad candidates.
5. Map the state and preserve human judgment.
6. Test failures, add observability, and hand control back.
7. Model price, labor, capacity, and support honestly.
8. Sell the diagnostic first and let the first pilot reject the business.

## Narrative verdict

Causality: pass

Recurring human situation: pass

Tool-tutorial risk: controlled by state, exception, and buyer-decision spine

Production authority: none
