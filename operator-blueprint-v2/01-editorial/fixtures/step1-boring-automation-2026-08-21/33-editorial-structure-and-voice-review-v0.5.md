### Earned thesis

The operator opportunity arrives after the normal system, human workaround, evidence, and fair cases for native connections, checklists, and unstable processes. The ruling is that a repeated consequential handoff may justify a service whose first job is diagnosis.

Decision: pass

## Business-of-one clarity

- Workflow tools handle stable flows, logs, alerts, retries, and errors.
- In the modeled service, AI organizes notes, drafts test cases, and helps document failures.
- The operator and client retain permissions, exception judgment, recovery ownership, safety, and acceptance.
- The first market test needs a small number of buyers with recurring consequential handoffs, not broad SME adoption.
- The economics section says the current scenario does not yet show whether one operator can live on it and names client count, delivery time, and price as the tests.

Decision: pass
