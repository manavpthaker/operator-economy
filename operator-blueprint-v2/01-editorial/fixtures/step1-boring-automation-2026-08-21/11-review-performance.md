# Performance review: workflow-reliability script v0.1

Status: completed against immutable `07-script-v0.1.md`

Verdict: revise before lock

## Findings

### WFR-PF-01 — Failure list is unperformable

Severity: high

“Ordinary, missing, duplicate, delayed, malformed, unauthorized, rate-limited, and downstream-unavailable” is accurate but sounds like a QA checklist. Group the failures into missing information, duplicate action, and unavailable downstream system, then state that the full test matrix lives in the Canvas.

### WFR-PF-02 — Economics need one comparison at a time

Severity: medium

The cash and labor-imputed contributions arrive too quickly. Separate the cash view from the economic view and pause before the 32-hour stress case.

### WFR-PF-03 — Tool names are appropriately limited

Severity: positive

The narration uses platforms only as evidence that the category and capabilities exist. It does not become a tutorial. Preserve this restraint.

### WFR-PF-04 — Repeated “workflow” creates mechanical rhythm

Severity: low

Use “handoff,” “process,” “system,” or the signed-client thread where the meaning stays exact.
