They belong to Narration Production. The observed speech profile is included here only because its lexical choices must already exist in the locked script.

## Rule

Reference material may inspire a rule, but it does not become V2 authority through mention or copying. A V2 rule must appear in an approved V2 standard and survive the V2 acceptance set.
