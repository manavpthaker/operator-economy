## Accepted editorial doctrine

Every future promoted V2 candidate must pass Gate E3I through an approved Episode Investment Thesis after the Operator Canvas locks and before narrative development begins.

The Investment Thesis keeps five decisions distinct:

1. **Show thesis:** an experienced professional can use AI to build, own, and operate a sustainable business of one around a reachable share of a large, costly problem.
2. **Opportunity thesis:** what changed, why the problem matters, and what complete company belongs in the gap.
3. **Operator thesis:** why the viewer's experience, judgment, access, or relationships create an advantage beyond access to tools.
4. **Entry-wedge thesis:** the first bounded purchase through which the operator begins building the larger company.
5. **BUILD verdict:** the evidence-safe recommendation to begin bounded construction under explicit proof and stop conditions.

The episode should give material weight to two flexible acts:

1. Make the opportunity worth believing.
2. Show the operator how to begin building the company.

The target is approximately half the narrative for each act, with a roughly 40–60 range unless the owner records a reasoned exception. This is a pacing target, not a rigid timer or mandatory slide order.

The complete mature company appears before a diagnostic, audit, sprint, installation, or other first offer is allowed to define the business. The entry wedge is a credible route into the company, not its ceiling.

The first 30 days begin constructing a buyer-facing asset, delivery capability, prospect motion, or paid engagement while replacing weak assumptions with operating evidence. The plan may include research needed for construction, but it cannot end in indefinite consideration.

A published Operator Blueprint requires an honest company-level `BUILD` verdict. If the evidence supports only `RETURN` or `PARK`, the candidate returns or parks before production. An individual buyer engagement may still responsibly end in no-build, refuse, narrow, defer, or stop.

`BUILD` is an editorial recommendation to begin bounded construction. It is not proof of demand, accepted pricing, repeat purchase, results, sustainable economics, or income.
