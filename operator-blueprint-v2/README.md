# Operator Blueprint V2

Status: Step 0.2 is canonical. Step 1 v1.4 is approved editorial authority. Step 2 is proposed v0.1. Stages 3-8 remain boundary-only and non-authoritative.

Operator Blueprint V2 is the planned end-to-end system for turning a validated business opportunity into an Operator Economy episode, an Operator Canvas, an audio edition, a finished video, an episode page, and distribution assets. The brand promise is to help a capable professional build, own, and operate a sustainable business of one using AI, without requiring venture-scale market share or promising income.

`00-intake/` owns opportunity intake, candidate research, validation, and promotion under the locked Step 0.2 rules. `01-editorial/` owns the Operator Canvas, Episode Investment Thesis, narrative, exact script, and editorial lock under approved v1.4. The editorial system separates the complete company from its entry offer, earns belief in the opportunity before teaching the build, uses a short spoken business name plus a precise internal definition, and requires an evidence-safe affirmative BUILD verdict before an episode can proceed. `02-narration-production/` contains a proposed narration standard, gates, roles, and templates, but is not yet production authority. No V2 episode exists yet.

## Episode workspaces

Process standards live in the numbered stage folders above. Future episode artifacts will live under `episodes/EP###-kebab-case-slug/`, with the same numbered stage folders inside each episode workspace. Step 1 assigns an episode number only after a valid Step 0 promotion and a complete editorial handoff. No number is assigned to a candidate merely because research started.

The `episodes/` directory currently contains documentation only. It is not an episode and does not authorize creating one.

## V1 boundary

`studio/` and `blueprint-cinema/` remain in place as V1 production systems and evidence. Nothing under this V2 root authorizes moving, deleting, or continuing a V1 artifact. Material enters V2 only through an explicit port record.

Step 0.2 and Step 1 v1.4 are canonical only for new V2 opportunity intake and editorial development. Step 1 approval does not promote a fixture, assign an episode number without a valid Step 0 promotion, lock a fixture script, authorize narration, or make a proposed company, demand level, price, outcome, or livelihood claim true. Do not describe Stages 2-8, or Operator Blueprint V2 as a complete end-to-end production system, as canonical until each relevant stage is separately reviewed and approved. Root agent guidance remains unchanged by this stage design work.
