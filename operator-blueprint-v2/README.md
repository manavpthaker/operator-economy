# Operator Blueprint V2

Status: Step 0.2 is canonical, amended by the approved Step 0.3 POV factor (2026-09-01). Step 1 v1.5 is approved editorial authority. Step 2 v0.5 is approved narration authority, locked 2026-09-01. Stages 3-8 remain boundary-only and non-authoritative. The first candidate was promoted 2026-09-01; no episode number is assigned yet.

Operator Blueprint V2 is the planned end-to-end system for turning a validated business opportunity into an Operator Economy episode, an Operator Canvas, an audio edition, a finished video, an episode page, and distribution assets. The brand promise is to help a capable professional build, own, and operate a sustainable business of one using AI, without requiring venture-scale market share or promising income.

`00-intake/` owns opportunity intake, candidate research, validation, and promotion under the locked Step 0.2 rules. `01-editorial/` owns the Operator Canvas, pitch-deck coverage map, Episode Investment Thesis, narrative, exact script, and editorial lock under approved v1.5. The editorial system separates the complete company from its entry offer, earns belief in the opportunity before teaching the build, maps the complete blueprint across VO, visuals, and the downloadable Canvas, uses a short spoken business name plus a precise internal definition, and requires an evidence-safe affirmative BUILD verdict before an episode can proceed. `02-narration-production/` owns the performance, capture, edit, conformity, timing, and narration lock for the locked words, and is approved production authority as of 2026-09-01. Its narration method is a two-stage acted-guide chain that separates performance from identity: Google `gemini-2.5-pro-tts` performs the exact locked words under a method-level register, and ElevenLabs Voice Changer transfers that performance onto the owner's saved voice identity. N3 configuration freeze and N4A calibration both passed on the AI Visibility v1.1 fixture. N4B full capture remains unauthorized and requires a real episode with a current Step 1 lock. The first candidate was promoted through Step 0 on 2026-09-01 and awaits editorial development; no numbered V2 episode exists yet.
