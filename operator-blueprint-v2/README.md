# Operator Blueprint V2

Status: Step 0.2 approved and canonical for V2 opportunity intake; Stages 1-7 remain boundary-only and non-authoritative.

Operator Blueprint V2 is the planned end-to-end system for turning a validated business opportunity into an Operator Economy episode, an Operator Canvas, an audio edition, a finished video, an episode page, and distribution assets.

Only `00-intake/` is approved. It owns opportunity intake, candidate research, validation, and promotion into editorial development under the locked Step 0.2 rules. No V2 episode exists yet.
