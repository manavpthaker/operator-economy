## Evidence pass

### Fact (verified, primary)

- Los Alamos County, NM contracted LSP Data Solutions (effective 2023-12-13, NTE $350,000) to
  "review, prepare, process, respond and document" IPRA/FOIA requests — search, review, redaction,
  production — on task orders at $137.50–$245/hr; completed requests may go to the county's records
  manager before release (CLM-001).
- Michigan MCL 15.234(1)(b): an agency lacking capable staff may charge contracted separation labor
  like employee labor, capped at six times the state minimum wage, naming the contractor (CLM-002).
- Washington 2024: 483,861 requests, $128 million, 1.7 million hours, 24 days average (CLM-005).

### Reported (secondary)

  months vs 53 in 2025, $96,556.58 outside legal for records processing (CLM-003, CLM-004; AI-written
  meeting summaries).
- Los Alamos contract raised to $722,000 through 2026-12-12 citing request volume (CLM-001b).
- Ithaca, NY RFP for a "firm or individual" to supplement FOIL staff; city keeps disclosure decisions
  (CLM-006; news, RFP not opened).
