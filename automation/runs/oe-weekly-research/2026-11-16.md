## Evidence pass

Same agent as the adversarial pass; not independent.

- Fact: a daily money manager that takes client money into its own account is a money transmitter
  (FinCEN FIN-2012-R004, CLM-010). Texas regulates third-party bill paying (CLM-011).
- Fact: a person the consumer gives a login or card to makes "authorized" transfers; the consumer
  is fully liable if that person exceeds their authority, until the bank is told (Regulation E,
  12 CFR 1005.2(m)(1) and comment 2(m)-2, CLM-012).
- Fact: view-only third-party access is a voluntary bank feature (CFPB 2016; interagency statement
  2024, CLM-013).
- Fact: in Texas and Florida any person must report suspected exploitation (CLM-014; Texas read
  through a mirror of the official text).
- Fact: 60+ complainants reported 201,266 complaints and $7.748 billion in losses to FBI IC3 in
  2025 (CLM-015). Scam side only; says nothing about missed bills.
- Seller statement: EverSafe monitors accounts daily for $7.49 to $26.99 a month and cannot move
  money (CLM-016).
- Anecdote (Reddit, read through Xpoz): an out-of-town child asks about a household manager and
  what security checks exist; a commenter praises a bank-referred, bonded agency's quarterly
  accounting to everyone (CLM-017, CLM-018).
- Model: unchanged and untested. `Modeled scenario, not observed performance or an earnings
  forecast.`
- Inference: the defensible desk never holds money and never pays with a shared login.

  the payment to them.
