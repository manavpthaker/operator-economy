## New lead: `DISC-2026-10-02-017` — condo financeability readiness for small self-managed associations (held)

- Change: Fannie Mae retired Limited Review for established condo projects for applications from
  2026-08-03 (Freddie Mac ended its streamlined review in parallel); roughly 40% of condo project
  reviews had used it (Mile High Title Guy, citing industry figures). From 2027-01-04 the minimum
  reserve allocation rises from 10% to 15% of budgeted assessment income unless the association has a
  reserve study within three years and funds at its recommended level (Habitat Magazine 2026-06 and
  2026-09; Eclipse Communities, CommunityPay — trade and vendor copy, context).
- Buyer / decision: boards of small, especially self-managed, condo associations deciding this fall
  whether to raise dues, commission a reserve study, or accept that units may become hard to finance.
- Costly problem: every sale now needs a full-review lender questionnaire answered from the
  association's budget, insurance, delinquency, litigation and minutes; a project that fails becomes
  non-warrantable, shrinking the buyer pool to cash and portfolio lenders.
- Offer / outcome hypothesis: a fixed-fee financeability check for a small association — gap list
  against Fannie/Freddie project standards, a pre-answered full-review packet the board signs, and a
  budget option set for the reserve vote. Outcome: project passes full review; questionnaire
  turnaround in days.
- Mechanism: AI reads declarations, bylaws, budget, insurance certificates, minutes and delinquency
  reports against the Selling Guide project standards and drafts answers and gaps; operator checks
  and assembles; the board signs.
- **AI-change test: plausibly passes.** AI reads hundreds of pages of association records against a
  long criteria list; before, this was management-company staff time bundled into the management fee
  or a volunteer board member's evenings.
- **Delivery boundary: partly resolved.** Answering a lender questionnaire needs no licence; the
  board or manager certifies. The reserve-study route (the alternative to 15%) needs a reserve
  specialist, credentialed in some states (and a structural study by an engineer or architect in
  Florida); interpreting governing documents goes to association counsel. Residual: an unlicensed
  operator assembles and pre-answers the full-review packet and gap list for the board to sign.
- **Substitute (decisive for the hold):** CondoCerts (free to the association; requester pays), 
  Association Online, and CommunityPay (auto-fills Form 1076 from ledger data) already output the
  questionnaire; lender-side Rexera does project review with AI; management companies bundle it
  (about $275 per questionnaire, Brick Underground 2023, NYC). Whether self-managed small associations
  are actually served by these platforms is not established.
- **Problem-value signals:** `problem cost` — deals stalling on association records, worst for
  self-managed boards (Mile High Title Guy 2026; Brick Underground 2023); non-warrantable status
  shrinks buyer pools (trade press). `old-way spend` context — management-company questionnaire fees
  (~$275, 2023, NYC, seller-side figure); Connecticut legislated on questionnaire fees in 2026 (Public
  Act 26-31, secondary summary, unverified).
- **Payment evidence:** unknown — viewer's first test.
- Audience signal: weak. Google Trends US 12 months: both terms sparse and low; "condo questionnaire"
  peaked around June 2026, then near zero through August–September. No board-member or owner voice
  found; sources are title, realtor, lender and vendor copy.
- Strongest invalidating question: once software fills the questionnaire, is anything left for an
  operator that is not a reserve study (credentialed) or a legal opinion (counsel)?
- Reopening condition: (1) a board-side or owner question surface (forum, Quora titles, Stack
  Exchange) asking for help with full review or the 15% rule, **and** (2) evidence that small
  self-managed associations are not served by CondoCerts / CommunityPay-type platforms, or a residual
  those platforms do not output (for example, the reserve-vote budget pack). Recheck 2026-11-15, before
  most boards adopt 2027 budgets; expires 2027-03-31.
- Possible OE point of view: a lender rule written for Fannie Mae's risk now lands on volunteers;
  the association's records decide every owner's resale price, and AI makes reading them cheap.
- Semantic deduplication: repository search for "HOA", "condo", "resale certificate" found no lead,
