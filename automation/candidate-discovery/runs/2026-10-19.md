## Re-screens

Chosen because their rejections named self-serve tools.

1. **Distributor deductions for emerging CPG brands (rejected 2026-09-29): stays rejected.**
   Fresh observations #1–#8 show real problem cost and old-way spend ($21/hr to $80k deductions
   staff; a founder paying full-time people). But under test 4(a) a done-for-you AI service already
   delivers the outcome to this exact buyer: Glimpse "runs the entire deductions process" for 200+
   brands from emerging to $150M in the natural channel, for a low monthly fee plus commission,
   and replaced a $3,000–$4,000-a-month contractor at one brand. One operator cannot beat a
   $35M-funded service on price for the same segment. Reopening condition: buyer-side evidence that
   brands below a revenue floor are refused by Glimpse and OverDeduct, or left them, with the reason.
2. **Back-of-book indexing for authors (rejected 2026-10-10): stays rejected.** Fresh observations
   #9–#10: publishers make authors responsible for the index, and an academic author indexed his own
   book and then built an AI tool to do it, now used by authors and indexers directly. That is test
   4(b): buyers run the tool themselves, and the remaining edit goes to the author or an existing
   indexer. Reopening condition: authors or small presses paying an outside, non-indexer provider per
   book for AI-built indexes, with price.
