## Ideas screened

### Estate settlement for executors (lane 3) — rejected

- Question: family executors spend months closing accounts, inventorying assets and filing
  paperwork, and pay lawyers or bank trust departments for it. Can one AI-equipped operator do the
  non-legal work for a flat fee?
- `old-way spend` (context, seller and bank pages): bank trust schedule at 5.0% on the first
  $400,000 (Chain Bridge Bank, 2021 schedule); guides put basic probate at about $3,000–$10,000
  (content marketing).
- Substitute: Alix sells a non-lawyer settlement service at 1% of the gross estate (elayne.com
  pricing page); AI self-service tools sell to executors directly, one with a free tier and a $39
  one-time option (Swift Probate's 2026 roundup); EstateExec software.
- Delivery boundary: court filings and legal advice stay with counsel in many states; residual is
  paperwork and account closing — what Alix and the tools already sell.
- Why rejected: fails the substitute check; AI-backed service and direct tools already cover the
  residual.
- Reopening condition: evidence that Alix-type services refuse small estates while executors pay
  someone between the $39 tool and a 1% service.

### Amazon seller suspension appeals (lane 3) — rejected
