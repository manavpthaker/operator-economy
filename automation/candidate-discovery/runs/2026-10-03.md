   Old-way spend exists: Maricopa County Public Fiduciary bills $85/hr, about 5 hours a year, to
   prepare conservator accounts (<https://www.maricopa.gov/517/Fiduciary-Fees>); courts disapprove
   deficient accountings (Florida order form; El Dorado and Santa Barbara tentative rulings, SERP).
   Rejected on the substitute: courts themselves now give lay guardians free bank-linked accounting
   tools (Guardianship Accounting Program, 22nd Circuit IL <https://www.22ndcircuitil.gov/?p=2176>;
   Ramsey County MN CAMPER; Societal Systems public portal), and California AB 2090 proposes exempting
   small estates from accounting (CalMatters Digital Democracy, SERP). The residual is what the free
   portal outputs. Reopen only with a state whose court offers no portal and a family guardian paying
   an outside non-attorney preparer.

2. **Submittal packages for small construction subcontractors (lane 3).** No buyer-side spend or
   question found; and AI submittal builders already sell the job: Procore AI Submittal Builder
   (March 2026, per Datagrid/Procore copy), mbue Submittals (AEC Magazine), Anyset, Pype. Reopen only
   with a small subcontractor paying a person per package after those tools.

3. **Contents inventory for total-loss home claims.** Regulators are pushing the other way: California
   and Colorado require or urge contents payments without an itemized inventory (CDI notices; Colorado
   DOI advisory via UP Help, 2026-07); State Farm advances 65% without one. Help for the rest is
   public adjusting, licensed in most states. Fails the delivery-boundary test. Reopen only with a
   state where unlicensed inventory preparation is permitted and buyers pay for it.

4. **Title abstracts and runsheets (lane 3).** Spend exists (Baltimore County on-call abstracting
   solicitation, 2026; abstractor job postings, SERP), but AI products sell the job to the people who
   buy abstracts: RecordsOnline AI Suite (ChainOfTitleAI, RunsheetAI, June 2026), Energy Domain
   TitleLab (2026-02-18), Enverus. Reopen only with buyers who are refused or underserved by those
   tools.

5. **Marketplace policy changes, autumn 2026.** Only dated change found: Amazon commercial liability
