- **Operating change (primary):** LCRI published 2024-10-30; EPA page (updated 2025-12-29) states the
  ten-year identify-and-replace requirement. Baseline inventory and replacement plan due 2027-11-01
  (the date is from secondary summaries citing the Federal Register notice; the notice itself was not
  read in full this run — the bench should confirm).
- **Audience/question signal (independent of the rule text):** Google autocomplete returns
  buyer-side formulations — "lead service line inventory deadline / requirements / template /
  guidance / grant". Weak, but independent.
- **Willingness to pay (accepted type: disclosed engagement):** Victoria, Texas (primary city release)
  paid RJN Group for records review plus non-excavation meter-box inspection, which is the residual; no
  amount. O'Fallon, Illinois ($62,850, about 500 unknowns, WHKS, 2025-07-29) and Ladd, Illinois
  ($5,000, Chamlin Engineering, 2025-11-25) are secondary reports of disclosed contracts. A primary
  search for O'Fallon's packet returned only city index pages.
- **Delivery boundary:** stated in one sentence in the pool. The system's certified operator certifies
  and submits; Utah guidance accepts observations by staff, plumbers, operators and contractors;
  excavation and engineering judgment stay with licensed parties.
- **Substitute:** 120Water software and BlueConduit prediction record or prioritise; neither verifies
  a pipe physically.
- **Risk:** AWWA v. EPA oral argument in the D.C. Circuit reported for 2026-09-30. Every observed
  payee is an engineering or consulting firm, which is the lead's main weakness.

Why shortlisted rather than held: it passes both tests on the evidence standard the 2026-09-21 run
applied. That run treated disclosed engagements without a price as accepted-type evidence that failed
only because the buyer did not match the lead. Here the buyer matches. The payee caveat and the
primary-minutes gap go to the bench as its first checks, not as reasons to lower the bar.

### 2. No-tax-on-tips W-2 reporting install — rejected

- Change (secondary but consistent across RSM, IRS newsroom title, Inquirer 2026-09-02): 2026 W-2s
  must carry qualified tips (Box 12, code TP) and the Treasury Tipped Occupation Code (Box 14b); only
  voluntary tips qualify, not service charges or automatic gratuities.
- Question signal: autocomplete returns "does no tax on tips apply to auto gratuity", "no tax on tips
  employer requirements", "service charge vs tip restaurant".
- Rejected on the substitute test: Gusto documents tip and service-charge handling, and TipCompliance
  maps roles to TTOC codes and splits tips from service charges for $79–$149/mo (product page, read
  2026-09-23). The residual is substantially a software output. The only non-software part —
  whether a restaurant should turn automatic gratuities into voluntary tips — had no payment signal.

### 3. SBA SOP 50 10 8.1 quality-of-earnings requirement — screened out as a duplicate, noted for the bench

- From 2026-10-01, SBA 7(a) change-of-ownership loans of $3M or more need an independent quality of
  earnings report, ordered by the lender, reconciling books to bank statements and tax filings; all
  acquisitions move to full underwriting (PBMares, AAFCPAs 2026-09-14, EisnerAmper).
- Buyer-side QoE is ordered and paid for by the lender and usually done by accounting firms. The
  seller-side preparation it rewards is the promoted `candidate-2026-09-01-exit-readiness-prep`. That
  candidate names its transfer risk as "an owner's exit is neither scheduled nor mandatory"; a lender
  requirement for earnings substantiation partly answers that. The scout may not edit Step 0 artifacts,
  so this is recorded here only as possible "why now" evidence for the bench.
