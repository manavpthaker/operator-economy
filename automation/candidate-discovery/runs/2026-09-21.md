### Cross-border duty, classification, and landed cost for small importers

1. **CBP national media release, 2026-06-24 — "CBP modernizes low-value shipment processing."**
   States that duty-free de minimis treatment was suspended by executive order effective
   **2025-08-29**, that imports valued at $800 or less lose duty-free status, that shipments valued
   at $2,500 or less are subject to applicable duties, and that narrow gift and personal-article
   exceptions remain. CBP describes enhanced information requirements for mail shipments and a new
   electronic entry process under test. The release does not itself address HTS classification
   mechanics.
   <https://www.cbp.gov/newsroom/national-media-release/cbp-modernizes-low-value-shipment-processing>
2. **Federal Register, published 2026-06-24 — "Indefinite Suspension of the De Minimis Exemption for
   Merchandise Arriving Through All Modes Other Than the International Postal Network."** Indexed

   read directly in this run; title, date, and effective date are taken from the search index and are
   flagged as unverified against the document text.
   <https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/06/24/2026-12670/indefinite-suspension-of-the-de-minimis-exemption-for-merchandise-arriving-through-all-modes-other>

   <https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/06/24/2026-12669/indefinite-suspension-of-the-de-minimis-exemption-for-mail-shipments-and-new-postal-informal-entry>

## Dispositions

### Shortlisted — `DISC-2026-09-21-006` — cross-border duty and landed-cost system for small importers

Meets the qualification bar through the operating-change route: one material operating change (CBP's
low-value shipment regime, with the de minimis suspension effective 2025-08-29 and CBP's modernised
entry rules published 2026-06-24) plus one independent audience/question signal (the r/smallbusiness
classification thread surfaced in the SERP with 16 answers, reinforced by a live People Also Ask set
on exactly that question). Audience and thesis fit are strong: this is a margin, dependence, and
accountability story, and the importer of record keeps the liability whatever a forwarder or a
chatbot says. The work is showable without a guest — a SKU master with codes, origin evidence, and a
landed-cost model can be put on screen. Editorial priority is **first**, for one decisive reason: it
is the only lead in the pool attached to a rule that has already changed the buyer's cost per parcel,
and its delivery boundary is itself the argument.

The boundary is severe and must stay visible. Classification and valuation are named in 19 CFR 111.1
as customs business; doing it for others without a licence risks penalties. Any viable version of
this service either stops short of determining another company's classification or runs alongside a
licensed broker, with the operator owning the data, the supplier documentation, the margin model, and
the decision record rather than the filing.

### Held — `DISC-2026-09-21-007` — card acceptance cost and surcharging decision install

Plausible and squarely on thesis — merchant margin, pricing rights, and recourse against a
network-set cost — but blocked on two named conditions. First, the rules settlement that supposedly
expands surcharging has not been verified against a court document; the only primary source read this
run documents a damages distribution. Second, every description of the new merchant rights came from
firms that sell payment services. Held, not rejected, because the underlying buyer problem is real
and dated.

Reopening condition: a court document or other primary record confirming the injunctive-relief
settlement's approval status and surcharging terms, **and** one buyer-side signal that a merchant
paid someone other than its processor to make and implement the acceptance decision. Recheck
2026-11-01.
