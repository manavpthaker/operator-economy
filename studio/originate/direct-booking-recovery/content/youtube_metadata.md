## Description

Independent hotels lose about two thirds of their bookings to Booking.com and Expedia, paying 18–30% in all-in commission once loyalty programs like Genius and Accelerator are added (Cloudbeds, 2026 State of Independent Hotels, ~90M bookings analyzed: https://www.cloudbeds.com/online-travel-agencies/commissions/). Run the arithmetic on a 20-room, $180-ADR property at 70% occupancy and that's roughly $135,000 a year — an illustrative model with stated assumptions, not a universal figure.

The infrastructure to fix this just got heavily funded: Mews raised $300M at a $2.5B valuation in January 2026, the largest round in hospitality software history (PR Newswire: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/mews-secures-300-million-investment-to-cement-position-as-worlds-leading-hospitality-operating-system-302668120.html). Cloudbeds has raised $250M including SoftBank and serves 27,000 properties.

This episode covers direct booking recovery: installing and running the rate-parity, booking-engine, missed-call, and past-guest re-book stack for independent hotels — priced against the commission saved, in a market where almost no provider publishes a price.
