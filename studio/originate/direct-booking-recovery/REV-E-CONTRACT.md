# Direct Booking Recovery — Rev E Production Contract

## Governing Idea

The approved **WIN THEM BACK** thumbnail is the episode’s visual thesis. OTA gravity pulls guests, keys, bookings, and margin away from an independent inn. Direct-booking recovery creates a visible path back to the operator. Every scene must express peril, mechanism, intervention, or recovered agency. Decorative slides do not qualify.

## Opening Sequence

1. Hold the exact thumbnail for 0.8 seconds so the click and video match.
2. Run a 15–25 second cold open that dramatizes the loss before explaining it: guest relationship, OTA share, commission, annual consequence, estimate caveat.
3. Cut to the universal Operator Economy ident and sting. Do not overlay it on unfinished narration.
4. Follow with: “This is The Operator Economy. This week, we’re looking at direct booking recovery.”
5. State the outcome before inventorying the stack.

## Narrative Waveform

`gravity → consequence → recognition → counter-system → installation → recovery → agency`

- **Gravity:** OTAs are useful discovery engines whose incentives steadily pull the relationship away from the property.
- **Consequence:** Show commission, cancellation, and lost repeat-booking control with sourced numbers.
- **Recognition:** The operator is not negligent; the property lacks manpower and revenue-management infrastructure.
- **Counter-system:** Introduce the demand task force as a connected path, not a logo collection.
- **Installation:** Audit discovery, repair the destination, assign agent roles, connect booking, publish pricing, measure recovered mix.
- **Recovery:** A returning guest books direct and is welcomed by the property.
- **Agency:** Close on a manageable operator practice, not lifestyle fantasy or anti-OTA absolutism.

## YouTube-First Visual System

- **Type:** Supreme 800 for titles and arguments; Supreme 500 for explanation; Fragment Mono only for sources, numbers, and operational labels. No editorial serif in video.
- **Grounds:** sunlit photography and warm paper for humans/outcomes; navy and ink for mechanisms/peril.
- **Motion:** OTA gravity moves down/right in Booking blue. Direct recovery moves back toward the property in ledger gold. Motion must encode the argument.
- **Scale:** one dominant idea per frame; titles 72–148 px; proof numbers 180–260 px; body copy at least 32 px.
- **Brand use:** Booking.com and Expedia marks may identify the market force. Tool logos appear only inside an explained workflow.

## Scene Families

- **Human footage:** full-bleed, warm, minimal caption, explicit story role.
- **Gravity map:** guests/keys/bookings pulled toward OTA channels; gold counter-path appears only when recovery begins.
- **Evidence chart:** one claim, one dominant comparison, large direct labels, source at the edge.
- **Proof frame:** one number plus assumptions or provenance; estimates must visibly say estimate.
- **Outcome stack:** a left-to-right operating path from discovery to direct booking to re-booking, never a floating logo cloud.
- **Operator playbook:** numbered installation steps with the active action owning the screen.
- **Screen evidence:** real or clearly labeled reconstructed interfaces, tightly cropped to the action being narrated.
- **Case memory:** Coqui Coqui is sensory resort footage plus the specific operational lesson; the B&B is quaint, residential, and manpower-constrained.
- **Risk frame:** show the renewal/failure mechanism, not generic warning decoration.
- **CTA:** return to the host–guest relationship, then reveal the blueprint as the practical next action.

## Production Gates

- Exact thumbnail is frame zero.
- Cold open completes before the ident.
- No narration continues invisibly beneath the ident.
- By 30 seconds: human relationship, market force/proof, and counter-outcome are visible.
- No masks, pandemic reception, corporate lobbies, generic offices, or unexplained stock.
- Every chart and number has an on-frame source or estimate label.
- Every screen family passes a representative-frame review before the full render.
- The full episode cannot render with unresolved footage, screen capture, rights, or placeholder tickets.
