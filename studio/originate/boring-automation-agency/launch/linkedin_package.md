## 1. Episode post — Mon 2026-07-27, 11:00 AM ET (attach carousel-003.pdf LAST, then Schedule)

Zapier is worth about five billion dollars for one boring job: moving data between apps that don't talk to each other. No breakthrough, no moat, just plumbing.

The solo version of that same job reportedly pays $500 to $5,000 a month per client, for work most people find too dull to bother with. That gap between boring and valuable is the whole episode.

Episode № 003 breaks down the boring-automation agency: build the workflows on n8n or Make, drop an AI node in the middle to read the messy stuff a human used to handle, and charge a build fee plus a monthly retainer. Who pays, the stack (under $100 a month before the client's own software), how to price it, and the three ways it fails: platform risk, commoditization, and the one nobody automates, distribution.

The honest part: there is no audited solo case study at scale here. The low-end numbers come from agency how-to guides, not books, so they stay flagged as reported. Realistic year-one is $2,000 to $6,000 a month from a few clients, and that's an estimate, not a promise.

At Lovingly I watched a team move the same data between three systems by hand, every week. A few workflows automated about 80% of that busywork. The hours that came back were the entire point.

Full episode, blueprint, and sources in the comments.
