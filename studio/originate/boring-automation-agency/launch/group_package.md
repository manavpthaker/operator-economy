## Post copy (paste-ready)

Zapier is worth about five billion dollars for connecting apps that don't talk to each other. The solo version of that same job reportedly pays $500 to $5,000 a month per client, on a tool stack that costs under $100 a month. Those low-end bands come from agency how-to guides, not audited books, so hold them loosely.

Here's the part I keep going back and forth on, and I'd take this group's read over my own. Once a solo operator has three or four of these retainers running, what's the smarter next move: stay bespoke, hand-building each client's workflows and defending the messy, judgment-heavy integrations nobody wants to touch, or productize the same five workflows and sell them a hundred times? The bespoke path is harder to commoditize but caps out at your hours. The productized path scales but starts competing with the platforms themselves. If you were building this, which one are you building?

Breakdown attached. Source: The Operator Economy.
