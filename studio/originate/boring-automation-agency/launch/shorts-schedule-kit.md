Small companies run on tools that were never designed to talk to each other. The CRM, the invoicing tool, the web form, the spreadsheet, Slack. Someone loses hours a week wiring them together by hand.

The reason one person can now do what used to take an integration team: the plumbing became drag-and-drop. At Lovingly I watched a few workflows take about 80% of that busywork off a team's plate.
