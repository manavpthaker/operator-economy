## 1. Episode post — Mon 2026-08-03, 11:00 AM ET

*Media: `carousel-004.pdf` once rendered (see RUNBOOK). Composer order: set text → set schedule → attach media LAST → Schedule immediately.*

One business. Five different revenue numbers. Same year.

Designjoy, a solo design agency with no employees, has been reported at $1M, $1.5M, $1.7M, $2M and $3.1M in annual revenue, all covering roughly the same window. Every one of those traces back to the founder's own posts. There is no audited figure anywhere in that chain, so the episode carries all of it flagged as self-reported.

What is consistent across every version: zero employees, about 35 concurrent clients, a flat $4,995 a month, roughly five hours of work a day.

Episode № 004 breaks down the solo subscription design agency. What AI actually collapsed (the production work, not the picking), the four-tool stack that runs under $200 a month, how the category is priced from a $549 commodity floor up to Superside's $15,000 minimum, and the two failure modes nobody puts on a pricing page: churn math, and the fact that distribution was always the real moat.

The comparison that frames it: Superside reports $44.9M in ARR selling the same subscription-design product, with about 850 employees behind it. That gap is either an efficiency story about what one person can now cover, or a warning about what one person can't. Probably both, depending on which client you ask.

The episode also runs its own unflattering number in the open. Six cold outreach emails on a live experiment, one callback, zero signed clients. One in six looks great against reported 3-5% benchmarks. Six emails is not a sample size, and calling it a 17% response rate would be exactly the move the episode spends five minutes criticizing.

Full episode, blueprint, and sources in the comments.

## Composer gotchas (the ones that have actually cost us posts)

Set text → set the schedule → attach media LAST → hit Schedule immediately. Media dropped before a schedule-dialog round trip gets discarded. The time field only commits via dropdown-select or blur; typed values silently don't count. You can't schedule less than 10 minutes out, and you can't open a new composer while a video upload is still processing.

**Video and document attach cannot be automated.** Confirmed on EP003, 7/30: the media button opens a native OS file picker and no `input[type=file]` exists in the DOM until it does. Every post above needs the file attached by hand.
