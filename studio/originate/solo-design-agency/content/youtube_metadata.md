## Description

AI collapsed the production side of design work — layouts, variants, wireframes, working sites — down to one person and about $150 a month in tools. This episode breaks down the solo subscription design agency model: the most-cited case (Designjoy, reportedly $1M–$3.1M/yr depending on the source), the venture-scale proof of market (Superside, $44.9M ARR with 850 employees), and the honest gap in between. We name the number variance instead of picking the biggest one, show the real tool stack, and walk through what actually gets a first client in this category. Includes the build plan, realistic economics by effort level, and the failure modes nobody puts on the pricing page.
