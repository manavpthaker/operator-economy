You can build a direct-booking practice for small hotels. Get one constraint right before you pick a tool. Booking dot com's own partner help says it does not share private guest email addresses. Both sides see an alias ending at guest dot booking dot com, and partners are asked to keep the conversation on the platform. So you cannot win a guest back from a relay address, and you are not supposed to try. What the inn needs first is not an email tool. It is a guest book of its own, at the front desk, that the guest chooses to sign. The registration card and the wifi login ask for the guest's own email. That list runs under the American email law, and European rules for European guests. Check your jurisdiction. This is not legal advice.
