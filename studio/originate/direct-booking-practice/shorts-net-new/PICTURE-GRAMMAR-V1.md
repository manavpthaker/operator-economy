### Rules that travel

1. **Three movements, in order.** Presenter opens. Full-screen designed proof owns the mechanism.
   Presenter returns to land the conclusion. No fourth movement, and the presenter does not
   reappear inside the screen movement.
2. **Open on the presenter, never on a logo, bumper or graphic.**
3. **The presenter returns beside the settled graphic**, which reduces to a small number of stable
   labels. The mechanism is not replayed and not replaced with success icons.
4. **Hard cuts at every movement boundary.** No crossfade, whip pan, zoom punch, ambient drift or
   pulse. The only exception is the end bridge's shared-element match cut.
5. **Continuous program audio.** One exact narration master runs under every frame; the presenter
   video is muted and visible speech is restored to the master. Modes are `presenter_address` or
   `silent_graphic`.
6. **Clean frame.** No persistent heading, no persistent root footer. The owner's words were "do we
   need the heading through the whole thing? do we want to make it seem more natural less
   produced".
7. **No double-tracking.** When a phrase lands as embedded on-screen type, it drops from the caption
   rail for that moment. A proof card never repeats an overlay verbatim, which is what
   EP009-SHORTS-004's Short 01 did.
8. **Protected holds.** A resolved graphic holds into its cut. The completed answer holds after the
   last word before anything routes away.
9. **End bridge.** 2.000 s, silent, declarative, entered by a shared-element match cut after the
   full post-word hold. No URL, link capsule or fake button; native Related Video is the click
   path. Keep the rightmost 180 px and bottom 300 px clear of platform UI.
10. **No episode CTA before the standalone payoff is complete.**

Mean 0.441 against EP007's 0.384. Shorts 1 and 4 run presenter-heavy because their payoffs are
verbal: the three failure states and the ceiling are conclusions the presenter states, not

three-movement arc or cut the presenter return to about three seconds, which reads as a flash
rather than a return. EP007's return frame was 8.958 s.

**This is a judgment, not a measurement.** If you want these closer to EP007's ratio, the lever is
the presenter return on Shorts 1 and 4, and I would rather be told than guess. No ratio target
exists in any owner record; the grammar does.
