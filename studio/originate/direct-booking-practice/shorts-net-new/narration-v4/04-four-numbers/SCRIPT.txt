You can build a direct-booking practice, and price it honestly off four numbers you ask the owner for. Rooms. Average nightly rate. Occupancy. And the share of bookings arriving through the sites. That gives you the revenue the sites carry. Apply a commission band and you have the annual cost. Then assume a shift in the mix, and say out loud that the size of that shift is a hypothesis nobody has measured. Subtract what running direct costs. What is left is the recoverable amount, a month. That is the ceiling, and your fee lives under it. On an illustrative twenty room inn at a hundred and eighty a night and seventy percent occupancy, it lands near a thousand to fourteen hundred dollars a month. Some properties cannot fund this. The arithmetic tells you which, before you quote.
