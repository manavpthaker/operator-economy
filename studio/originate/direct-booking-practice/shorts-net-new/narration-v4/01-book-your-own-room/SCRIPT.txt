You can build a practice that helps a small hotel get its returning guests to book direct, instead of paying a booking site to meet them again. You can run the first piece tonight, on your phone. Pick an independent property near you, twenty to forty rooms. Search for it the way a guest would. Does a free link to the hotel's own site show up beside the site rates? Google shows one for a property connected through a partner, and charges nothing for it. Compare the direct rate to the site's. Try to finish a direct booking yourself. Whatever breaks is your finding. Not findable, not bookable, or a direct rate that costs the guest more. Ten minutes, and you have half an audit.
