## Job

Give a cold viewer one credible way to investigate a sale-readiness service without pretending the
channel or demand is already proven. Manav opens the conversation, a broker inquiry board defines
and tests the idea, and Manav returns to separate access from demand.

## Viewer movement

- Before: “I understand the service idea, but I do not know whom to ask or what I would learn.”
- After: “I can ask a broker one concrete question, interpret two conditional paths, and remember
  that a referral is access—not evidence that an owner will pay.”

The Short must stand alone. Only after its answer is complete may a separate visual-only card point
toward the full episode.
