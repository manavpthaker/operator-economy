# EP007 Short 01 - Avatar-led phone prototype

## Job

Make the viewer feel the buyer's question before explaining the service. Manav must carry the Short;
the graphic exists only to prove why the pause matters.

## Conversion promise

- Immediate premise: the buyer already likes the numbers.
- Consequential question: what happens if the owner is gone for a month?
- Useful finding: the answer stops before it becomes an operating record.
- Opportunity turn: a business may be hiding inside that gap.
- Full-episode open loop: what business is it?

## Review standard

At phone size, by second 7 the viewer should understand the one-month question. By second 13 the
pause should read as a business dependency, not a failed sale. By second 18 the viewer should know
exactly what EP007 still has to answer.

## Status

This is a no-provider structure test built from accepted EP007 media. It supersedes the 31.5-second
silent graphic prototype but does not replace it on disk. It is not the final original spoken Short.
