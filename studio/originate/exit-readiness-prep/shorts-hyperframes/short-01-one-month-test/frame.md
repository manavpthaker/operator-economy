# Design truth - The Question After the Numbers

## Concept angle

Manav is the host and the primary picture. The Short opens after the buyer has already approved the
numbers, lands the one-month question directly to camera, proves the pause with one causal trace,
then returns to Manav to reveal that a business may be hiding inside the failure.

The useful finding is complete: a profitable company can still fail to demonstrate that work can
continue without the owner. The Short withholds the service model, boundary and economics. Its last
question is the reason to watch EP007.

## Canvas and composition

- Canvas: 1080x1920, portrait, 24 fps, 20.25 seconds.
- Avatar is visible for 16.85 seconds, including the final hold: about 83% of runtime.
- Opening: authored 4:5 crop inside the vertical frame, preserving the face, torso and central hand
  performance. It is not a centered full-height crop.
- Proof: one 3.4-second full-frame trace. Four neutral answer marks travel toward an operating
  record and stop. This is illustrative diagnostic logic, not a sale outcome.
- Close: tight presenter crop, then a matched still of the same accepted performance under the
  unanswered episode question.
- Critical content stays inside x=58-958 and above y=1748. The rightmost 122 pixels and bottom 172
  pixels carry no irreplaceable copy because Shorts controls can cover them.
- Captions are phrase overlays. They do not reserve a permanent lower band or become a dashboard.

## Semantic palette

- Warm paper `#F5F0E6` and `#FBF8F1`: diagnostic field.
- Deep mineral `#173530`: presenter field and final open loop.
- Steel `#586D74`, `#71868D`, `#33464C`: current dependency and neutral trace.
- Oxide `#B5482F` / bright oxide `#FB8B69`: the single active question or interruption.
- Sage `#A4C6B2`: episode identity only, not a success state.
- No risk red. No success check. No valuation or transaction outcome is claimed.

## Type and motion

- BL Boska Bold: final open-loop question.
- BL Zodiak Bold: phrase captions and proof copy.
- BL Supreme: episode identity and disclosures.
- Motion law: trace, interrupt, settle. Hard cuts occur only when the argument changes.
- No ambient drift, pulse, wobble, floating cards, progress bars or report chrome.

## Prototype boundary

This review file uses existing owner-accepted EP007 footage and exact mapped narration so phone
pacing, portrait direction, captions and avatar dominance can be judged without a new provider call.
The final net-new spoken Short still requires a portrait-native avatar take after its exact script is
approved.
