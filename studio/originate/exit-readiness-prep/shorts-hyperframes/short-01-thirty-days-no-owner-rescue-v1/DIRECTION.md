# Direction — Short 01 channel-bridge prototype

Status: **agent-built animatic for owner review; no acceptance inferred**

## Decision

Open as a conversation with Manav: `HOW DO YOU GET A BUSINESS READY TO SELL?` State the business
with authority: an operator can build a sale-readiness practice around helping an owner prepare
before a buyer appears. Keep that recommendation distinct from the unproven demand question. Only
then reveal `30 DAYS. NO OWNER.` as the first practical test. Let one visible mechanism turn three
illustrative `I` statements into a first working dependency map. Hold the completed map and its
evidence boundary before the episode continuation appears.

The channel system is visible but quiet:

- `THE OPERATOR ECONOMY` identifies the recurring channel for a cold viewer.
- `BUSINESS UNDER TEST` names the recurring discovery promise.
- `THE WORK` names the practical transformation.
- Native Related Video placement carries the one continuation after the payoff.

The map applies `WHO ELSE?`, `NEEDS WHAT?`, and `WHAT STOPS?` to every circled dependency. The
shared `ASK ALL 3` hub is explicit so the diagram cannot be read as one question per row.

## Why this form

The rejected structure spent its opening establishing a buyer scene and asked the episode to
supply the practical answer. The first revision overcorrected toward explanation: it named the
practice, but still felt like a diagnostic being administered. The next revision said Manav was
“exploring” the model, which weakened the owner-approved BUILD position. This revision answers the
opening question directly, names the business a viewer can build, and reserves uncertainty for
willingness to pay. The full episode is useful for building out the service, not for repairing an
incomplete Short.

## Strongest alternatives not selected

- A direct episode trailer: rejected because it still makes the Short dependent on the episode.
- A dense all-graphic explainer: rejected because the channel needs Manav as the recurring human
  carrier and the user explicitly asked to lean on the avatar.
- Reusing accepted V5 speaking footage under new words: rejected because the performance and
  narration do not match.
- Buying a new avatar take now: deferred until exact copy and claims are locked and spend is
  explicitly authorized.
