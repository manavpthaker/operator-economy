# Copy draft — structure-test timing track

Status: **prototype discussion copy; not owner-locked**

> How do you help someone get their business ready to sell? You start before they even have a
> buyer. You can build a business around that preparation: a sale-readiness practice. First
> question: could the business run for thirty days without its owner?
>
> Try answering without saying “I.” “I call the customers. I approve the exceptions. I know where
> the records are.” Circle every “I.”
>
> Then ask three things about every circle: who else could act, what would they need, and what stops
> without the owner?
>
> Do that for each one, and you’ve got a first working map of the business’s dependencies.
>
> It isn’t a prediction about the sale. It shows where there’s no handoff yet, so you know where to
> start.
>
> The bigger question is whether an owner would pay someone to clear that map before a buyer
> appears. The full episode shows you how to build the service.

## On-screen identity

- `HOW DO YOU GET A BUSINESS READY TO SELL?` is the cold-viewer invitation.
- `A BUSINESS YOU CAN BUILD`, `A SALE-READINESS PRACTICE`, and `HELP AN OWNER PREPARE BEFORE A
  BUYER APPEARS` state the model with authority without presenting demand as proven.
- `30 DAYS. NO OWNER.` arrives only after the opportunity has context.
- `BUSINESS UNDER TEST` is visible from frame zero.
- `THE WORK` appears when the dependency map becomes usable.
- `ILLUSTRATIVE` stays attached to the three sample `I` statements.
- `FIRST WORKING MAP` names the payoff; the prototype never calls it a validated diagnostic.
- `RELATED VIDEO · TARGET PENDING` is a native-placement placeholder, not a URL.

## Claim boundary

The Short does not claim the business would fail, sell for less, become sellable, receive a buyer,
improve its valuation or produce a successful service. It recommends a business model, proposes a
working diagnostic and keeps willingness to pay as the commercial question.
