# EP007 Short 01 — Thirty Days, No Owner

## Message

This Short opens with an authoritative proposition: you can build a business helping an owner get
ready to sell before a buyer appears. Manav names that business—a sale-readiness practice—then
earns the thirty-day absence test. Repeating the word `I` becomes a first working dependency map.
The useful diagnostic closes inside the Short. The full episode shows how that work becomes a
service while preserving the unresolved commercial question: whether an owner will pay for it
before a buyer appears.

## Audience and destination

- Audience: experienced owners, operators and professionals considering one-person service businesses.
- Destination: YouTube Shorts first; LinkedIn adaptation is a later derivative, not this cut.
- Output: 1080×1920, 24 fps, approximately 43.85 seconds on the scratch timing track.
- Register: curious, upbeat, conversational and practical. No hype, outcome promises or invented
  buyer evidence.
