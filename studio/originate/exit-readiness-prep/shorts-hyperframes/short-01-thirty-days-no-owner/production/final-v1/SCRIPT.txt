How do you help someone get their business ready to sell? You start before they even have a buyer. You can build a business around that preparation: a sale-readiness practice. First question: could the business run for thirty days without its owner?

Try answering without saying “I.” “I call the customers. I approve the exceptions. I know where the records are.” Circle every “I.”

Then ask three things about every circle: who else could act, what would they need, and what stops without the owner?

Do that for each one, and you’ve got a first working map of the business’s dependencies.

It isn’t a prediction about the sale. It shows where there’s no handoff yet, so you know where to start.

The bigger question is whether an owner would pay someone to clear that map before a buyer appears. The full episode shows you how to build the service.
