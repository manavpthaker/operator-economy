# Frame direction - One commercial work order

## Concept

Use the slate's clean-frame grammar without cloning Short 01's worksheet mechanism: host, one evolving
commercial work order, host, then a two-second visual-only full-episode handoff. The legal screen and
scope screen are two states of the same work order. There is no compliance dashboard and no
always-on brand or QA furniture.

The film explains business design with a legal limit. It is not a legal infographic.

## Semantic palette

- Human context: warm paper `#F5F0E6`, raised paper `#FBF8F1`, inset paper `#EDE7D8`.
- Accountable records: deep mineral `#204440`, dark mineral `#173530`.
- One active route or end-card accent: oxide `#B5482F`; bright oxide `#FB8B69` only on mineral.
- Conditional perimeter and secondary structure: steel `#586D74`, dark steel `#33464C`, light steel `#71868D`.
- Derived supporting neutrals retained from the accepted silent frame: paper shadow `#E2DCCD`,
  muted mineral text `#BCC8C9`, and muted paper text `#C4B99E`.
- Sage is absent. Nothing is legally verified.
- Risk red is absent. No sourced violation exists in this Short.

Color never makes the legal claim. The words `MAY APPLY`, `DEPENDING`, `VERIFY`, `THIS ISN'T LEGAL
ADVICE`, and `LOCAL COUNSEL` carry the limit.

- Canvas: 1080x1920 at 24 fps.
- Critical side-safe region: x = 58-958.

- Work order: x = 58-958, y = approximately 110-1380. It retains its geometry across both screen scenes.
- Full-episode handoff: mineral field, one oxide accent, all text inside the same safe region.

1. Host face and spoken opportunity.
2. `fee-route-token` entering the conditional brokerage perimeter.

No scene carries a permanent series title, docket header, footer, status band, or channel slug.

## Caption policy

Captions use exact locked words in phrase-sized windows. The legal screen itself holds the complete
qualifications so those words are not duplicated in a competing caption rail. Final audio-derived
word timings replace every provisional cue before sound-on review.

## Legal and disclosure prohibitions

- No state map, state name, legal/illegal coloring, licence count, gavel, courthouse, scales, seal,
  shield, approval stamp, pass check, or compliance state.
- No claim that fixed-fee wording resolves licensing, brokerage, legal, or ethical questions.
- No generated legal document presented as evidence.
- No static V5 identity plate appears in this sound-on assembly.
- Related-video target status remains private composition metadata until an episode URL is actually bound.
