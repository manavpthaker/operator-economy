## Motion and performance

- Plain hard cuts across the root timeline; the same route and work item retain geometry from
  scenes 2 to 3. No cut-the-curve seam, decorative wipe, crossfade, or persistent framing chrome.
- Scene motion changes state and settles: redirect, route, pause, trace, record, distinguish, bound.
  `OWNER-ONLY?` must retain its question mark. A current-state record is not a success state.
- Final host footage, when separately authorized, needs a matching exact-script portrait-native
  performance. The accepted V5 opening clip says different words and is identity reference only.
- Host delivery is warm and practical. “Operations work” is a clear reframe, not a sales-pitch
  flourish. “Less dependent on one person” is the service target, not a guaranteed client result.
- Dry exact narration is the essential audio. Music and effects are not required by this direction.
  Provisional on-screen phrase guides are not a final caption file or timed subtitles.

## Claim limits

- The owner-only route is a diagnostic question. Never depict the company stopping, a fixed process,
  buyer approval, saleability, higher valuation, a completed engagement, or a transaction outcome.
- AI may organize interview material and draft procedures. It does not conduct the operator's
  interview, choose the decisive missing process, or determine what a buyer will question.
- Valuation, buyer search, and negotiation remain outside the engagement. No legal, tax, licensing,
  pricing, income, demand, or performance claims are added.
- The endcard routes to broader episode depth, not the missing answer to this Short. Actual episode
  URLs remain governed by `studio/originate/exit-readiness-prep/launch/links.json`.
