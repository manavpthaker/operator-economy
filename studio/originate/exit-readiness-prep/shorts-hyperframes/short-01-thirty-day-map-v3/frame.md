`ESTABLISH → TRACE → SETTLE`. Every motion changes the readable relationship. Rings are authored
irregular strokes that stop after landing. Routes extend once. The paper, presenter environment,
caption anchor, source labels, and completed marks remain still. No ambient motion is permitted.

The final distribution boundary adds `ROUTE → SETTLE`: the existing bracket is the sole continuity
carrier across a hard surface cut, lifts once, and stops by 0.34 seconds. All copy stops by 0.46
seconds. The remaining hold is motionless reading time. End-card critical copy stays left of x=900
and above y=1620, leaving the right control rail and bottom platform region clear.
