Status: **exact clean-frame V4 render owner accepted; release, upload, and publication remain pending**

## Decision

Build the first locked Short as a conversation that hands the diagnostic to the working sheets.
The generated V5 presenter names the service before the buyer during the opening seven seconds.
At the first practical test, cut to a full-screen question plate, then remain in the full-screen
paper world while the three owner-held answers are circled and turned into one dependency map.
The presenter returns beside the settled map only when the Short shifts from method to judgment:
transferring or documenting knowledge, decisions, and relationships that still have no handoff.

Keep the video edge-to-edge rather than inside a continuous channel frame. Remove the persistent
top identity/short-number rail and bottom qualification/episode rail. The opening names the
opportunity, the working sheets label the steps as they become useful, and the brief end card
carries the full-episode route. Nothing needs to remain on screen merely to brand the piece.

After that complete answer and its protected hold, carry the existing oxide bracket into a
two-second, visual-only full-episode handoff. The card must add a clear distribution route without
turning the standalone lesson into a cliffhanger or changing a word of locked narration.

This restores the stronger host → screens → host rhythm of the earlier preview while retaining the
newer authoritative script and approved episode route. Context still arrives before mechanism, so
the viewer never enters on an unexplained `thirty days` instruction. The new cut uses the existing
7.000, 13.250, 21.500, and 32.583333-second boundaries; narration, captions and audio are unchanged.

## Strongest alternatives not selected

- Open directly on `Thirty days. No owner.`: rejected because it recreates the cold-viewer context
  failure the owner already identified.
- Keep the old willingness-to-pay close: rejected because it is not in the locked script and makes
  the Short end on a different promise.
- End on the standalone payoff with no route: rejected after owner review because it completes the
  lesson but leaves the discovery-to-episode handoff implicit.
- Burn an episode URL or fake button into picture: rejected because the canonical URL is not yet
  authorized and YouTube's native Related Video should carry the eventual click path.
- Keep Manav visible through the question and circling operation: rejected after owner review
  because the screens read as overlays and the earlier preview's decisive picture handoff was
  clearer.
- Remove Manav entirely: rejected because the channel discovery path still needs him to open the
  conversation and return for the judgment.
- Reuse V5 speaking footage under the new narration: rejected because the mouth movement and words
  do not match.
