## Intent

Make a portrait-native companion film that works for someone who has never seen EP007. Manav opens
the conversation by reframing work that sounds like M&A as an operations service. One causal model
then shows why a buyer may inspect customer service, decisions and records when the owner steps
away; how an operator traces and documents the dependency; and where AI support stops. Manav
returns to land the qualification and the engagement boundary.

The delivery is curious, upbeat and authoritative. It borrows only the high-level virtues of a
strong conversational explainer—quick orientation, visible causality and forward momentum. It does
not imitate Cleo Abram's persona, likeness, voice, cadence, wardrobe, graphics, typography or music.
