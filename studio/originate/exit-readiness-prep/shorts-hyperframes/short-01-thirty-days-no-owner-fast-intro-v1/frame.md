# Visual direction — Business Under Test

## Frame idea

The vertical frame behaves like an operator's annotated working sheet, not a resized episode or a
stack of social cards. Manav's accepted identity establishes who is doing the work. The sheet then
turns three owner-held statements into a dependency map and returns to him for the commercial
question.

## Boundary Ledger bindings

- Human context / paper: `#f5f0e6`, raised paper `#fbf8f1`, inset paper `#ede7d8`.
- Accountable frame / mineral: `#204440`, deep mineral `#173530`.
- Active commitment only / oxide: `#b5482f`, dark oxide `#87341f`, bright oxide `#fb8b69`.
- External dependency / steel: `#586d74`, dark steel `#33464c`, light steel `#71868d`.
- Ink: `#19201f`, soft ink `#303b39`, muted ink `#566461`.
- Sage is not used: nothing in this prototype has been verified as a live operating state.
- Risk red is not used: the diagnostic exposes a dependency but does not establish failure.

## Typography

- Singular editorial idea: BL Boska 700.
- Working labels and the commercial question: BL Zodiak 700.
- Captions, evidence labels and operator instructions: BL Supreme 400/500.
- No generic display fonts, gradient text, pill-card grid or logo pre-roll.

## Portrait composition

- Canvas: 1080×1920 at 24 fps.
- Critical side-safe area: x = 58–958.
- Critical bottom limit: y = 1748; platform controls remain clear below it.
- Caption band: y = 1418–1738, with 62–68 px Supreme and no more than three short lines.
- The first frame contains Manav and `HOW DO YOU GET A BUSINESS READY TO SELL?`; the series label
  cannot delay the conversation.
- `A BUSINESS YOU CAN BUILD`, `A SALE-READINESS PRACTICE`, and `HELP AN OWNER PREPARE BEFORE A BUYER
  APPEARS` establish authority without claiming demand is proven.
- `30 DAYS. NO OWNER.` is an earned test reveal after the opportunity is legible, not unexplained
  opening jargon.
- The accepted V5 still is a labeled animatic identity plate, never represented as matching speech or lip-sync.
- One focal operation at a time: oxide circles the active `I`; steel carries every circled dependency
  through the shared `ASK ALL 3` hub to all three handoff questions.

## Motion and edit

Rhythm: **invite — orient — reveal — inspect — build — settle — open**.

Hard cuts happen only when the argument changes. Within the diagnostic, statements stamp in,
oxide circles draw around the repeated `I`, and steel routes extend to the handoff questions. Motion
must reveal causality, then stop long enough to read. No ambient motion is added merely to keep the
frame busy.

## Prototype disclosure

`STRUCTURE TEST · SCRATCH VO · FINAL PORTRAIT PERFORMANCE PENDING` remains readable in the upper
status line. It is production metadata, not channel-facing final copy.
