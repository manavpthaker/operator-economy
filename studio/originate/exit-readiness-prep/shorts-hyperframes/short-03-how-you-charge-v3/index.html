You can build a service helping owners prepare a business for sale.

But how you charge changes what job you’re doing.

A fee tied to the sale

Keep the offer focused on preparation:

map owner dependencies,

organize records,

document operations,

and deliver a readiness plan.

One bounded model is a fixed fee

for that defined work,

independent of whether a sale closes.

Fixed-fee wording doesn’t settle every legal question,

but it gives you a clear scope

to take to local counsel.
