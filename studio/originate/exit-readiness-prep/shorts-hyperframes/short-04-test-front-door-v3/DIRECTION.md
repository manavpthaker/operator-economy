# Direction — Short 04 V3 clean-frame revision

Status: **implemented as a silent local structure candidate; exact script remains locked; final Studio review, narration, presenter performance, captions, and render remain pending**

## Decision

Use the accepted EP007 clean-frame grammar without cloning Short 01's worksheet mechanism:

1. Full-bleed host opens the conversation.
2. A full-screen broker inquiry board defines the service, holds the exact broker question, reveals
   one conditional answer at a time, then reduces the conversation to two bounded outputs.
3. Full-bleed host returns for the correction: referral proves access, not demand; owner payment is
   the first demand signal.
4. A separate two-second visual-only card routes toward the full episode after the standalone payoff.

There is no persistent top rail, footer, episode label, QA text, or subtitle slab. The broker board
is specific to this Short: one interview question passes through conditional states. It is not the
circle-and-map worksheet used in Short 01 and never becomes a funnel.

## Viewer movement

1. Opportunity: this is a service an experienced operator could investigate.
2. Definition: the work is documenting operations, cleaning records, and mapping owner dependence.
3. Action: ask a broker what happens to a business that is not ready to list.
4. Interpretation: either the broker already absorbs the work, or the answer creates two follow-up
   questions.
5. Payoff: the conversation can surface readiness problems and possible owner access.
6. Boundary: access is not demand; an owner agreeing to pay begins the demand test.

The Short must complete that movement for a cold viewer before the episode handoff appears.

## Conditional logic contract

- `IF broker already does the preparation free` → `referral channel may be blocked`.
- `IF broker turns the business away or refers it elsewhere` → ask `what was missing?` and `who
  handles it now?`.
- Either answer may expose readiness problems; the second may expose possible owner access.
- `REFERRAL = ACCESS`.
- `ACCESS ≠ DEMAND`.
- `OWNER AGREES TO PAY` is the first demand signal, not market validation.

Both branch labels remain visibly conditional. Do not add frequency, volume, leads, conversion,
validation, a seller outcome, or a broker-to-owner-to-sale path.

## Motion and seam direction

- Hard cuts carry each change in argument.
- The broker question is the semantic carrier across Frames 3–5.
- The middle board reveals one state at a time on a top-to-bottom route.
- Oxide marks the current question or condition; completed routes settle to steel.
- The host conclusion uses phrase-sized type over the lower torso, not a card covering the person.
- No ambient drift, looping route, camera float, glow, pulse, particle, zoom punch, or decorative
  transition is present.
- All timings remain provisional until exact narration and word timings exist.
