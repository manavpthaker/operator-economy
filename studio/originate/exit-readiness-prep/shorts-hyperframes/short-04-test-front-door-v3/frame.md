# Visual direction — A broker question becomes a testable path

## Frame idea

Manav opens a practical conversation. The picture then yields to one broker inquiry record: define
the service work, ask the question, follow two conditional answers, and reduce the interview to the
two things it can honestly provide. Manav returns for the access-versus-demand correction. A separate
two-second card routes to the full episode only after the standalone answer lands.

## Portrait composition

- Canvas: 1080×1920 at 24 fps.
- Critical side-safe: x = 58–958; decisive copy stays left of the platform control rail.
- Critical bottom limit: y = 1748.
- Host scenes are full-bleed with no permanent banner, footer, identity rail, or framed media card.
- Screen scenes own the full canvas. They do not retain the host as picture-in-picture.
- The host conclusion uses phrase-sized lower-torso cues, not a solid card covering the person.
- Verbatim captions are absent from this silent candidate and must be timed from final narration.

## Broker inquiry geometry

The screen sequence is a top-to-bottom working record:

1. `THE PREPARATION IN BETWEEN` with the three proposed service actions.
2. The exact broker question, held as the persistent record.
3. `IF THEY ALREADY DO THAT WORK FOR FREE` → `REFERRAL CHANNEL MAY BE BLOCKED`.
4. `IF THEY TURN IT AWAY OR REFER IT ELSEWHERE` → `WHAT WAS MISSING?` and `WHO HANDLES IT NOW?`.
5. `READINESS PROBLEMS` + `POSSIBLE OWNER ACCESS`.

The branches are vertically sequenced, not compared as a dense side-by-side grid. The exact question
remains visible when the answers appear.

## Boundary Ledger application

- Warm paper holds the proposed service and interview model.
- Deep mineral holds the host environment and accountable end-card surface; it does not signify
  proof.
- Steel carries possible routes and completed conditions.
- Oxide marks the current question, condition, or evidence correction.
- Sage and risk red remain absent because no channel, demand state, or failure is verified.
- Roughness is not added. The relationship is carried by typeset records and exact geometry.

## Motion law

`ESTABLISH → TRACE → SETTLE`.

The service actions establish in order. The broker question holds. Each conditional branch becomes
active once, then settles before the next one appears. The output screen reduces the same record
without introducing a new mechanism. The host correction moves through three evidence phrases and
then holds. Hard cuts are the default seam; there is no decorative transition.
