## Shared limits

- Preparation is not claimed to cause a sale, raise valuation, or make a business sellable.
- A working dependency map does not prove failure or predict buyer behavior.
- AI may organize interview material and draft documentation; it does not replace the operator's
  interview or judgment about which gap matters.
- A fixed fee is one bounded commercial model, not automatic legal safety.
- Broker behavior, referral access, owner demand, pricing, and delivery economics remain unproven.
- No visual may imply that Manav has delivered this service to a client or represented a seller in
  a transaction.
