# EP007 standalone companion storyboard v2

Status: **direction proposal; V2 exact copy and timing not locked**

The V1 storyboard visualized episode premises. V2 visualizes complete mini-stories. Every cut must
make sense after hiding the episode cue, title, description, post and first comment.

## Shared performance contract

- Fresh portrait-native direct address using the accepted EP007 V5 Manav identity and look.
- Manav is visible in the first frame, for the interpretive turn and for the final narrow payoff.
  Designed evidence may carry the causal middle. The episode cue follows the payoff rather than
  replacing it.
- The spoken tension lands inside three seconds. The title sits behind the presenter during that
  opening beat.
- Split any avatar passage longer than about twelve seconds into shorter takes before lip sync.
- Use one concrete mechanism per cut: dependency map, spreadsheet-to-two-tests or
  agreement-to-deferral. No generic explainer montage.
- Designed proof beats use `silent_graphic`. They declare a before state, one operation, an after
  state and a settle. Motion changes meaning.
- Captions are verbatim, burned in and phrase-grouped. The same words never appear simultaneously
  as both captions and focal embedded type.
- Critical content stays out of platform-control zones. Phone-scale Studio review decides
  legibility.
- YouTube may add a Related Video cue only after the useful payoff lands and only when the
  canonical episode target exists. LinkedIn has no spoken episode CTA.

| 02 — The answer keeps returning | 5–14s | Three **ILLUSTRATIVE** answer fragments appear beside Manav: `I CALL`, `I APPROVE`, `I KNOW WHERE`. Each routes back to one owner node. | `presenter_address` plus typeset examples / route | Makes dependency visible without inventing a buyer case. |
| 03 — Build the map | 14–28s | The three fragments move into a compact record with `WHO ELSE CAN ACT`, `WHAT WOULD THEY NEED`, `WHAT STOPS`. Empty fields remain visibly empty. `FIRST WORKING MAP` stays pinned. | `silent_graphic` / transfer → pin | Delivers a proposed exercise the viewer can use without presenting it as a validated diagnostic. |
| 04 — What the map shows | 28–37s | Manav returns beside the working dependency map. `MAP, NOT SALE PREDICTION` settles once. | `presenter_address` / correct → settle | Closes the narrow loop and states the evidence limit. |

Mute review: a muted viewer must understand that the owner-dependent statements are illustrative,
move into a testable map and do not prove a sale outcome.

| 02 — Modeled route | 5–17s | Eight modeled engagement tokens route through `$96K GROSS` and `$10K MODELED COSTS` to `$86K BEFORE OWNER COMP + TAX`. `MODELED, NOT OBSERVED` remains pinned. | `silent_graphic` / route → pin | Shows the arithmetic and status once. |

| 04 — Two observations | 27–39s | Manav returns. A stopwatch attaches to one engagement's interviews, cleanup, writing and follow-up. A separate real-offer card attaches to one price response. | `presenter_address` / split → correct | Delivers the complete decision rule: observe one delivery cycle and one real price response separately. |
| 05Y — What else ends it | 39–47s | The two experiments settle inside a larger offer boundary. Unopened channel and deferral gates remain visible behind the Related Video cue. | `presenter_address` / open → settle | Opens the larger episode system without withholding the first answer. |

Mute review: every figure stays visibly modeled. `$86K` never reads as profit, salary, take-home pay

routes into a demand result.

## Isolation and phone review

Before owner lock, review each cut twice:

1. Hide the final EP007 sentence, Related Video cue, description, Page post and supporting comment.
   The remaining cut must still establish the situation, mechanism, consequence and action.
2. Play the cut at phone scale with sound off. The first frame must carry the tension, the causal
   operation must remain legible and the payoff must be visible before any episode cue.

If either review fails, revise the story. A stronger CTA cannot rescue it.
