## Pilot 01: The One-Month Question

### YouTube Short

**Spoken copy**

Here's the one question that can turn a good-looking P and L into an uncomfortable silence. What
happens if you're gone for a month? In EP007's illustrative opening, the owner gets about four words
into the answer and stops. That pause does not prove the business fails. It shows that operating
independence has not been demonstrated. The hidden question is whether a buyer is getting a
business, or a job only the owner knows how to do. EP007 builds a service around making that answer
inspectable. Then the model hits a number the research could not supply. The full episode is linked
below.

**Useful payoff:** The stopped answer establishes a narrower, defensible finding: operating
independence has not been demonstrated.

**Episode gap:** What the fixed-fee preparation service is, what it may honestly sell and which
unmeasured capacity input can stop it.

## Pilot 02: The Model Misses Its Own Target

### YouTube Short

**Spoken copy**

I built a model for a one-person service, then refused to make the spreadsheet pass. Eight modeled
engagements at twelve thousand dollars produce ninety-six thousand gross. Subtract ten thousand in
modeled costs and eighty-six remains before owner compensation and tax. My target was one hundred
twenty thousand. The base case misses it. This is a modeled scenario, not observed performance or
an earnings forecast. That does not kill the idea. It changes the next test from a spreadsheet to a
stopwatch, because delivery hours are still unknown. EP007 shows what to time and the two answers
that could stop the build. The full episode is linked below.

**Useful payoff:** Under its declared assumptions, the base case misses the host's modeled
owner-compensation target.

**Episode gap:** What the first diagnostic must time, plus the separate demand and channel answers
that can stop the build.

**Related Video close:** `The full episode is linked below.` Mount only after the canonical Related
Video gate passes.

**Pinned comment draft:** This is a modeled scenario, not observed performance or an earnings

EP007 shows what the first diagnostic should measure.

## Pilot 03: The Empty Seat

### YouTube Short

**Spoken copy**

I think there is a business hiding three years before a business sale. At and around the deal, the
broker, accountant, lawyer and wealth adviser each have a clear role. That timing can be rational:
an owner may not want to pay for a sale that never happens. Now rewind three years. No buyer. No
deadline. No transaction. But the preparation work already exists, and no transaction is funding
it. That is the empty seat. It might support a fixed-fee practice. Demand is not proven. EP007 builds
the offer, then names the two answers that could stop it. The full episode is linked below.

**Useful payoff:** The episode's incentive-structure model leaves an earlier preparation role
without a clear transaction-funded owner.

**Episode gap:** What occupies the role, what the bounded offer excludes and which two local
answers should kill the build.

**Related Video close:** `The full episode is linked below.` Mount only after the canonical Related
Video gate passes.

**Pinned comment draft:** The empty seat is the episode's incentive-structure thesis, not a market
survey or proof of demand. EP007 builds the bounded fixed-fee offer and the tests that can end it.

### LinkedIn Page video

**Spoken copy**

The possible opportunity appears before the transaction. In the episode's incentive-structure
model, brokers are paid around a closing, accountants and lawyers work at the deal, and wealth
advisers have proceeds to manage afterward. That structure can be rational. Three years earlier, the
preparation work can already exist, but there is no transaction funding it. The gap may support a
fixed-fee service that documents operations and owner dependencies before brokerage starts. It is
not proof of demand. The first test is whether an owner will pay before a deadline exists.
Agreement followed by deferral is not evidence of demand.
