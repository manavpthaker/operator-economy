# EP007 net-new Shorts: standalone discovery-film contract

## Editorial promise

Each Short starts a conversation with a cold viewer about a service they could build: helping an
owner prepare a business for sale before a buyer appears. It asks one narrow question, explains one
useful mechanism and reaches a complete bounded conclusion or action. The full episode may add the
complete offer, adjacent constraints, economics or falsifiers. It never supplies the answer the
Short promised.

These are original companion films, not excerpts or summaries:

- fresh spoken copy;
- a portrait-first causal visual mechanism;
- one real payoff inside the Short;
- optional episode depth after the complete native answer;
- no landscape episode frame used as the base canvas;
- no imitation of another creator's persona, delivery, graphics, typography, music or likeness.

Accepted episode material may appear only as a clearly attributed evidence inset when the script
actually requires it. It may not substitute for a new 9:16 composition.

## Story architecture

1. **Cold-viewer entry.** In the first sentence, name the sale-readiness opportunity, the owner as
   customer and the viewer's service-building perspective. Context is a sharp conversational entry,
   not a greeting, logo pre-roll or slow episode recap.
2. **Causal reveal.** Give every new causal idea a new visual state. A change should reveal state,
   mechanism, proof or consequence, never energy for its own sake.
3. **Useful payoff.** Resolve the exact narrow promise made by the hook with a diagnostic, completed
   causal reveal, operator decision or test the viewer can use.
4. **Isolation check.** Remove the EP007 sentence, Related Video cue, description, post and first
   comment. The Short must still establish its situation, mechanism, consequence and action.
5. **Optional depth.** The spoken story may end on its conclusion. If an episode bridge is used, it
   comes after the payoff and may name broader system design, adjacent constraints, economics or
   falsifiers. It cannot supply context, mechanism or the promised conclusion.
6. **Native continuation.** On YouTube, Related Video may be attached after the completed story. A
   spoken episode CTA is optional, never the climax and never required for coherence.

For LinkedIn, omit the spoken episode continuation and end on the complete professional consequence.
The institutional Page video must pass the isolation check by itself; an accompanying post or bound
first comment may route to EP007 after the native value is complete. Do not carry YouTube language
such as “tap the Related Video” into LinkedIn.

First person is evidence of access, not a default register. Use it only for something the host
actually built, tested, observed or reported. An illustrative scene must be labelled. Generated or
constructed material is never documentary evidence.

## Format

- Canvas: 1080x1920 at 24 fps.
- Discussion target: 30 to 75 seconds after real voice timing. Runtime follows the story, not a
  mechanical cut quota.
- First two seconds: the subject is already visible and moving.
- Pacing: dense sections usually introduce a new visual state every 1.5 to 3 seconds, with authored
  holds for the pause, contradiction and missing number.
- Captions: verbatim overlay rail timed from the locked original narration. Do not reserve a
  permanent lower band and do not duplicate identical focal text.
- Embedded words: one dominant phrase per causal beat. Captions carry the full sentence.
- Close: land the bounded conclusion. Any episode bridge follows it and can be removed cleanly.

## Evidence boundaries

- Preparation is not claimed to cause a sale or increase value.
- Demand, delivery hours and viable pricing remain unproven.
- AI may compress documentation; it does not supply judgment or reduce owner-interview time.
- The checklist is a proposed acceptance mechanism, not legal compliance or buyer approval.
- Broker referrals are a testable unknown, not established channel behavior.
- No typical fee, income promise, valuation opinion, state-specific licensing statement or implied
  host transaction experience.
- Generated or constructed imagery, if authorized, is labelled illustrative and never presented as
  evidence.
