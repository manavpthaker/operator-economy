Helping owners get a business ready to sell sounds like M&A. But the service before the deal is really operations work.

A buyer needs to know whether customers get served, decisions get made, and records can be found when the owner steps away.

An experienced operator can trace those dependencies and document how the business actually runs.

AI can organize interviews and draft procedures. It can’t decide which missing process a buyer will question.

So the core qualification isn’t deal experience. It’s knowing how to make a company less dependent on one person.

Valuation, buyer search, and negotiation stay outside the engagement.
