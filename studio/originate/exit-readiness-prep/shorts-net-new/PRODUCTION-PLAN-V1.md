## Shared directing contract

- Mode: `presenter_address` for the opening, reasoning, and conclusion; brief `silent_graphic`
  support only when spatial causality helps comprehension.
- Avatar target: visible for roughly 60–75% of each final cut and present in the first frame.
- Performance: Manav speaks to one capable peer—curious, optimistic, certain about the method, and
  precise about what remains unknown. No named-creator imitation.
- Picture: one vertical relationship at a time. Graphics enter after the topic is clear, perform one
  trace/route/correct operation, then settle.
- Captions: verbatim phrase overlays from the final word timings. Focal thesis type may replace, but
  never duplicate, the corresponding caption phrase.
- Edit grammar: mostly hard cuts; at most one continuity seam per Short when a persistent object
  genuinely crosses between presenter and model.
- Sound: dry voice first. Music and SFX are optional later decisions, not prerequisites for energy.

## Phone composition
