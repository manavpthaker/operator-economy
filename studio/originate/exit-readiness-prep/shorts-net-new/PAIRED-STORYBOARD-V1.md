# EP007 paired pilot storyboard v1

Status: **direction proposal; exact copy and timing not locked**

This video system tells prospective one-person operators that an attractive service idea is only
useful when its dependency, economics and demand tests remain inspectable. Each premise uses one
shared causal picture core plus a distinct YouTube and LinkedIn spoken wrapper.

## Shared performance contract

- Fresh portrait-native direct address using the accepted EP007 V5 Manav identity and look.
- Host visible at the hook, the main interpretation and the platform-specific close. Target 55 to
  70 percent of final runtime after real narration timing.
- Presenter mode is `presenter_address`: exact approved audio, lens or deliberately adjacent
  eyeline, synchronized visible speech, hands mostly at rest, one phrase-earned gesture.
- Designed proof beats use `silent_graphic`. They declare a before state, one operation, an after
  state and a settle. No generic activity or duplicated full narration.
- Pilot 01's stopped-answer picture is explicitly **ILLUSTRATIVE**. It is a designed trace, not
  documentary buyer footage or a generated reenactment presented as evidence.
- Default transition is a cut. One persistent object may carry across a cut only when identity,
  direction and function remain unchanged.
- Captions use the Boundary Ledger `rail` state. At most one earned phrase per beat uses `embed`;
  the same words never appear simultaneously in both.
- Critical content avoids the right-side and bottom platform-control zones. Final safety is judged
  on a phone-sized Studio preview, not inferred from coordinates alone.

## Pilot 01: The One-Month Question

Rhythm: **direct question → protected pause → causal reveal → presenter consequence → platform close**

| Frame | Approx. beat | On screen | Mode / operation | Why |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| 01 — The question | 0–7s | Portrait-native Manav begins mid-thought and asks the one-month question directly to camera. No logo pre-roll. | `presenter_address` / establish | The host earns attention with the precise continuity test rather than setup. |
| 02 — The answer stops | 7–15s | On warm paper, an **ILLUSTRATIVE** owner-held answer trace begins, reaches “about four words” as a non-numeric approximate cue, then stops before an operating record. Hold the stop. | `silent_graphic` / trace → interrupt → settle | Makes the pause inspectable without fabricating a buyer outcome. |
| 03 — Business or job | 15–27s | Manav returns. A compact owner-held job mark and transferable operating record sit beside him; only the current comparison changes. | `presenter_address` plus typeset labels / correct | States the narrower inference and prevents the pause from reading as a failed sale. |
| 04Y — Missing number | 27–39s | A covered delivery-hour field appears. Manav names the larger episode gap; EP007 Related Video cue settles only when the canonical route exists. | `presenter_address` / pin → settle | Gives YouTube a consequential unanswered test and a native next action. |
| 04L — Inspectable dependency | 27–39s | The routines, decisions, relationships and records attach to one operating record. Manav lands the complete LinkedIn consequence. | `presenter_address` / pin → settle | Leaves the professional viewer with a usable action even without clicking. |

Mute review: Frame 02 may show no readable human speech. A muted viewer should understand that an
illustrative answer path stops before becoming an operating record, without inferring a failed
sale.

## Pilot 02: The Model Misses Its Own Target

Rhythm: **builder decision → arithmetic route → miss and hold → stopwatch correction → platform close**

| Frame | Approx. beat | On screen | Mode / operation | Why |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| 01 — Refuse the pass | 0–6s | Manav says he built the model and refused to tune it until it passed. | `presenter_address` / establish | The builder's decision is the hook, not an inventory of numbers. |
| 02 — Declared base case | 6–19s | Eight modeled engagement objects route into `$96K GROSS`; a pinned receipt labels every value modeled. `$10K MODELED COSTS` leaves `$86K BEFORE OWNER COMP + TAX`. | `silent_graphic` / route → pin | Shows the arithmetic once, with evidence status attached to the exact values. |
| 03 — Miss | 19–27s | `$86K` stops below `MY MODELED TARGET: $120K`. No risk red, failure icon or success sound. Reading hold. | `silent_graphic` / interrupt → settle | Shows a target miss without upgrading it into a failed category or business. |
| 04 — Stopwatch | 27–35s | Manav returns. The spreadsheet settles; one stopwatch attaches to interview, cleanup, writing and follow-up. | `presenter_address` / correct | Changes the next test from assumption tuning to capacity measurement. |
| 05Y — Two more tests | 35–44s | Demand and channel remain two still, unopened gates. Related Video cue appears only after the spoken caveat and canonical route check. | `presenter_address` / establish → settle | YouTube receives a real gap that EP007 answers without saying hours control demand. |
| 05L — Separate unknowns | 35–44s | Capacity, willingness to pay and demand settle as three separate fields. | `presenter_address` / settle | LinkedIn gets the complete operating distinction. |

Mute review: the Working Model must show only a modeled route and chosen target. It must not look
like observed revenue, verified demand, salary or take-home pay.

## Pilot 03: The Empty Seat

Rhythm: **time-gap hook → fair current route → rewind → unresolved seat → platform close**

| Frame | Approx. beat | On screen | Mode / operation | Why |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| 01 — Three years earlier | 0–6s | Manav opens on the claim that a possible business sits three years before a sale. | `presenter_address` / establish | Makes the time gap, not professional blame, the premise. |
| 02 — Current transaction route | 6–18s | Broker, accountant, lawyer and wealth-adviser role records enter around a transaction. A short note keeps the route fair: `RATIONAL WHEN A SALE MAY NEVER HAPPEN`. | `silent_graphic` / route → settle | Represents the current incentive structure before proposing an alternative. |
| 03 — Rewind | 18–29s | The timeline rewinds three years. Buyer, deadline and transaction leave; the same preparation object remains beside one unowned seat. | `silent_graphic` / trace → interrupt → settle | Exposes the timing vacancy without claiming nobody performs the work. |
| 04Y — Possible practice | 29–41s | Manav qualifies the fixed-fee possibility and unproven demand. Two unopened kill-condition gates settle behind the Related Video cue. | `presenter_address` / establish → settle | Gives YouTube an honest opportunity plus a consequential falsification gap. |
| 04L — First demand test | 29–41s | Manav lands the pre-deadline willingness-to-pay test; `AGREEMENT + DEFERRAL ≠ DEMAND EVIDENCE` becomes the single embedded thesis. | `presenter_address` / correct → settle | Gives LinkedIn a complete operator test without depending on the episode link. |

Mute review: the role map must read as an incentive model, not a compensation survey or accusation.
The empty seat means “no clear transaction-funded owner in this thesis,” not “nobody does this.”
