The model stays rough.

Icons and accents borrow the working paper’s authored marks without turning the whole interface into a sketch. They explain actors, routes, evidence, tests, and decisions. They never decorate a claim into credibility.

Status: design-language draft. The locked EP006 illustration remains the visual reference.

Small marks

Recognizable at 24 pixels. Rough when enlarged.

Each icon uses a primary steel contour, a lighter construction pass, and oxide only when the icon itself represents an active decision or owned route.

Editorial accents

A relationship, not a spot illustration.

Large accents use the same actors and paths as a Working Model but carry no episode facts, handwriting, or evidence claims. They can support a hero, chapter opening, empty state, or methodology explanation.

Scale

One drawing language, three levels of detail.

At 24px, retain only the silhouette. At 32px, construction strokes may remain. Above 72px, the doubled contour becomes visibly authored. Never shrink an editorial accent into an icon.

Use and rejection

Roughness stays inside the model layer.

UI labels, evidence, sources, status, and controls remain typeset. Reject uniform wobble, closed geometric perfection, fake notebook props, decorative oxide, generic lightbulbs, rockets, brains, handshakes, and icons whose meaning depends on handwritten text.

AccessibilityDecorative accents use empty alt text. Meaningful illustrations receive relationship-based alt text. Icons paired with visible labels are hidden from assistive technology; icon-only controls require an accessible name.
