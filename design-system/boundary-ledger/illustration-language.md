# Boundary Ledger working-model illustration language

## Purpose

Each episode illustration is a working model of a business relationship. It exposes a dependency,
leak, exchange, or return path before the publication treats the answer as finished.

The static model is not automatically a cinematic environment, mascot, icon set, blueprint
background, or decorative sketch. In motion, its actors, routes, corrections, and evidence anchors
may become a causal System World scene when they are authored as persistent layers and every change
is tied to the argument.

## Required static composition

- Build at **3:2**. Preserve the complete composition at every standard display size.
- Establish one central relationship that reads without relying on every handwritten note.
- Use recognizable objects or actors rather than abstract nodes when the episode provides them.
- Declare one thesis-bearing movement, decision, or exception in core oxide.
- Keep meaningful marks away from the outer safe margin used for docket attachment.
- Design the composition so the central model survives at roughly 10% of source size.
- Provide meaningful alt text describing the relationship, not the drawing style alone.

## Mark-making

The sketch should feel authored while thinking:

- Lines overshoot, stop short, double back, and vary in pressure.
- Corners sometimes remain open.
- Parallel strokes remain visibly imperfect.
- Construction strokes, abandoned attempts, and small corrections remain visible.
- Hatching varies in density and direction.
- Handwriting changes spacing and baseline naturally.
- Objects stay discernible even when lines fail to meet.

Roughness must come from the drawing, not from a uniform wobble filter applied to clean vector art.

## Color behavior

- Mineral and steel carry the majority of lines and handwritten notes.
- Paper remains warm and quiet. Texture may be subtle but cannot become a stationery effect.
- Oxide marks **one** thesis-bearing movement or decision.
- Risk color is reserved for contradictions or actual negative exceptions.
- Do not introduce a second competing accent path.

## Information hierarchy

1. The central business relationship.
2. The oxide path that changes the model.
3. The actors, objects, or transaction points.
4. Supporting notes and calculations.

Small calculations may become texture on a phone. The central relationship and oxide path may not.
If they fail at mobile scale, make a separate mobile drawing. Do not crop the desktop composition.

## Surface bindings

### Web and static editorial

- The illustration is foreground content, never a page background.
- The accountable docket may attach to the blank top margin by exactly **16px**.
- No thesis-bearing mark may sit beneath the docket.
- Desktop shows the complete working paper across the content columns.
- Mobile keeps the docket within 24px gutters and lets the complete paper bleed to the viewport edge.
- Do not repeat blueprint grids, leader lines, crosshairs, or sketch labels in surrounding UI.

### Designed scenes and motion graphics

- Preserve the locked still unchanged. Create a motion-ready derivative with persistent object IDs,
  separate steel and oxide routes, evidence anchors, and authored layers or masks.
- Use [`../boundary-ledger/motion-ready-asset.schema.json`](./motion-ready-asset.schema.json) for the
  derivative. A flattened JPEG cannot prove rough-stroke or object-level motion.
- Motion reveals a causal operation and then settles. It does not animate every rough mark.
- Use authored irregular stroke groups, correction passes, or hand-drawn frame sequences. Never add
  uniform jitter or a wobble filter to clean vector art.
- Evidence, captions, labels, and metadata remain typeset and attributable.

### Aspect-ratio adaptation

- Treat 3:2 as the canonical static reference, not a mandatory video viewport.
- Recompose 16:9, 9:16, and 1:1 around the same actors, current route, owned path, and consequence.
- Do not blind-crop, letterbox a portrait card inside landscape, or shrink the static master until the
  causal relationship becomes texture.
- A format may be declared unsupported when a credible recomposition does not exist.

## Reject when

- The image could be mistaken for vector iconography or a polished architectural rendering.
- Every corner closes and every stroke has the same weight.
- A texture or wobble filter is the only source of irregularity.
- Decorative notes overwhelm the central relationship.
- Multiple colored paths compete for attention.
- Cropping the oxide path would not change the meaning.
- Handwritten labels are required to identify the basic model.
- The drawing uses coffee stains, torn edges, tape, notebook spirals, or scrapbook props.
- The illustration depicts generic business activity instead of the episode's actual relationship.

## Episode handoff checklist

- [ ] The episode thesis is stated in one sentence.
- [ ] The owned actor, rented dependency, and exchange are identified.
- [ ] One oxide decision or movement is declared.
- [ ] The 3:2 composition reads without cropping.
- [ ] Lines are materially rough, not merely filtered.
- [ ] The central model survives at phone width.
- [ ] The top attachment margin contains no thesis-bearing marks.
- [ ] Alt text describes what the model shows.
- [ ] Source and evidence claims remain in the docket or editorial copy, not implied by the image.
- [ ] A human reviews the image for subject accuracy and brand drift.

For a motion derivative also confirm:

- [ ] The locked still remains unchanged and hash-identical.
- [ ] Persistent actors, objects, routes, and evidence anchors have stable IDs.
- [ ] Each layer or mask has dimensions, media type, and SHA-256 provenance.
- [ ] Roughness is authored into the motion asset rather than simulated after the fact.
- [ ] Every aspect ratio is an authored recomposition rather than a crop.
