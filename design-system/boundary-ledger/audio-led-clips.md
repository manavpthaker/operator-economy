# Boundary Ledger audio-led clips

Status: canonical direction, provisional implementation binding.

> **The voice sets the clock. Sound and motion mark the argument, not the beat.**

Audio-first is the system’s honesty test because color and page geometry cannot carry it. If the
meaning disappears when the screen is removed, the semantic sound binding has failed. If the spoken
argument depends on the screen, the editorial contract has failed.

## Valid modes

### Model-led

The Working Model changes only when the spoken clause changes the relationship. Use for causal
explanation, routing, ownership, costs, gates, and outcomes.

### Text-led

The caption rail carries the verbatim voice while one genuine thesis, reversal, warning, number, or
quote becomes scarce kinetic type. Use clause or line reveals, never per-syllable bounce.

### Voice trace

A time-faithful utterance trace may provide a temporal index. It is derived from the actual PCM,
advances only while speech is active, and stays subordinate to voice, speaker identity, captions,
and the argument. It is not a generic equalizer or entertainment layer.

### Conversation handoff

Two compact speaker identities remain anchored. One persistent question, claim, or object passes
between them. Active-speaker state uses weight, contrast, or one bounded line—not bouncing portraits,
two accent colors, or dueling waveforms.

## Captions versus kinetic text

Use the canonical `drop / rail / embed` model:

- **Drop:** filler, stutters, and self-corrections when editorially permitted.
- **Rail:** default verbatim captions, clean and typeset, one or two lines, composited over the frame.
- **Embed:** one earned phrase per beat at most; never two adjacent or co-visible peaks. In an
  audio-only frame, Boundary Ledger realizes this state as thesis type rather than a subject matte;
  the caption state remains `embed` for compatibility.

Captions are access and verbatim speech. Kinetic type is an editorial visual. They cannot show the
same words simultaneously. Handwriting may annotate the model but never becomes transcript text.
If oxide already carries the active model path, caption emphasis uses weight or inversion rather
than a second oxide locus.

## The utterance trace contract

- Source is precomputed RMS or voice-activity data from a hashed audio asset.
- Extraction settings, sample interval, source checksum, and derived-data checksum are recorded.
- The trace is a single irregular steel or ink line on paper; past audio remains still.
- Amplitude may modulate pressure modestly. It may not scale the whole frame or text by every syllable.
- Oxide appears only across the actual commitment or thesis segment, not whenever the voice is loud.
- Runtime Web Audio analysis is prohibited for deterministic production output.
- A literal waveform is used only as a temporal index or when the audio artifact itself is evidence.
- When speech stops, the trace stops. Silence is stillness.

## Sound bindings

Sound supports an event without pretending to carry evidence:

| Role | Sound expression |
| --- | --- |
| Human context | Actual room tone, close tactile contact, or restrained organic motif |
| Dependency | Muted friction, material movement, restrained mechanical current, or descending phrase |
| Evidence | Usually silence; occasionally one dry placement sound |
| Commitment | One key, relay, pen, contact event, or short answering phrase |
| Risk | One interruption, suppression stop, or silence—not an alarm |
| Verified state | Normally none; at most one quiet confirmation after verification |
| Settled outcome | Silence or a short natural tail; the human motif may return with less pressure |

Voice and room tone are the default for podcast clips. Music is optional. If a bed exists, carve it
for speech and stop using it as a source of decorative visual motion. The OE identity sting is not
mandatory on excerpts.

## Format behavior

- **9:16:** model-led and text-led layouts are separately authored. Identity sits near an upper or
  side edge; captions remain an overlay. Critical small text avoids likely platform UI collisions.
- **1:1:** use a compact loop or consecutive model/thesis beats. Keep source and speaker identity at
  phone-readable scale.
- **16:9:** valid for long-form excerpts and podcast video. Do not merely center a vertical card in
  a landscape frame.

No format may be produced by blind crop. A format can be declared unsupported.

## Minimum specimen

Before this binding is called implemented, test one real 20–30 second excerpt in two treatments:

1. model-led;
2. text-led, with one actual-audio trace or clause ledger.

Render 9:16 plus at least one secondary format. Verify word sync, caption-over-content legibility,
silent comprehension, voice intelligibility, actual-PCM provenance, social UI collision risk,
fine-line survival after compression, flash/strobe safety, and a composed settled end state.

The interactive specimen in [`specimens/motion-and-audio.html`](./specimens/motion-and-audio.html)
uses the locked EP006 return-loop voice excerpt to test these rules. It is a browser reference—not an
encoded production delivery.
