01 / Principle

The page stays composed. The model stays rough.

Boundary names who owns the capability and where dependence begins. Ledger names what the publication is prepared to record. Neither requires the page itself to become a diagram.

A restrained institution with visible working models

The editorial page is calm enough to carry evidence. The episode image is where uncertainty remains visible: open corners, revised paths, unfinished calculations, and the one decision that changes the model.

This separation keeps the illustration intimate without making the surrounding interface feel provisional or decorative.

  • Compose the page. Use hierarchy, whitespace, and accountable records.
  • Expose the model. Let the illustration show the relationship under inspection.
  • Mark one decision. Oxide identifies the active path, not general emphasis.

03 / Working models

Draw the relationship before polishing the answer.

Each episode gets a specific model of a business relationship, dependency, leak, or return path. The image is not a scene, icon, or decorative background.

Discernible, intimate, unfinished

Lines overshoot, stop short, double back, and vary in pressure. Corners may remain open. Parallel strokes should not align perfectly. Construction marks remain visible.

Mineral and steel carry most of the drawing. Oxide marks one thesis-bearing movement. Side calculations add intimacy but never carry the whole explanation.

Read the complete illustration contract
  • Unfinished corners. Objects remain readable without closing every shape.
  • Overdrawn strokes. Repeated marks show searching and correction.
  • One oxide path. Color identifies the decision that changes the economics.
  • Whole composition. Keep the 3:2 paper uncropped and undistorted.

04 / Docket

The accountable surface.

The illustration may be provisional. The episode identity, evidence count, date, and artifact state are not.

05 / Typography

Type sounds certain where the drawing does not.

Typography belongs to the publication. Handwriting belongs inside the episode model.

The relationship is the asset.

Discovery is useful. Paying for the same guest twice is a leak.

The episode separates the first transaction from the relationship that follows. The operating question is whether the hotel can earn the return stay directly.

08 / Boundaries

The rejection test is part of the system.

The most likely failure is not bad craft. It is letting the illustration language spread until the entire publication looks like an unfinished diagram.

Keep

  • One expressive model. Keep the surrounding page quiet.
  • Whole 3:2 artwork. Redraw for a new format instead of cropping.
  • One oxide movement. Make the thesis-bearing path obvious.
  • Accountable metadata. Dates, source counts, and status stay explicit.

Reject

  • Checkered grids, crosshairs, node maps, or leader lines outside the artwork.
  • Vector-perfect sketches, closed corners, uniform strokes, or faux wobble filters.
  • Photography, scrapbook props, coffee stains, tape, torn edges, or stationery theater.
  • Gradients, glass, pills, rounded SaaS cards, or multiple competing status colors.
