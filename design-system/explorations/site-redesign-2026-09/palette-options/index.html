Decision standard

A palette passes only if the thesis survives without color.

  1. Five seconds: core and perimeter are distinguishable.
  2. Grayscale: observed, modeled, and unknown remain legible.
  3. Association: readers say research, ownership, and real business, not AI, finance, or lifestyle.
  4. Comprehension: the existing Canvas T1–T4 tasks do not regress.
