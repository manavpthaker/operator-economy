02 / The thesis

Answered phones, sold as an outcome.

The thesis as recorded in 2026-07, unchanged.

As recorded

Local businesses will pay a person, not a platform, to make their phones stop bleeding. You bundle ElevenLabs or Retell with a phone number and a booking system, and sell it as an outcome: missed calls captured, appointments booked, front-desk time saved. Solo operators are already running these; the wedge is the industry you already know.

  • Buyer. Local businesses whose phones go unanswered.
  • Bundle. ElevenLabs or Retell, a phone number, a booking system.
  • Sold as. An outcome: missed calls captured, appointments booked, front-desk time saved.
  • Wedge. The industry you already know.
