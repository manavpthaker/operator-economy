15 / Stop conditions

The only place risk color appears.

Stop conditions are actual negative exceptions: a failed condition, not a warning style. Risk color marks the list edge and its heading and appears nowhere else on the page.

Why it stays scarce

Sage marks verified state only, and risk marks actual contradiction only. If either color spreads into general emphasis, the reader loses the one signal that says something has already failed.

A stop condition names the observation that ends or redesigns the first test. It never decorates a caution, a tip, or an estimate.

Stop or redesign when

  • The property can't establish a credible baseline.
  • Guest permission or data access is unclear.
  • The destination can't complete the promise.
  • Activity rises while direct share doesn't move.

17 / Boundaries

The rejection test is part of the system.

The most likely failure isn't bad craft. It's letting the illustration language spread until the entire publication looks like an unfinished diagram.

Reject

  • Checkered grids, crosshairs, node maps, or leader lines outside the artwork.
  • Vector-perfect sketches, closed corners, uniform strokes, or faux wobble filters.
  • Photography replacing the signature episode model; documentary Reality World footage remains valid.
  • Ambient drift, infinite pulse, faux wobble, generic waveforms, or kinetic text for every word.
  • Gradients, glass, pills, rounded SaaS cards, or multiple competing status colors.
