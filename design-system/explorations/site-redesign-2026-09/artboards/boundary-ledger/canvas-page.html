00 / Opportunity

Is the problem worth solving?

OTAs do discovery well. The opportunity appears when the hotel pays again to reacquire a guest it has already served.

Is there a real problem worth operating?

Start with the tension, not the tools.

  • Who already solves discovery well.
  • Where the relationship breaks after the stay.
  • What changes if the second booking becomes direct.

Your decision: is the return path valuable enough to deserve an operator?

What already works

The first booking can come through an OTA. Distribution gives an independent property reach it couldn't efficiently reproduce alone.

What the operator changes

Earn the second booking direct: permission, useful guest context, a relevant reason to return, and a destination that completes the promise.

  • Entry wedge. One property: install the recovery stack, move its direct share.
  • Proof before expansion. One property's direct share moved against its own baseline.
  • Aspirational destination. A guest-relationship practice serving independent properties.

04 / Guardrails

What should you test first?

The first engagement should reduce uncertainty, not hide it. Establish the baseline, repair the destination, respect permission, and define the result that would justify continuing.

What can you test before making a bigger claim?
  • The baseline required before intervention.
  • The booking and phone path that must work first.
  • The stop conditions that prevent activity theater.

Your decision: can a bounded test produce a credible yes, no, or redesign signal?

Start with permission and a baseline

  1. Measure booking mix, commission, cancellations, missed calls, and repeat stays.
  2. Repair the mobile booking and phone path before running outreach.
  3. Begin with past guests who have permission to hear from the property.
  4. Track direct share before and after; use other metrics to diagnose the path.

Stop or redesign when

  • The property can't establish a credible baseline.
  • Guest permission or data access is unclear.
  • The destination can't complete the promise.
  • Activity rises while direct share doesn't move.
