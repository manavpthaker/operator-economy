# OE Sting v1 Candidates

All three candidates were generated with ElevenLabs Sound Effects v2 at exactly 2.4 seconds. The three-note ascending D-minor/Dorian identity, organic pulse, metallic resolve, tempo neutrality, and exclusions remain fixed. Only material emphasis changes.

## Candidates

1. `oe-sting-v1-1-human.mp3` — warmer chamber strings and a brass hotel-key resolve.
2. `oe-sting-v1-2-system.mp3` — tighter strings and a crisper relay/key resolve.
3. `oe-sting-v1-3-balanced.mp3` — equal human warmth and system precision.

Raw generations are preserved in this directory. `level-matched/` contains audition copies processed toward `-16 LUFS` and `-1 dBTP`; use those for comparison, then master the selected raw candidate in context with the cold open.

## Selection test

Judge each candidate in three conditions:

- phone speaker, without the episode score;
- over the moving OE logotype and episode title;
- pre-lapped into both `Operatic Baroque Afrobeat` and `Ornate Motion`.

Select for recognition and transition quality, not loudness. After selection, export a clean WAV master plus separate `pulse`, `strings`, and `resolve` stems if the source workflow permits them.
