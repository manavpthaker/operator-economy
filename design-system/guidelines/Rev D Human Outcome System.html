Production selection · Voice 1.3

Voice 1.3 is the identity anchor.

Keep the selected render as the comparison reference. Save its derivative voice ID before production configuration changes, then direct each section through script rhythm and generation settings.

Preserve the speaker's identity, age, accent, and natural timbre. Shift from smooth, relaxed narration to a close, grounded documentary operator: more forward consonants, firmer endings, moderate pace, and clear contrast between contained urgency, conversational disbelief, decisive explanation, and warm resolution. Sound experienced, human, and naturally uneven. Avoid trailer gravitas, radio polish, sales cadence, hype, theatricality, and sleepy lounge softness.
Episode score · two validated lanes

Score the story. Keep the sting.

Operatic Baroque Afrobeat supplies epic propulsion and system-building momentum. Ornate Motion supplies a more restrained electronic lane for denser explanation. Choose by episode; neither is the universal bed.

  • Peril: low cello pulse, location sound, and fragments of the string ostinato.
  • Extraction: descending strings and gathering Afrobeat percussion.
  • Reversal: hard silence, then one ascending violin figure.
  • Build: 104–112 BPM, full polyrhythm with precise baroque ostinato.
  • Resolve: release the percussion while the strings open into the logo harmony.

Challenger lane: 94–102 BPM downtempo deep house, sub pulse, tactile percussion, and restrained analog movement. Cleaner under dense explanation, but less ownable. Use it as an A/B, not the default.

Universal OE sting: exactly 2.4 seconds and unchanged across episodes. One deep organic pulse, three ascending D-minor/Dorian string notes, then a precise metallic key/relay resolve with a short warm tail. Free-time and tempo-neutral; overlay it on continuing story motion around 0:02 instead of cutting to a bumper.

2.4-second audio logo, no vocals. A memorable three-note ascending mnemonic in D minor/Dorian, played by intimate baroque strings over one deep organic drum pulse, resolving with a precise metallic key or relay strike and a short warm tail. Premium, intelligent, human plus system, confident but not heroic. Free-time and tempo-neutral. No riser, boom, choir, brass blast, EDM drop, or melody beyond three notes.
