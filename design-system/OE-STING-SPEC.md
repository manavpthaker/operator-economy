# OE Universal Sting v1

## Identity

The sting is a **2.4-second, tempo-neutral audio logo** used across every Operator Economy episode. Episode scores may change; this mnemonic does not.

## Composition

- `0.00`: one deep, organic pulse; OE logotype begins to appear.
- `0.18-1.55`: three ascending notes in D minor/Dorian, voiced by intimate baroque strings. The interval shape must remain identical in every export.
- `1.55`: precise metallic key or relay strike; episode title locks.
- `1.55-2.40`: warm tail, under 0.6 seconds of audible decay, ready for the episode score to pre-lap.

Keep the phrase free-time and without a beat grid so it can bridge into Afrobeat, orchestral, or downtempo deep-house cues. It should feel premium, intelligent, human plus system, and confident without becoming heroic.

## Generation or composer prompt

> 2.4-second audio logo, no vocals. A memorable three-note ascending mnemonic in D minor/Dorian, played by intimate baroque strings over one deep organic drum pulse, resolving with a precise metallic key or relay strike and a short warm tail. Premium, intelligent, human plus system, confident but not heroic. Free-time and tempo-neutral so it bridges into Afrobeat, orchestral, or deep-house scores. No riser, boom, choir, brass blast, EDM drop, or melody beyond three notes.

## Integration rules

- Overlay the sting on story motion around `0:02`; never create a separate intro bumper.
- Let location sound continue beneath the first pulse.
- Duck VO only if a title line overlaps the metallic resolve.
- Export a clean master plus stems for `pulse`, `strings`, and `resolve`.
- Test at phone-speaker level and confirm the three-note contour remains recognizable without sub-bass.
- Do not alter pitch, intervals, duration, or resolve sound per episode. Only the following episode score changes.
