# Creative reference stack

No single reference represents the full OE format. Each is useful for one production behavior. The goal is to combine those behaviors inside OE's own restrained blueprint identity, not to imitate any channel's look.

## Ray Dalio: a persistent causal machine

Reference: [How The Economic Machine Works](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PHe0bXAIuk0&t=80s)

Study:

- Persistent actors and objects.
- Cause-and-effect animation.
- Small transactions accumulating into a larger system.
- A model that grows with the explanation.

Do not copy the surface illustration style. The transferable idea is that the viewer learns one machine over time.

## Branch Education: one object becomes an explorable system

Reference: [How Does Computer Hardware Work?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d86ws7mQYIg&t=20s)

Study:

- One recognizable object opening into components.
- Stable spatial relationships.
- Movement between system and component views.
- Labels attached to physical or functional parts.

OE should apply this spatial logic to a business without imitating expensive technical 3D rendering.

## Wendover Productions: footage and system explanation

Reference: [How Amazon's Super-Complex Shipping System Works](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2qanMpnYsjk&t=65s)

Study:

- Real footage establishing physical reality.
- Simplified animation explaining invisible routing and logic.
- Movement between the model and the operation.

Do not default to maps, flags, or logistics tropes when the business does not require them.

## Primer: a model that can be run

Reference: [Simulating Supply and Demand](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PNtKXWNKGN8&t=45s)

Study:

- Persistent actors and variables.
- Visible decisions and consequences.
- Changed assumptions producing changed outcomes.
- Economics behaving as a model rather than a static table.

Do not copy the character style or academic pacing.

## fern: a coherent rendered episode world

Reference: [How a CIA Base Works](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RZs5Hz1klno)

Study:

- One environment and visual language per episode.
- Restrained cinematic camera movement.
- Recurring locations and characters.
- Documents and external evidence integrated into the world.

Do not copy true-crime mood, faceless mannequin treatment, or darkness as the default OE tone.

## LEMMiNO: evidence as cinematic space

Reference: [The Vanishing of Flight 370](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kd2KEHvK-q8)

Study:

- Maps, timelines, documents, and technical facts treated spatially.
- Movement between macro and micro evidence.
- Restrained typography and pacing.
- Proof that feels investigated rather than pasted onto a slide.

Do not copy mystery pacing where the operator needs actionable clarity.

## neo: archival material connected through place

Reference: [How Singapore Became the World's Hub](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wdfF-doIVjo&t=50s)

Study:

- Archival material embedded in a larger graphical environment.
- Geography acting as continuity.
- Historical and present material sharing one visual language.

Use this selectively when location, infrastructure, market development, or supply chains actually shape the thesis.

## Film-direction method references

These repositories are pinned production-method inputs, not OE authorities, runtimes, or assets. Blueprint Cinema, the OE picture/audio contract, and Boundary Ledger govern when a borrowed method applies. Source paths and commit pins are recorded in `../SOURCE-MAP.md`.

### OpenMontage: concrete slots and editorial selection

Reference: [calesthio/OpenMontage at `08e2151`](https://github.com/calesthio/OpenMontage/tree/08e2151fa02de28a5d6a312b3d575692bf147ad7/skills/pipelines/documentary-montage)

Study:

- describe a shot as a concrete visual job rather than a topic or mood label;
- arrange and trim footage by narrative beat rather than retrieval score;
- find the useful sub-window instead of treating the complete generated clip as a shot;
- cut before an action exhausts itself and keep the transition vocabulary small.

Do not import its runtime or montage pipeline. The repository declares AGPL-3.0; OE references principles only and copies no code or templates.

### visual-skills: shot function and physical performance

Reference: [smixs/visual-skills at `3c55471`](https://github.com/smixs/visual-skills/tree/3c554715b5eb30f54de78fac3c0df4a7105e4955/video)

Study:

- every shot must reveal information, advance action, change pressure, or produce a consequence;
- camera movement needs a visible reason;
- abstract emotion must become concrete body behavior or environmental pressure;
- each clip needs a deliberate final image.

Do not import its dramatic-escalation cadence, dialogue assumptions, or genre references as universal OE rules. The material is CC BY 4.0; attribution: Serge Shima / `smixs/visual-skills`.

### AI Cinematic Pipeline: physical blocking over narrative prose

Reference: [billpar/ai-cinematic-pipeline at `ed7e286`](https://github.com/billpar/ai-cinematic-pipeline/tree/ed7e28652214b4f3b7fd147e2980596f02b8e2ca)

Study:

- translate narrative meaning into bodies, hands, props, positions, and visible consequences;
- keep voiceover text out of generation prompts;
- use concrete micro-actions rather than asking the generator for an abstract emotion;
- state multi-character geography before asking for performance.

Do not impose its dialogue coverage pattern merely because people are present. Choose it only when the scene form and actual soundtrack justify interaction. The repository is MIT licensed.

### Narrative Film Direction: geography and coherent coverage

Reference: [Nagacash/narrative-film-direction at `4ee8b42`](https://github.com/Nagacash/narrative-film-direction/tree/4ee8b42484cb57407f4a7772216aa79e20b0971f/skills/narrative-film-direction)

Study:

- establish master geography before generating closer setups;
- derive setup frames from the approved master rather than from another close-up;
- preserve the action line, screen direction, and spatial continuity;
- animate one primary action per clip and assemble before finishing.

Do not treat its five-setup protocol as a mandatory shot or cut count. Select coverage for the scene's informational or emotional turn and preserve meaningful holds. Conversation coverage may serve explicitly narrated dramatization as well as heard dialogue, under the separate OE picture/audio safeguards. Code is MIT; documentation is CC BY 4.0. Attribution: Naga Codex / Maurice Holda.

## OE reference equation

The intended result is:

> Dalio and Primer's causal systems + Branch's spatial teardown + Wendover's footage integration + fern's rendered world + LEMMiNO's evidence discipline + physically directed, spatially coherent footage, expressed through OE's own Boundary Ledger identity.

Modern MBA is useful as a baseline for a clear narrated business argument, but it is not the visual destination. Blueprint Cinema should make the operating system itself memorable and actionable.
