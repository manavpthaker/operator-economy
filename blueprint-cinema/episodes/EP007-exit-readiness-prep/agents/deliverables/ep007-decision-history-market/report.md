## Minimal continuing decision record

Use one appendable event per decision or correction, linked to its prior event. Record:

- **Trigger and authorship:** exact feedback/message ID or defect evidence; separate owner instruction from assistant interpretation.
- **Audience job and context:** what becomes understandable, emotional stake, mechanism chosen, withheld information, alternatives and reasons.
- **Direction and implementation:** picture/audio mode, persistent objects, action/expression arc, exact word/frame/source cues, hashes and every timing dependency.
- **Authority and disposition:** proposed, authorized, generated, technically checked, reviewed, selected or superseded; spending and canonical gates remain separate.
- **Result and transfer:** observed outcome, evidence method and limits, rejected options, protected decisions, applicable conditions, exceptions and re-review trigger.
