## Verdict

The first frame fixes R5's central story problem: it immediately establishes an owner, a buyer, and
an ordinary working business. The generated 15-second shot does **not** yet pass as a complete
`narrated_observation` take, however. The buyer's page-point, head movement, and recurring parted-mouth
appearance read as an unheard explanation rather than the prescribed silent visual scan.

The shot-03 close-up reference is also not usable as-is. Its tan shirt carries a plainly visible
embossed/swirl texture across the collar, chest, and sleeves, with related synthetic contour texture
on the owner's neck and face. At close-up scale this reads as a material/wardrobe discontinuity from
the plain worn canvas shirt in shots 01 and 02, not harmless grain. It was not treated as corrected.

Production authority and the required `inputs_locked` prerequisite remain unestablished.

## Consequential findings

- **Character and place establish.** The wide reference makes the owner screen-left and buyer
  screen-right legible on first contact. Their age, hair, glasses, tan/blue wardrobe, workshop,
  table, green binder, blank page, pencil, keys, and background worker remain coherent in sampled
  shot-01 frames (`DIRECTION.md:7-13, 19`). This is narratively stronger than a hands-only opening.
- **No false evidence claim.** The page remains blank and the binder remains an ordinary prop. The
  shot does not present invented records, an operating failure, stopped work, or a solved mechanism.
- **Shot-01 performance drifts.** Around the early action the buyer points or taps toward the blank
  page, then retracts and changes arm posture. This is more authored action than the specified small
  downward visual scan. The point is not itself a geography or prop-continuity failure, but paired
  with mouth/head movement it assigns the buyer an explanatory or questioning performance. That
  violates the narrator-only mode and weakens the mute test (`DIRECTION.md:9, 19, 23, 31`).
- **The full 15 seconds do not cleanly settle.** The buyer continues small posture and facial changes
  after the point; sampled late frames do not give the specified unequivocal closed-mouth, quietly
  occupied end state. A short closed-mouth subwindow could still be editorially useful, but the full
  take should not be represented as passed.
- **Reference continuity is otherwise coherent.** Shot 02 preserves the owner, wardrobe color,
  left/right action line, table, blank page, and green binder as closer coverage. Shot 03 preserves
  identity and geography, but its amplified synthetic surface pattern is consequential at the exact
  close-up intended to carry human hesitation.
