## Recommendation: follow one business through the broker's workbench

Leave the presenter and enter one persistent, rough Working Model of a sale desk. Keep the same illustrated business dossier identifiable throughout. It is a conceptual example, not the opening owner's documented sale or a fabricated real listing. Three earned beats:

1. **The broker takes on the work.** The owner brings the business dossier to the broker. Valuation, preparation and buyer conversations accumulate around that same dossier. Do not animate a separate icon for every narrated verb. The useful picture contribution is continuity: the broker is doing substantial work before there is a completed transaction.
2. **Payment waits for the outcome.** In a clearly identified success-fee example, keep the broker's work and the conditional payment visibly separate. Only when the example reaches closing does the fee move to the broker. Hold that relationship while the narration explains why it is fair to the owner. This reveals the timing and allocation of risk, rather than merely showing a handshake when the voice says “closes.” Do not imply that every listing reaches this state, that all professionals use this fee structure, or that the opening owner has sold her business.
3. **The same process faces a changed market.** Keep the sale desk and dossier grammar. Widen the view to reveal more businesses entering and buyers comparing alternatives. Let inspection and comparison become the focal action, with no winners, rejections, counts, conversion rate or invented offers. New inference: the brokerage arrangement can remain useful while the conditions around it become more demanding.

This is where explanation earns a Working Model: the audience can see who works, when payment occurs, and what changes around that arrangement. It avoids another presenter through a persistent accountable object, not through unrelated stock activity.

Withhold the empty preparation role, a broken funnel, an owner-removal test and the new practice. Those would answer S04/S05 early; the dependency mechanism belongs to S08. If the three-beat treatment feels crowded after exact timing, end this scene after the success-fee explanation and make the market change a new scene. Do not squeeze it by speeding the model up.
