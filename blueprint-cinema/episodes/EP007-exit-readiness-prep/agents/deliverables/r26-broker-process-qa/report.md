## Material finding

**Major for portrait-phone viewing: the conditional terms and work label are too subordinate.** The contact sheet gives the large “Success fee” / “No success fee” outcomes immediate weight, while the conditions and completed-work label require closer reading. At 390 CSS pixels across a scaled 1280-wide frame, the 29px conditions become 8.84px, the 27px work/upfront labels 8.23px, and the 19px example qualifier 5.79px. These are scale calculations, not a completed mobile-browser test. The model's meaning depends on the conditions being readable; a large fee outcome alone is insufficient.

Recommended correction: make the conditions at least as readable as the outcomes and remove redundant small copy to make room. Short labels such as **If sold** / **If not sold** permit approximately 40px source text without long wrapping. Keep **Work already done** prominent. Drop the small final summary (“The work stays. The fee depends on closing.”), which repeats both the fork and the narration; reclaim that space and the large upper gap for the causal model. Root should verify at the actual phone viewport before handoff. Do not simply enlarge the whole diagram beyond its safe area.

## Semantics

No semantic blocker found in the observed states. The same accumulated broker work persists before both explicitly conditional fee outcomes. “No success fee upfront” qualifies the claim to the displayed fee arrangement; it does not claim every professional works this way or that every transaction has no other costs. No sale is presented as guaranteed, no opening-owner result is asserted, and no preparation vacancy or new-practice thesis is introduced early.

This contributes a useful relationship beyond the spoken task list: **the work remains completed even when the success fee does not arrive**. The grouped paper accumulation is preferable to separate phone, magnifier, handshake and payment animations. Its remaining limitation is explanatory scope: it visualizes compensation risk more specifically than the broker's individual operating tasks. That is consistent with this bounded scene's declared purpose; do not describe it as a detailed brokerage workflow.
