## What the evidence supports

1. **A matching prompt is not a preserved performance.** M–Q requests all use `type: image`, an image asset and audio asset. None supplies an avatar ID, source-performance video, motion-reference ID, or seed. N changed one requested action, but there was no same-recipe repeated control to estimate generation variability. O additionally confounded composition, face scale, starting hand pose and direction. Q isolates the source-image change at the request level, not its causal effect from single-sample generation variability. See [M request](/Users/brownmanbrain/GitHub/operator-economy/blueprint-cinema/experiments/EP007-PRESENTER-001/repair-r14/video-request.json) and [Q request](/Users/brownmanbrain/GitHub/operator-economy/blueprint-cinema/experiments/EP007-PRESENTER-001/repair-r18/video-request.json).

2. **Stronger wording did not deliver selective motion control.** P's 668-character direction specified minimal jaw opening, no nods and a pause-only wrist turn; speech-time gestures remained. Q's 242-character M prompt still produced two lifts. This is evidence against continuing this same prompt strategy, not proof that prompts have no effect. The existing [O/Q image](/Users/brownmanbrain/GitHub/operator-economy/blueprint-cinema/experiments/EP007-PRESENTER-001/media/repair-r16/study-visible-hands-source.png) already shows an open right hand slightly raised above the desk. That may encourage gesture completion, but it is unisolated; retain the image unchanged under the owner's constraint.

3. **Smaller measured movement is not the target by itself.** The accepted M had a larger sampled normalized mouth-opening peak on “straight” than Q (0.239 versus 0.217). Q still drew the head-motion complaint. These sparse 2D measures support diagnosis, not a scalar naturalness score. See [word comparison](/Users/brownmanbrain/GitHub/operator-economy/blueprint-cinema/experiments/EP007-PRESENTER-001/repair-r18/word-motion-review.json).

4. **Internal temporal QA was insufficient for the perceptual problem.** Owner playback occurred repeatedly, and reports explicitly withheld acceptance. The gap was that our internal checks concentrated on sparse stills, contact strips, audio correspondence and tracking statistics. [S3 visual review](/Users/brownmanbrain/GitHub/operator-economy/blueprint-cinema/experiments/EP007-PRESENTER-001/repair-r20/edge-cleaned-visual-review.json) explicitly records no full-speed playback; it checked four edge-defect frames. Those checks could not rule out a brief size pulse or a detached-looking head. This was not an undocumented gate pass, but it was an inadequate basis for confidence in motion.

5. **Exact audio is already controlled; lip-sync still requires moving review.** The 5.600s WAV is a sample-exact master excerpt, 591.050–596.650, W001663–W001678. M–Q use the same asset; reported returned delay is about 23.021ms without inter-sentence drift. R–T copy Q's AAC and frame timestamps. This rules out those changes as a new audible-emphasis cause; it does not verify visemes, word/mouth correspondence or reaction appropriateness. Use the **current O/Q still image unchanged** and the **same 5.600s WAV**. Q is the same-image control. M is a historically accepted, differently framed mouth/head reference; its old approval does not transfer to new candidates.

Parent-selected photo-only alternatives: OmniHuman 1.5 and Hedra, subject to the parent's separate verification of the exact available model/version and existing-audio route. This forensic report does not certify their current capabilities. Generate **two independent samples per model, four total**, with identical inputs and one frozen concise direction where supported. Record exact settings and unsupported controls; do not rewrite the prompt between samples. No appearance changes, new voice, hand-pose edits, reference video, retiming, or local motion repair.

Judge each full clip at **1x with its exact audio first**, at normal framing and practical display size. Record separate judgments for: second-sentence word/mouth match; visual accents on “straight” and “never”; head/neck continuity; restrained hand behavior; natural pause and ending. Then use slow playback/stills only to locate specific defects. Review candidates without showing motion-reduction percentages or model labels first. Owner judgment determines preference; automated checks verify identity, timing and integrity.

**Stopping rule:** one four-sample batch, no further synonymous-prompt retries or Q warps. If neither model gives an acceptable current-photo result, report that the constrained approach has not met the requirement. If only one of a model's two samples passes, label it an isolated candidate with unproven repeatability. Do not extend to a longer passage until one exact short candidate is explicitly accepted. Two samples expose obvious inconsistency; they do not establish reliability.
