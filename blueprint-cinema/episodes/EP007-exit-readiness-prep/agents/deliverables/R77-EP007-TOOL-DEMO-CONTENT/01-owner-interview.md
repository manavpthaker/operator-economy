# Example Cabinet Workshop — owner interview

Illustrative draft · fictional business · not a client case.

## Q1 — Walk me through a standard order.

Owner: Sam checks the specification against our standard price list, prepares the quote and sends it to the customer. Once the customer approves, Sam saves the quote and releases the job to the workshop.

## Q2 — What can someone check for JOB-014?

Owner: The approved specification, quote and release note are in its job folder. They all use JOB-014. The release note links back to the approved specification.

## Q3 — Who approves a price outside the list?

Owner: Only I do. If I am away, it waits. I have not authorised a backup. Writing a handoff note does not change that.
