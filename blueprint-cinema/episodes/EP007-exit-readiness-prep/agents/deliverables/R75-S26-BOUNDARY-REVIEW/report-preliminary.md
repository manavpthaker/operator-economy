Recommendation: proceed with the proposed mineral identity end card. Use the accepted R36 identity typography and color relationship: **#173530 mineral**, **#F5F0E6 paper text**, **THE** in Supreme 500 and **Operator Economy** in Boska 700. The R36 source uses 25px and 88px respectively at 1280 × 720, establishing a useful scale precedent without requiring identical placement.

Exact hierarchy and copy:

1. Prominent publisher wordmark: **THE Operator Economy**.
2. Subordinate **Build. Own. Operate.**
3. One visually attached destination group: **Download the Operator Blueprint** immediately followed by **theoperatoreconomy.com**.
4. Plain **Subscribe**.

Keep the full card visible from its first frame through the final hold. This is an institutional identity and editorial invitation, not a measured outcome, product UI, status ledger or new diagram. A simple typeset card is sufficient for the approximately 6.2-second close. The static closing context does not require repeating the sting's timed word reveals.

Concrete mismatch risks:

- Merely recoloring the legacy navy/grid/gold layout leaves the blueprint decoration that prompted the correction. Use a flat mineral field; no grid or replacement accent.
- Making **Build. Own. Operate.** compete with the wordmark repeats the legacy hierarchy. Keep publisher identity dominant.
- Separating the domain from the download invitation weakens the action/destination relationship. Attach those two lines as one group, with clear space before **Subscribe**.
- Oxide, sage, risk colors, checks or status boxes would add unsupported commitment/verification meaning. There is no need for them in this card. The `EditorialAction` visible-focus requirement is explicitly part of the web DOM binding; it does not require a decorative video button or oxide accent.
- Tiny subordinate copy would make the requested website/download functions nominal. Check the actual encoded 1280 × 720 frame, especially the domain and the plain subscribe line.

The semantic core says accountable treatment does not create certainty.
