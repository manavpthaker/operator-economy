## Corrections needed before submission

1. **Make populated content unmistakable.** Request clearly visible dark printed text groups occupying a substantial area of the exposed page. The viewer must distinguish a completed record from blank paper at the final review size. Characters remain too small and oblique to decipher: no invented legible names, values, signatures or operating facts. Do not use “soft rows” alone; it can produce an effectively empty page. Content must be present when first exposed, remain stable during pointing, and survive the A/B seam. Keep the original loose blank sheet untouched.

2. **Replace conflicting v2 direction.** The inspected DIRECTION-v2 and prompts prohibit pointing, tracing, nods, smiles and looking back; they also leave A closed and put opening in B. Those instructions conflict with the owner's correction and must not remain in the new generation packet. Replace the blanket reaction ban with the specified single acknowledgment; preserve the no-handshake, no-signature and no-transaction-result boundaries. The new non-speaking action and acknowledgment fit the overall narrated dramatization; no character lines or exact lip-sync claim are required.

3. **Protect the causal order.** Owner indicates the row, withdraws, buyer reads it independently, then acknowledges. An immediate nod on arrival or a nod before looking at the page reads as politeness rather than liking the answer. A smile alone is too ambiguous. Keep his finger by the inspected row during the short acknowledgment, then return attention to the page. Do not manufacture a second reciprocal nod from her.

4. **Keep the physical geography possible.** Owner left, buyer right, same cast, wardrobe, workshop, light and action line. Open the binder where it can be reached and seen; do not force a separate reorientation merely to make it square to camera. The open cover must not conceal the record or intersect hands/props. Preserve the row's location across the cut, not just the binder's green cover. B's reference must show the successful populated page and her withdrawn hand. Keep the buyer's face, pointing hand, record and her resting hands visible; a face-only crop loses the reason for the nod.
