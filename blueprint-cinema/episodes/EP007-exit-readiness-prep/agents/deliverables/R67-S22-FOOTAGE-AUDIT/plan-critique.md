Keep the open record, the buyer's task-focused face and the owner's resting hands legible in one frame. The buyer may open the delivered binder once and then remain occupied with the exposed page. Do not add a second record, calculator, signature, ticking boxes, repeated page turns or an approving nod. “He can check it” states availability for checking; the image must not imply that an audit was completed or the answer certified true.
