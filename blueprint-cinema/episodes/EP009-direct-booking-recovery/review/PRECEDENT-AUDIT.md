## Headline findings

1. **The existing EP009 opening study is built in a visual language the owner has not accepted for
   any episode.** It uses Boundary Ledger paper, mineral, steel and oxide roles, typeset records and
   anonymous contours, with film, presenter and generated imagery explicitly excluded. EP007's
   accepted language is different: filmed people, the V5 presenter, rough authored pencil marks in a
   persistent illustrated world, and an evidence surface with interest notes. The owner has already
   returned one EP007 test as "disconnected" when it matched the palette but not the representation
   (`r40-owner-style-continuity-return-v1`). EP009 should not inherit the study's grammar by default.
   The study's shot jobs and claim boundaries are sound and are worth keeping.

2. **The cold open has people in it, and the study drew them as contours.** EP007's accepted cold
   open uses film for the owner and buyer (early-03, early-04) and the presenter for first-person
   lines. EP009 S00 has an innkeeper, a guest, a front desk and a return visit, then a first-person
   line ("I couldn't find anyone selling it…"). Precedent points to film for the stay and return,
   a screen insert for the relay address, and the presenter for the first-person line (S10 and S14
   cases: assign each part to the speaker who owns the claim).

3. **Two open EP007 items trigger on EP009.** `presenter-head-variety` is deferred "until the next
   avatar recording," and EP009's presenter takes are that recording. The owner also required
   visible natural hand movement in any rebuilt avatar (`r40-owner-movement-test-authorization-v1`).
   Both must be planned into EP009's presenter takes as a set, not take by take.

4. **About half of EP009 has a close EP007 analog already owner-accepted.** The objection answered
   at other scales (S09 ↔ S12 R55), the empty role (S05 ↔ S11 R54), why you (S12 ↔ S14 R59), what AI
   does and does not do (S13 ↔ S15 R60), the first sale (S14 ↔ S16 R61, still pending in EP007), and
   the callback that pays off an earlier absence (S15, S20 ↔ S13 R57). These are the cheapest scenes to
   direct well.

5. **The two long arithmetic scenes have no accepted analog.** S10 (the ceiling, 109 s) and S17 (the
   operator's side, 104 s) are the hardest passages in the episode and EP007 never built a sustained
   calculation. The nearest cases are relative weight without share figures (R49) and a billable-hours
   bar (R60). Both scenes must keep "illustrative" and "modeled scenario" visible for their whole
   length, and the 10-point shift must stay marked as a hypothesis.

6. **The narration has no repeat of EP007's rushed "Fair objection."** EP009's "Fair question here."
   runs 2.6 words per second against EP007's returned 4.1. One join needs an ear check before picture
   is cut to it: the S19→S20 seam aligns with 0.03 s between "only." and "Say the first retainer's
   running.", and "Go back to the inn." aligns at 0.68 s. The aligner may have absorbed the inserted
   room into "only."; listen at master 1122.8–1127.5 before treating it as a defect.

## Presenter plan implications

Candidate presenter passages from the map above: end of S00, S02, S04, S08 opening, S10 ruling,
S11 bridge, S12, S17 ruling, S22–S24. That is roughly 3.5 to 5 minutes of presenter across 12 to 16
takes. EP007's recipe cost 81–90 Higgsfield credits per 9–10 s take plus about $1–2 of Fal Sync per
restoration, and longer takes drift up to 0.9 s (lesson sync-offset-varies-and-drift). At EP007
rates the presenter alone is about 1,300–2,000 credits and $20–35 of Fal. The balance on 2026-09-16
is 3,802 credits.

Carry into the take plan: head-movement variety across takes, visible natural hands, one continuous
take per passage where a sentence boundary would otherwise force a cut (R47), mono-to-stereo upmix at
unity (lesson mono-upmix-minus-3db), and each Sync output aligned to its own audio track.
