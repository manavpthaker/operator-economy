The intended visual mechanic remains a fair relationship-leak and recovery loop:

- The first OTA booking can be useful acquisition.
- The opportunity is the next appropriate booking from a permissioned returning guest.

- Permission, qualification, suppression, human review, and sensitive-case exceptions stay visible.
- The physical key tag represents the stay and becomes guest memory only after consent.
- An operator-side settlement or commission ledger represents economic consequence.
- A direct confirmation is the visible outcome object.
