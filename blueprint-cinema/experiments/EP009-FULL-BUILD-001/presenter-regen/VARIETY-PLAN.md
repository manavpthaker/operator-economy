Rules applied:
- At most one gesture per part, placed on one phrase, returning to rest.
- Adjacent parts (in film order, across passages) never repeat a gesture kind.
- Some parts have no gesture: 6 of 21.
- Eyes near the lens on every promise and question. Look-aways only on first-person recall lines (P01, P08b), never on a promise.
- No nods, head shakes, pointing, air-drawn lines or held hands. The r1 set's air-drawn ceiling line (P04, P12) is dropped: a held line drove long gesture choreography, which the drift lesson warns against.
