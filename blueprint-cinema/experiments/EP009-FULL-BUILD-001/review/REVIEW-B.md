## Method and calibration

- Frames extracted frame-accurately at 1 fps (full size) and 4 fps over the whole range, laid out as contact sheets with the words spoken in each window from `word-transcript.json`; 12 fps and 6 fps strips at presenter phrase starts and F08; seven-frame strips (-12, -3, -1, 0, +1, +3, +12 frames) at 36 boundaries; full-size stills at every major state change. Seam frame differences measured on decoded frames.
- Calibrated against accepted EP007 R57 to R63 QA renders. Those accepted scenes already use typeset cards, centred headings and generous empty paper (about 6 to 12 text blocks), so the card grammar itself is not a finding here; density well beyond that, claims, seams and repetition are.
