## Opening direction

**Dramatic question:** The opening holds the locked final line as its unanswered question: “The
strange part is who left the job undone.”

**Visual sentence:** One archetypal after-hours order-status ticket enters the bundled agent, is
routed to an unstaffed human queue, and remains visibly unresolved while the vendor and human cost
relationships become legible. The camera then finds a bounded but unclaimed span of work and ends
without assigning blame, proving demand, or showing a completed service.

**Rhythm:** human action -> current dependency -> handoff consequence -> cost mechanism -> dual
burden -> unresolved work -> proposed operator -> unanswered question. Cuts primarily land in
recorded pauses; the final question begins on a clean word-boundary cut. Internal actions keep
meaningful development below the shot duration.

### Semantic and expression ruling

- Canonical color follows the pinned color binding: warm ledger paper for open human context, steel
  for the current external route, and deep mineral only for subordinate source context. No oxide is
  used because no active commitment is made. No sage or risk red appears because the opening proves
  neither a verified success nor a separately sourced risk operation.
- Semantic operations are limited to `establish`, `trace`, `route`, and `settle`. `BO-022` is the
  only candidate business-state change depicted. Uses of `trace` reveal existing relationships and
  do not cause payment, purchase, configuration, or success.
- Every suggested motion expression is **provisional**, inherited from the provisional motion
  binding. Cuts, holds, and real physical action remain valid static-direction choices even if that
  binding changes.
- Locked narration is the only sound authorized here. No music, sound effect, ambience, or caption
  treatment is proposed by this packet.
