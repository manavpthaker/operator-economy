## Continuity and mute checks

- The owner remains frame-left and the buyer frame-right in all human setups unless a cut is explicitly neutral.
- The dark binder, warm sheet, pencil, keys, worktable, wardrobe, and lighting remain continuous.
- The only essential human action is caused and legible: binder shifts, sheet appears, pencil moves, pencil stops, sheet covers binder.
- No shot asks the viewer to infer a missing exchange. The question lives in narration and on the bounded graphic, never on moving lips.
- On mute, the human sequence reads as financial review → operating question → incomplete answer → re-ranked evidence.
- On mute, the graphic sequence reads as dependency exposed → one unfinished practice path identified.
