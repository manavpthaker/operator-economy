# Frame direction — The Unfinished Answer

## Governing idea

This opening has two materials, not two competing styles:

1. **Observed reality:** a restrained, warm business interior organized around one work surface.
2. **The Working Model:** the unfinished pencil mark expanded into a rough, accountable explanation.

The cut between them is causal. The illustration exists because the owner’s answer stops.

## Semantic palette

| Role | Value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Warm ledger paper | `#F5F0E6` | unresolved work, blank operating sheet, human context |
| Deep mineral | `#204440` | primary accountable graphic surface |
| Mineral depth | `#173530` | deepest institutional field and high-contrast structure |
| Core oxide | `#B5482F` | the single active thesis path or unfinished brace |
| Perimeter steel | `#586D74` | dependencies, rented capability, secondary structure |
| Ledger ink | `#19201F` | institutional type and dark pencil-equivalent detail |

Palette use is semantic. Oxide does not appear in the opening reality shots or the question card. It first appears when the opportunity becomes legible.

## Type

- **Display / identity:** Boska
- **Editorial title:** Zodiak
- **Institutional question and supporting copy:** Supreme
- **Small technical copy, only if functionally necessary:** system monospace

The bounded question is typeset exactly:

> WHAT HAPPENS HERE IF YOU ARE NOT AROUND FOR A MONTH?

The episode turn is typeset exactly:

> SALE-READINESS PRACTICE

Do not simulate handwriting for brand, identity, titles, or interface copy. No metadata, source IDs, claim numbers, status labels, or decorative subheads appear.

## Observed-reality frame

- One woman owner and one buyer share a worktable in an established operating business.
- The space should show ordinary competence: maintained tools, stock, records, or work in progress. It must not look distressed, chaotic, luxurious, or staged as a corporate boardroom.
- The owner is the visual center of gravity without being isolated as a victim.
- The buyer is attentive and procedural, not predatory or performative.
- Continuity anchors: the same table, dark finished binder, blank warm sheet, pencil, and keys.
- Wardrobe and light stay understated and natural. Avoid glossy commercial polish, shallow-focus glamour, or overt “cinematic” haze.
- The blank sheet remains blank except for faint, non-readable pencil marks. The binder never exposes fabricated financial details.

Generated plates are still animatic placeholders. Hold them as composed frames. Do not create motion by panning, zooming, warping, or drifting the stills.

## Working Model frame

The model is a back-of-the-napkin operating sketch, not architectural drafting and not a polished infographic.

- Build contours from short, individually placed strokes.
- Let neighboring strokes overlap, miss, and overshoot.
- Keep some corners open and some parallel lines visibly imperfect.
- Show one or two correction passes where the maker reconsidered a relationship.
- Use selective cross-hatching to indicate load, not uniform texture.
- Keep proportions uneven but recognizable.
- Never apply one global wobble or roughening filter to otherwise perfect geometry.
- Preserve blank paper around the model so the unresolved relationship has air.

The first model state contains a business block, the owner support, and perimeter-steel dependency paths. Nothing collapses. The final state adds one incomplete core-oxide brace and stops before resolution.

## Composition

- Primary content stays inside a 12% horizontal and 10% vertical safe area.
- One focal relationship per frame.
- Human shots use negative space to make the work surface and unfinished sheet legible.
- Model passages use a broad horizontal field, with no dense dashboard, mini-label system, or panel grid.
- The accountable card or title may overlap only blank illustration margin; it may not conceal a meaningful stroke or human action.

## Motion

- Default edit is a cut.
- Within reality passages, motion must come from a physical event in the eventual footage: binder shifts, sheet is revealed, pencil moves, pencil stops, sheet covers binder.
- Within model passages, reveal one relation at a time through finite draw-on or replacement actions, then settle.
- Draw-ons follow the rough source strokes; they do not trace a clean hidden master.
- No ambient loops, idle motion, perpetual line boil, breathing scale, generic camera push, or automatic parallax.
- The decisive hold is the stopped pencil. The rest of the frame must stay still enough for that cessation to read.

## Sound relationship

- The locked narration carries language.
- Human images carry action and consequence, not dialogue.
- Graphics carry bounded explanation and must remain intelligible on mute.
- Preserve the `44.50–48.52` narration break. Do not fill it with generic music, explanatory copy, or an unapproved sting.
- Any future ambience or foley must name its visible cause and sit beneath the narration.

## Prohibited appearance

- smooth vector contours disguised as pencil;
- evenly weighted line systems;
- generic blueprint grids;
- fabricated readable documents or figures;
- random gaze, lip motion, or conversational reaction coverage;
- source boxes and LLM-style annotations;
- illustrative collapse, sale closing, valuation multiple, price, or guaranteed outcome;
- a finished oxide solution before the episode has taught the practice.
