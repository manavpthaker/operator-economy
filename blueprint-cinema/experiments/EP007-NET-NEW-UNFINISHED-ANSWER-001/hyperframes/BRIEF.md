## Message

A profitable business can pass a review of its numbers and still fail the buyer’s more important test: can it keep operating without the owner? The pause before the answer exposes the opportunity.

## Emotional movement

**Competence → interruption → private calculation → structural exposure → opportunity**

The owner is never made foolish. She has built a profitable business over twenty-five years. The tension comes from a real dependency becoming legible, not from humiliation, collapse, or buyer theatrics.

## Picture strategy

The first half stays in an observational business setting. The buyer’s finished binder and the owner’s blank operating sheet create the contrast without invented figures or explanatory dialogue. The owner begins an answer, the pencil makes roughly four marks, and then it stops.

That incomplete pencil line becomes the bridge into the Boundary Ledger Working Model. The model does not decorate the scene or repeat every word. It exposes the dependency and then leaves one oxide brace unfinished: a practice that could be built, not an outcome already achieved.
