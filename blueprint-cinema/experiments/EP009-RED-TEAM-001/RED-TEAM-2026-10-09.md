## Blockers — these stop a schedule

### B1. The episode says "ten years in hospitality". facts.md says that is superseded.

The long-form narration at seg044 / P08 is, verbatim:

> "I spent ten years in hospitality, including Ace Hotel and Standard Hotels. I know this
> business from the inside out. I haven't sold this particular service, though. What a thirty
> room inn will pay for it is still something I'd have to test."

`content-os/facts.md:202` records: **"Over a decade in hospitality (owner correction,
2026-09-29: 'it's over a decade'; supersedes 'ten years')"**. Two later statements move it
again: 2026-10-07 ("the first decade of my career in hospitality"), and a 2026-10-09 preference
to frame the whole thing as hospitality operations / customer operations rather than hotels and
hotel restaurants.

The narration locked 2026-09-03, before all three. It is baked into the rendered VO
(`presenter-regen/body-r8/seg044/audio/AUDIO.json`) and into every presenter take generated
from it, r7 through r11.

This is a first-person biographical claim the owner personally corrected. Fixing it is a VO
re-cut of that line plus a regeneration of the P08 presenter take. Both are paid work.

### B2. EP009 has no thumbnail and no title.

Every thumbnail and metadata artifact under the slug is EP006's, dated August:
`thumbnail-a/b.json`, `thumb-ep006.json`, `thumbnail_inventory.json`, and
`content/youtube_metadata.md` ("Hotels Pay 30% to Book Their Own Rooms", Aug 16).
Nothing exists for EP009.

content-os names this as the system's own known failure: *"EP003 got 0.0% on 142 impressions
because no thumbnail was ever generated and nothing noticed"*, and *"The bottleneck is the
click, not the content."* This is the highest-leverage gap in the set and it has not started.

### B3. The current long-form cut has never been watched, and the last one that was got returned.

`assembly/qa/r11-picks-presenters/ep009-r11-picks-presenters-review-r1.mp4` exists: 20:22,
29,323 frames at 24fps, built 2026-09-29, status `encoded_unverified_review_only`.

The decision log carries **no owner verdict on r9, r10 or r11.** The last long-form verdict is
2026-09-25: *"lip-sync is all off. too exaggerated and looks completely fake"* against r6, plus
a single accepted test take at temperature 0.5. Four regeneration rounds followed and none was
shown.

Lip sync cannot be settled by measurement here — the cross-correlation audit was inconclusive
on this material and the timing tooling has returned two false readings this week. It is an
owner judgement. Excerpts at 0:51, 3:09 and 6:08 were cut for that purpose.

### B4. The slug collides with a live, already-published episode.

`studio/originate/direct-booking-recovery/launch/links.json` is **EP006**, published
2026-08-17 at `youtu.be/pOoQLaSyUGQ`, with a live trailer and four live Shorts.

EP009's internal id is `EP009-direct-booking-recovery` — the same slug. Its four new Shorts
live under a third slug, `direct-booking-practice`, which has no `launch/` directory at all.

If EP009's upload writes `links.json` under `direct-booking-recovery`, it overwrites EP006's
live URLs. That is precisely the failure content-os hard rule 3 exists to prevent. The slug
each surface publishes under has to be decided before anything is scheduled.

### B5. There is no facts.md section for EP009.

facts.md carries `## EP006 research` ("Added so the gate clears the EP006 launch copy") and
`## EP007 research`. There is no EP009 section. The only EP009 lines in the whole file are the
two hospitality entries in B1.

Every number these pieces state — 20 to 40 rooms, $180, 70% occupancy, $1,000 to $1,400 a
month, the commission band — lives in the episode's own claims map, not in facts.md. Launch
copy quoting any of them fails `doctor.sh --gate` on hard rule 1, "No number that isn't in
facts.md". This is also the open item the project memory already flagged: carrying the retired
legacy claims into facts.md.

### S2. Delivery specs are out of range.

- **Long-form r11 is 1280x720** — a review proxy, not a delivery master. No 1080p encode exists.
- **Long-form r11 is −22.5 LUFS**, 8.5 LU under the −14 target.
- **The four Shorts run −13.6, −14.7, −15.1 and −15.9 LUFS** — a 2.3 LU spread. Watched back to
  back they step in volume. YouTube normalizes toward −14 on platform, so this mostly
  self-corrects there and matters most for the masters and any off-platform use.

### S3. The four Shorts have no publication records.

No titles, no descriptions, no pinned comments. The contract validator's derived mode requires
a `pinned_comment` carrying long-form routing; none exists. (EP006's live pinned comments use
em-dashes, against voice.md's "NO em dashes anywhere in published copy". Historical, but live.)

### S4. Four Shorts on this subject are already published.

EP006's live Shorts include "The Real OTA Commission Isn't 15%. It's Up to 30%." EP009's
Short 04 is the commission arithmetic. Shipping this set puts eight Shorts about independent-
hotel direct booking on the channel, four of them routing to a pre-relaunch episode. That is a
positioning decision, not a defect, but it should be made deliberately.

## Worth fixing

**W1. Short 04 states a modeled number without the hedge its claim requires.** C020 is classed
`modeled (vendor low bound)` and its required attribution is "one vendor's own figure". The
Short says *"it lands near a thousand to fourteen hundred dollars a month"*, with "illustrative"
and the three assumptions spoken aloud, and the screen reads "derived" and "a hypothesis nobody
has measured". That is strong. But the vendor-low-bound hedge is absent and "lands near" reads
more settled than modeled. The long-form carries the full hedge; the Short does not.

**W2. facts.md cites the wrong record for its own correction.** The "over a decade" entry cites
`2026-09-17-owner-r2-feedback.json`, SHA `9bba1b45…`. The hash matches — and that record says
*"i spent 10 years in hospitlaity"*. No EP009 source record contains the word "decade". The
2026-09-29 correction's actual evidence is not retained in this repo.

**W3. Room counts vary across the set.** 20 to 40 rooms (Short 01's selection criterion), "a
twenty room inn" (02 and 04), "a thirty room inn" (long-form). Each is defensible alone; watched
together it reads loose. Short 04's 20 rooms / $180 / 70% matches facts.md's illustrative model
exactly, so that one is anchored.

**W4. Two claims expire 2026-12-03** — C007 (Booking.com partner help) and C009 (Google free
booking links). In date for an October ship. C010 carries an explicit instruction, "open the
vendor page before release", which has not been executed.

**W5. 197 stale evidence bindings** in the episode's decision log, from files edited after they
were bound. None from this week's work.

**W6. Tooling note.** `shorts_contract.py` position-checks `episode_bridge_line` inside
`spoken_copy`, which assumes the episode bridge is narrated. EP009's picture grammar puts it on
a silent end card. `episode_extension` is the correct field for non-spoken depth and is still
checked against the standalone payoff, so the contract holds — but the schema reads as though a
spoken bridge is the only shape.

## What passed

Stated because a red team that only lists faults is not a measurement.

- **All four Shorts PASS `shorts_contract.py`.** They had never been run through it: no manifest
  existed. One was written for this review with anchors set deliberately rather than extracted
  (mechanical extraction is what put Short 03's payoff on "Check your jurisdiction" in r4).
- **Zero banned words, zero em-dashes, zero income-promise patterns** across all four Shorts'
  spoken and on-screen text and across the 3,399-word long-form narration, against the current
  voice.md lists. Two apparent hits were false positives: "a kill condition one phone call away"
  is the kill-criteria sense, not "kill it"; "whether you're the person to build it" is not the
  banned two-branch summary closer.
- **The claims map is unchanged from its lock**, and the Shorts carry the attributions it
  requires: C007's "partner help" named, C009's "connected through a partner", C026's "This is
  not legal advice".
- **All four Shorts pass lint, runtime, layout, motion and contrast**, render at exactly the
  frame counts their storyboards specified before a presenter existed (1088, 1061, 1092, 1195),
  and run 44 to 50 seconds, inside the Shorts limit.
