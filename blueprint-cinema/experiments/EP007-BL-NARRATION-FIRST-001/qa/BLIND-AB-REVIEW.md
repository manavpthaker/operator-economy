# Blind A/B review

Status: directional select, not an execution lock or production approval.

The reviewer received only two unlabeled 7.75-second files and the locked narration with word
timings. The mapping was withheld until after the verdict.

- `variant-x.mp4`: Variant B, narration-first.
- `variant-y.mp4`: Variant A, clip-first.

## Forced choice

**X — the narration-first build.**

During “somebody finally looks,” X moves the buyer's attention away from the owner and into the
workshop. Its cut immediately after “looks” then places the owner inside the operation being
examined. Y holds that phrase in face-to-face conversation and does not make scrutiny explicit
until the buyer writes later.

## What the result supports

- X reads more as scrutiny than conversation.
- X keeps the owner upright and competent while making her centrality visible.
- X shifts the question toward the operation without depicting an answer.
- X's cut and focus concept are narratively motivated.
- Y has cleaner three-beat cadence, but its owner close-up personalizes the scrutiny and its
  notebook writing begins to imply a verdict.

## Current execution defects

X is a direction select, not a selected final generation. In the second shot, the focus migration
gets too soft on the owner and can read as synthetic facial degradation rather than a controlled
rack. The low-motion reverse can drag, workshop detail shimmers, geography and notebook
orientation are not fully convincing, and the buyer retains slight mouth movement.

Y's execution is cleaner, but the pad changes size across its edit, the owner's chin-on-hands pose
weakens her authority, the writing is stiff, and the transition line initially resembles a scratch
rather than a deliberate carrier.

## Choice-reversing condition

If a normal-speed owner review cannot read X's buyer gaze as moving into the operation, or reads
the late softness only as face blur, X loses its decisive advantage. Y would then be the safer
execution base, but its scrutiny timing, owner posture, and notebook continuity would still need
repair.
