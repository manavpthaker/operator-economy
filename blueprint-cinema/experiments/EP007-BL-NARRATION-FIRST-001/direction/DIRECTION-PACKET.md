# Narration-first direction packet

Status: shot logic locked before visual generation.

## Dramatic question

When scrutiny moves from the owner to the operation, is there anything legible that can keep
working without her?

The live-action passage poses that question. It must not answer it.

## Dramaturgy

- Viewpoint: scrutiny, not buyer-as-hero.
- Buyer desire: see past the competent owner into how the workplace runs.
- Visual obstacle: the capable owner is the most legible operating system in frame.
- Geometry: owner screen-left, buyer screen-right, operating space behind the owner; camera remains
  on the south side of their action axis.
- Gaze path: buyer → owner → operation.
- Rhythm: held master → gaze shift → pause → hard cut → held over-shoulder → rack focus → hold →
  hard graphic match to the rough model.

## Minimum coverage

### Shot 1 — relationship master

- Job: establish owner, buyer, workplace, and scrutiny.
- Scale: 35mm medium-wide two-shot, chest-height, moderate depth of field.
- Camera: locked tripod.
- Blocking: owner screen-left at her ordinary work surface; buyer screen-right in visitor posture;
  the operation recedes behind the owner.
- One action: the buyer shifts his gaze once from the owner toward the operation past her.
- Owner action: natural breath or blink only.

### Shot 2 — system shift

- Job: keep the owner present while scrutiny shifts from person to operation.
- Scale: 65mm medium over the buyer's right shoulder, more than 30 degrees from the master.
- Camera: locked tripod, same working side.
- Blocking: buyer is a soft screen-right foreground edge; owner remains screen-left/midground;
  ordinary operation remains visible behind her.
- One action: rack focus from the owner to the operating space. No one leaves or reacts.
- Carrier: retain a horizontal workbench edge or structural seam in the lower third for the rough
  pencil match.

## Phrase-locked edit

- Shot 1 begins on “The problem.” The buyer starts the gaze shift on “somebody,” completes it by
  “looks,” and holds.
- Hard cut after “looks” at excerpt-local `20.72` / passage-local `2.56`; never cut a word.
- Shot 2 holds the owner sharp through “Back to her question, then.”
- Rack focus begins on “here” and completes across “if you are not around.”
- Hold the operation sharp through “for a month.” Do not show an answer.
- At excerpt-local `25.90` / passage-local `7.74`, hard graphic-match the real horizontal seam to
  the rough model's broken pencil baseline. No dissolve.

## Coverage deliberately excluded

No owner reaction close-up, buyer face close-up, prop insert, exterior, arrival, employees, empty
workplace, empty chair, calendar, month montage, readable paperwork, handshake, contract, price,
keys, literal failure, owner disappearance, on-screen question card, generated dialogue, or
generated audio.

The exclusion matters: a close-up is not a default emphasis tool. This B arm tests whether shifting
focus inside a held relationship frame carries the thought more honestly.
