# RC007 Shot 1 film

## Purpose

Show competence through one complete work action rather than a pose.

## Prompt

Create one continuous locked-camera, natural-speed cinematic observational shot from the supplied first frame to the supplied last frame. Preserve the exact older woman owner, adult male buyer, faces, ages, hair, navy work clothes, closed notebook, industrial repair shop, scarred bench, tools, pump components, light, lens, focus, and owner-left/buyer-right geography. The owner smoothly completes one practiced operation: she reads the caliper on the machined part, makes one small precise adjustment, sets the caliper down, and places that same part into the shallow work tray. Her motion is economical, confident, and unhurried. The buyer remains secondary and simply tracks the work with neutral professional attention. Both mouths remain fully closed; neither person speaks, nods, gestures conversationally, or looks at camera. Camera and focus remain fixed. Normal real-time cadence, no slow motion. No extra person, object duplication, malformed hand, extra finger, tool or part morphing, dropped object, fumbling, owner confusion, buyer reaction, readable text, logo, handshake, contract, sale, price, money, keys, celebration, failure, dramatic lighting, camera move, focus pull, or advertisement.
