Brand-new continuity anchor generated only after the narration-first shot logic was locked. No
Variant A image or film is an input.

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: brand-new 16:9 first-frame continuity anchor for a
narration-first cinematic test. Create an entirely original observational documentary frame showing
a competent woman business owner in her own established workplace and a neutral inspecting buyer.
Use a lived-in independent equipment-repair and field-service workroom opening into an orderly but
imperfect working shop. Show real work surfaces, shelves, unmarked parts bins, practical tools, and
ordinary operating depth behind the owner. Exactly two people. The owner is a capable woman in her
late fifties, screen-left at her normal waist-height workbench, calm and fully at home in the space.

closed, completely unmarked notebook. Frame a 35mm medium-wide two-shot at chest height from a
locked tripod on the south side of their action axis. The owner sits visually between the buyer and
the deeper operating space without obstructing it. Their eyelines meet naturally; neither looks at
camera. Use moderate depth of field and preserve a clean horizontal workbench edge across the lower
third. Neutral practical daylight mixed with soft workshop fixtures; realistic skin, fabric wear,
wood grain, and minor workplace imperfections. Quiet professional scrutiny, not advertising or
prestige stock footage. No employee, active gesture, pointing, speaking, handshake, agreement,
