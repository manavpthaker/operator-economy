THE OPERATOR ECONOMY
EP009 · 04/04

At an all in commission of

eighteen to twenty two percent,

my assumption from the reported bands,

the inn is paying the sites

roughly a hundred and five to

a hundred and thirty thousand dollars

a year.

That's where the old version of

this argument stops.

It's the wrong number,

because the inn is never going

to move all of it.

It needs the sites.

The real question is what a

recovery service can shift.
