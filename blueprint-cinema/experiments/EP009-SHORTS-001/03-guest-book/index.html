EP009 · 03/04

So the guest book now lives

at the booking site.

The inn served the guest and

doesn't have his email.

Which means the recovery job has

a design constraint before it has

a tool.

You can't win a guest back

from a relay address,

and under the site's rules you're

not supposed to try.

What the inn needs first isn't

an email tool.

It's a guest book of its own,

at the front desk, that the

guest chooses to sign.

Fair question here.

If the parts to fix this

are cheap,

why doesn't the inn just do it?
