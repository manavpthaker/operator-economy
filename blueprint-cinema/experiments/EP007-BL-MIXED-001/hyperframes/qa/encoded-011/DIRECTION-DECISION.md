## Edit decision

- Keep the master-to-owner cut at 20.72 seconds, immediately after “looks.” The silent lead lets the
  viewer reach the owner before the narration says “Back to her.”
- Move the owner-to-inspection cut from 22.16 to 23.08 seconds, the native 24 fps boundary immediately
  before “What.” The v010 cut split the word “question” and abandoned the owner reaction before its
  stated job was complete.
- Use the 57-frame owner selection at native speed. Do not stretch, interpolate, freeze, or add a
  post-production camera move.
- Start the inspection eight source frames into its existing safe selected asset. This removes dead
  setup time, retains the complete mark–trace–look-back action, and ends before the asset's appended
  seven-frame hold.
- Do not add a buyer-face close-up. His action carries the meaning; his psychology does not. Do not
  add an empty-chair cutaway: it would literalize absence and imply an outcome the episode has not
  established.

## Reusable coverage logic

`relationship master → human-consequence reaction → procedural action → physical carrier becomes system geometry`

This is a directing heuristic, not a mandatory shot count. A close-up is warranted only when the
narration changes whose consequence the viewer must hold.
