## Customizations

- Start with two attributable evidence receipts carrying C005 and C001 separately in the manifest,
  while keeping internal claim numbers and source-claim identifiers off screen.
- Keep the generated-film disclosure in the show notes, media manifest, and platform metadata rather
  than placing a large explanatory caption over the cinematic shot.
- Use the approved short, overlapping, broken pencil strokes with unequal pressure and sparse
  cross-hatching. No smooth master contour or post-landing jitter.
- Preserve a hard epistemic cut from evidence to human context; do not morph a source receipt into
  generated paperwork.
- Match the generated table edge into a sparse rough inspection sketch. Make the buyer's checklist,
  the operating structure, and the owner tether legible as an inspection action; hold that resolved
  question through the reflective setup, then cut directly to `Revenue` without drawing a second
  blank folder.
- Keep claims and thesis type typeset. Handwriting may only orient objects inside the model.
- Keep visible labels only when they explain the model. Internal locators and production annotations
  stay in source metadata rather than in the picture.
- Use the sequence's only oxide route in the final conditional transfer test.
