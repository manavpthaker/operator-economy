# Boundary Ledger mixed-media frame specification

## Concept

The composed ledger survives every medium change. Evidence arrives as a formal receipt, human
context becomes cinematic generated film, and the argument becomes provisional only when it enters
the rough Working Model. The final oxide route is a conditional test, not an achieved outcome.

## Media registers

- Evidence: deep-mineral typeset records with the visible source and caveat, while internal claim IDs
  remain bound in the manifest rather than printed in the frame.
- Human context: natural generated live-action cinematography with disclosure carried in show notes,
  the media manifest, and platform metadata; no on-screen claim treatment. Coverage may move from
  shared space to one human face only when the cut changes whose consequence the viewer is holding.
- Working Model: fixed authored rough marks on warm paper—short overlaps, broken contours, uneven
  proportions, pressure changes, construction marks, and sparse hatching.
- Thesis type: composed Boundary Ledger typography; never handwritten and never duplicated as a
  caption rail.

## Composition

- 1920×1080. Paper is the persistent conceptual field; generated film may temporarily fill it.
- One primary focal point and one meaningful action plus its immediate consequence.
- Edge anchors and thin rules provide orientation, not a persistent HUD.
- Evidence-to-reality is an intentional hard epistemic cut. Within generated context, hard cuts may
  move from room to owner to inspection; they must preserve eyeline, competence, geography, and
  real-time performance. Reality-to-inspection uses the table edge as its continuity carrier. The
  resolved inspection question then holds through the narrator's reflective setup and cuts directly
  to the accountable `Revenue` record.
- Model actors keep stable identity: competent owner left, operating structure center, buyer or
  handover threshold right. A checklist must show inspection as an action rather than leaving an
  unlabelled arrow to carry the meaning.

## Motion

- Voice timestamps supply every cue. Snap visual cues to the nearest native 24fps frame; never retime audio.
- Use `establish`, `pin`, `trace`, `interrupt`, and `settle` only where their semantic state change is
  visible.
- Film motion must come from the generated shot itself: restrained camera travel, human
  micro-movement, and physical parallax. Do not simulate film with a Ken Burns move on a still.
- Generated film plays at native speed. Do not use slow motion, optical flow, or a second post camera
  move to manufacture cinematic weight.
- A reaction close-up must show recognition without prescribing emotion: one breath or blink is
  enough. No worried realization, performance beat, speech pose, or camera drift.
- Rough strokes reveal from fixed authored geometry and remain still after landing.
- No crossfade, page turn, scribble matte, paper tear, ink wipe, blur, glow, bounce, elastic ease,
  decorative pulse, or generic blueprint grid.

## Scale and legibility

- Evidence figures: 92–150px; evidence labels and qualifications: 21–30px.
- Thesis: 66–96px; supporting type: 24–32px.
- Working Model notes: 28–38px, but basic relationships must read without them.
- The evidence caveats and final conditional must remain readable at 50%.
