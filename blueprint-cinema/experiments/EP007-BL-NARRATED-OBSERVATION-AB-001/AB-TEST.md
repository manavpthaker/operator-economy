## Forced choice

Does Variant B make the viewer hear the question as a test of the operating system, rather than
wait for the characters to speak?

## Variants

- **A — silent dialogue staging:** the existing generated wide and answer/stop close-up.
- **B1 — narrated-observation diagnostic:** competent work, the ordinary staff dependency,

- **B2 — clean narrated observation:** competent work, the ordinary staff dependency, then one

Do not score the palette, rough illustration, binder ending, typography, or narration performance;

B2 passes if it removes the expectation of unheard dialogue while preserving the owner's
competence, the practical dependency, and the stop. The measurement must read as real work rather
than pantomime, both mouths must remain closed, and the action must not decay into random movement.

illustrative footage. Generated people remain illustrative context, never evidence.
