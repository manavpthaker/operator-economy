## Pass 1 — comprehension

Result: `PASS WITH MAJOR PLACEHOLDER LIMITS`.

Without production notes, the primary causal chain reads: an after-hours call creates `CALL-R01`; unanswered reality leaves `WO-R01` empty; the tag becomes unprioritized in voicemail; a source field is attached to the recovery route; uncertainty stops at a human gate; decline terminates while acceptance schedules the work order; the route resolves into a one-week measurement.

The system does not imply every call is a job. `NO GUARANTEE`, `PERMISSION TO RESPOND`, the empty card, the decline route, and the human gate visibly qualify the opportunity. The missing human-context, operator, dispatch, and outcome material remains conspicuous rather than masquerading as footage or evidence.

## Pass 2 — rhythm

Result: `MAJOR REVISION`.

The opening is deliberately slower, the voicemail leak adds pressure, route construction becomes denser, the human gate pauses, and the fork accelerates then settles. Meaning changes occur inside shots rather than triggering a new full-screen composition for each noun. The proof operation compresses source, highlight, extraction, attachment, classification, and acknowledgment into roughly 5.36 seconds, and the complete `CALL / REPLY / BOOKED APPT` endpoint holds for only about 1.14 seconds. Both need more reading time inside the locked duration. No 16-second span lacks a state change.

## Pass 3 — continuity

Result: `PASS`.

The red token remains `CALL-R01 / 20:47` throughout. Its subordinate states progress through permission, unprioritized, uncertain, reviewed, and finally `BOOKED` without creating a second customer token. The work-order card remains `WO-R01` and progresses `EMPTY → OFFERED → SCHEDULED → MEASURED`. It is not offered before the human release. The source disclosure persists after the proof-bench collapse. Intended progress remains left-to-right; decline turns down/left and terminates.

## Pass 4 — visual hierarchy

Result: `PASS AT 1920x1080; MAJOR REVISION AT SMALL DISPLAY`.

Each shot has one dominant relationship: phone/tag, tag/inbox gap, tag/route, tag/gate, tag/fork/card, gate/causal rail, or tag/card/measurement bracket. The render avoids a title-stat-list-card sequence, dashboard shell, website chrome, floating glass panels, permanent world inset, and decorative opening.

At full resolution, the primary tag, card state, gate, fork, and route labels are clear. Secondary ticket labels, the persistent proof-pin warning, and some source/measurement microcopy become too small at 480x270. The primary story survives, but the disclosure hierarchy does not yet meet a robust phone-size standard.

## Pass 5 — motion

Result: `PASS`.

Movement performs ring, tag, fall, separate, pin, highlight, extract, capture, classify, acknowledge, hold, review, release, offer, stop, book, attach, remove, and measure. Cuts remain the default. The only camera move is Shot 06's controlled `1.12 → 1.00` pullback, used to reveal the causal rail and remove the invalid automate-all bypass. There is no bounce, elastic energy, ambient loop, meaningless drift, or repeated zoom tour.

## Pass 6 — evidence and honesty

Result: `PASS FOR REHEARSAL; PUBLIC DELIVERY BLOCKED`.

Shot 03 introduces the local CSV in source context, displays the exact `SYNTHETIC TEST FIXTURE — NOT EVIDENCE` warning, highlights only `call_time=20:47`, extracts that field, attaches it to `CALL-R01`, changes the route behavior, and leaves the warning pinned. `WO-R01` separately carries `SYNTHETIC WORK ORDER — NOT EVIDENCE`. Neither object makes a performance, financial, customer, company, or conversion claim.

The fixture and work-order reconstruction would block public delivery if treated as real evidence. At phone scale, the persistent warning is not reliably readable after its larger introduction; that is a major disclosure-legibility finding for any future production version.

## Ranked findings

### DA-M01 — protected disclosure type is too small at phone scale

- Severity: `major`
- Shots: 03–07
- Evidence: `review/phone-legibility-final/contact-sheet-480.jpg`
- Consequence: the main story remains clear, but a viewer on a small display cannot reliably read every synthetic/evidence qualifier.
- Action: enlarge protected warning/disclosure roles, shorten nonessential microcopy, and retest at 480x270 before any production adaptation.

### DA-M02 — missing reality, operator, dispatch, and outcome assets prevent finish-quality judgment

- Severity: `major / expected rehearsal boundary`
- Shots: 01–05 and 07
- Evidence: conspicuous `AST-*` placeholder slates and `direction/asset-tickets.json`
- Consequence: direction and continuity can be judged, but photographic specificity, human performance, material integration, and final proof legibility cannot.
- Action: do not source in this rehearsal; use the exact tickets in a separately authorized production.

### DA-M03 — rendered container version metadata is unreliable

- Severity: `major technical control`
- Evidence: MP4 tag `hyperframes_version=0.0.0-dev` versus pinned/executed CLI `0.8.4`
- Consequence: an automated handoff must not infer tool version from the container tag.
- Action: retain command/package pin as authority and fix upstream metadata before shared enforcement.

### DA-M04 — proof choreography and completed endpoint are under-held

- Severity: `major`
- Shots: 03 and 07
- Evidence: independent post-correction editorial review, `agents/deliverables/wave3-final-editorial-verification/review.md`
- Consequence: viewers can infer both operations, but cannot comfortably inspect the proof transfer or read the fully completed measurement state during playback.
- Action: reallocate time and hierarchy inside the existing locked duration so the highlighted source field and extracted value receive a readable beat and the complete endpoint holds about 1.8–2.0 seconds.

### DA-O01 — system VO and absent captions are rehearsal-only

- Severity: `observation`
- Evidence: local Samantha synthetic VO; `handoff/CAPTIONS.md`
- Consequence: timing and sequence inference can be tested, but vocal performance and caption hierarchy cannot be creatively approved.

## Post-QA correction record

The initial independent Wave 3 reviews evaluated the first rendered hash and found no blocking failure but identified concrete major issues. The final render resolves the zeroed opening counter (`8:47 PM` now appears), promotes the human hand at the gate, changes the call tag to `BOOKED` when the card becomes scheduled, visibly ties the Shot 06 hand to `AST-OPERATOR-003`, and applies a ceiling-frame policy to Resolve boundaries. ## Recommendation

`REVISE, DO NOT BLOCK THE REHEARSAL CHAIN.` The animatic demonstrates that locked narration and reviewed direction can control a coherent HyperFrames sequence. It is not yet a finished or creatively approved film: proof cadence and the final endpoint hold remain open major findings, secondary protected text needs a phone-size pass, real ticketed material is absent by design, and several downstream controls remain manual.
