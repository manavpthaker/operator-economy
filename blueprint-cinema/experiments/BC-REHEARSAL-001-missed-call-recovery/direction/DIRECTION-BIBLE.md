# Direction Bible · BC-REHEARSAL-001

Status: direction draft  
Sequence: missed-call recovery  
Timing authority: `inputs/vo.wav` + `inputs/words.json`  
Duration: 61.411354 seconds at 30 fps

## Visual thesis

The viewer should understand recovery by watching one physical red call tag survive every state change. It begins as an unanswered after-hours signal, becomes buried paper, is recovered onto a causal rail, pauses under a human hand, and finally clips to an empty work-order card. The film proves the blueprint through the handling of these objects; it never tours a map or explains a dashboard.

The central contrast is not human versus automation. It is unprioritized signal versus bounded routing with visible judgment.

## Reality-world treatment

Reality is warm paper, large physical silhouettes, and specific but anonymous objects: a phone, a red service tag, voicemail sheets, an operator hand, and a work-order card. Asset gaps are conspicuous neutral ticket slates with exact IDs. No fictional office, company name, phone number, customer portrait, or software interface is fabricated. Human action has weight: the phone stops, paper receives the tag, the hand blocks or releases it, and the card remains empty until a booking occurs.

## System-world treatment

The system is one navy recovery rail built left to right. Nodes appear only when the corresponding operation begins. The rail is not a complete world overview: each shot shows the minimum causal neighborhood needed for the current operation. States use short operator language—`CAPTURED`, `UNCERTAIN`, `ACKNOWLEDGED`, `HOLD`, `RELEASE`, `STOP`, `SCHEDULED`—rather than narration sentences. No interface chrome, tabs, menus, dashboards, avatars, or generated platform UI.

## Proof-bench treatment

The proof bench is a paper source object at frame right. The synthetic CSV enters in full context with `SYNTHETIC TEST FIXTURE — NOT EVIDENCE` across its header. One field is highlighted in dark gold, the observed value detaches, and that same value attaches to the red tag. The source then collapses to a pinned proof tab that remains visibly labeled. The source never turns into a clean statistic card and never loses its warning. The final one-week measure shows process endpoints only; it invents no rate, revenue, or performance result.

## Typography roles

- Boska Rehearsal 700: 84–136 px, interpretive phrase or high-level state, at most six words.
- Supreme Rehearsal 500: 34–54 px, operation/state labels.
- Supreme Rehearsal 400: 28–38 px, short supporting language.
- IBM Plex Mono 700: 24–32 px, time, IDs, state metadata, source fields, measurement labels.
- A word already carried by the persistent object stays on that object; it is not repeated as a headline.
- There are no sentence-length subtitles, kinetic transcript blocks, or decorative numbers.

## Surface and texture

Paper uses a static 96 px registration grid at 8% drafting-blue opacity. Navy uses a static 48 px drafting grid at 8% paper opacity. Surfaces are flat, with 2 px rules and no glass, grain, gradients, bloom, soft shadows, or floating panels. A contact shadow is allowed only where the operator hand physically covers the tag or where the tag clips to the card.

## Camera grammar

- Each shot uses a locked authored composition and one primary relationship.
- Cuts change scale or location; cameras do not drift between nouns.
- Shot 6 permits one authored pullback from a close gate fragment to the complete causal recovery rail. It is the only scale-changing camera move.
- No camera flies among boxes, no permanent map inset, and no more than 12% scale change inside the pullback.
- Entry, action, consequence, settle, and exit frames remain separately readable.

## Screen direction

- Recovery moves left to right.
- Customer continuation also exits right, showing independent action without blame.
- Loss moves down into the relationship gap.
- Decline moves down-left to `STOP`; acceptance moves right to the work-order card.
- The red call tag never reverses direction solely to decorate a cut.
- The work-order card holds the right side until booking and then becomes the primary focal object.

## Motion verbs

`ring`, `tag`, `carry`, `fall`, `pin`, `highlight`, `extract`, `attach`, `capture`, `classify`, `acknowledge`, `hold`, `review`, `release`, `offer`, `stop`, `book`, `measure`, `pull_back`, `cut`.

Every tween must map to one of these verbs and to a world-state change. Decorative movement is absent.

## Transition rules

- Hard cut at 13.64, 34.20, 39.88, 47.68, and 56.05 seconds.
- One designed handoff at 26.37 seconds: the backgrounds cut from the broken paper path to navy while the root-level red tag preserves identity, x-direction, and velocity onto `CAPTURE`. This is a relationship-preserving match, not a flourish.
- Maximum designed transitions: one. No transition overlays, shader wipes, blur, zoom-through, bounce, light leaks, or motion used only to create energy.
- Every cut inherits one concrete responsibility from the previous shot and establishes the next operation within 0.4 seconds.

## Sound intent

Locked VO remains dry and intelligible at unity gain. No music. Test-only sound tickets may later supply: one offscreen ring, one voicemail-paper contact, one dry gate stop/release, one suppression thunk, and one paper stamp. These cues must clarify cause and never lead the animation. No whoosh library, riser, cinematic hit, or branded sting. A quiet room/after-hours ambience is ticketed but not added during direction. This animatic may remain VO-only if local synthetic sound would be mistaken for finish.

## Synthetic-fixture labeling

Every full or partial display of `fixture-missed-call-log.csv` must carry the exact visible line `SYNTHETIC TEST FIXTURE — NOT EVIDENCE`. The final proof pin carries the same wording, not an abbreviated icon. Any public-delivery validator must fail if this fixture is treated as evidence, a case study, or a real company record.

2. No Prezi-style movement among boxes.
3. No permanent full-world overview or minimap.
4. No website, CRM, dispatch dashboard, inbox UI, or generated platform interface.
5. No generic glass panels, floating UI, or soft card grid.
6. No constant ambient motion, breathing, shimmer, grain movement, or meaningless liveness.
7. No bouncing, elastic overshoot, spring pop, or playful energy treatment.
8. No text that repeats full narration phrases.
9. No more than one primary focal relationship per shot.
10. No transition whose only job is decoration.
11. No new composition merely because narration changes nouns.
12. No camera drift, orbit, fly-through, or repeated zoom.
13. No generic dark technology or AI imagery.
14. No fictional business, customer, phone number, conversion, revenue, or software screenshot.
15. No unlabeled synthetic source or polished placeholder that resembles approved evidence.
16. No opening polish that is disconnected from the same objects and grammar used later.
17. No duplicate call tag at the outcome fork; one tag proves identity.
18. No booked card before the accepted branch reaches it.

## Continuity invariants

- `call-tag-red` is one root-level DOM object from first appearance through final measure.
- `work-order-card` is one root-level DOM object; its shell and ID never change, only its state field.
- The source pin stays at frame right after its introduction.
- A cut may change surrounding geography but cannot change object color, label, proportions, or route direction.
- Human review must visually block the uncertain route until the operator action releases it.
- Decline must end at `STOP`; no downstream line remains active.

## Approval tests

- Pause any shot at its settle frame: one primary relationship should be obvious without narration notes.
- Mute the VO: the current path, leak, recovery rail, human gate, stop branch, and booked work order must remain distinguishable.
- Hide labels: call-tag and work-order identity must still persist through color, proportion, and location.
- Scale a review frame to 480×270: primary state and synthetic warning remain readable.
- Remove every motion without a named business verb; the intended sequence should be unchanged because there should be none.
