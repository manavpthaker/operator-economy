# Frame direction

## Concept angle

An unanswered ring leaves a physical red service tag in the world. The same tag persists as it falls into a paper voicemail stack, is recovered onto a navy routing rail, pauses under a human hand, and finally stamps an empty work-order card. The system is understood through causal handling of two objects, not through a map tour.

## Palette roles

- Paper `#F5F0E6`: human reality and readable holds.
- Ink `#1A1A1A`: primary type and physical silhouettes.
- Schematic navy `#14263E`: only the active system surface.
- Drafting blue `#1F3A5F`: route geometry and secondary structure.
- Muted brick `#9B3E2E`: the persistent call signal; never alarm-red decoration.
- Sage `#5E7F6A`: booked/resolved outcome only.
- Dark gold `#7A5E24`: proof-field highlight only.

Any frame uses paper + ink/navy + one semantic accent. Synthetic-warning yellow is prohibited; disclosure uses a high-contrast ink-on-paper band so it cannot be mistaken for an editorial highlight.

## Typography roles

- Boska 700, 84–136 px: one short interpretive phrase or section-level idea; never narration transcription.
- Supreme 500, 34–54 px: state names and viewer-facing operational language.
- Supreme 400, 28–38 px: concise supporting copy.
- IBM Plex Mono 700, 24–32 px: time, state, ticket, source, and measurement labels.
- No paragraph longer than two lines. No body text below 28 px. No label below 22 px.

## Composition

- 104 px outer safe margin; 80 px lower caption-safe reserve even though captions are omitted.
- Screen direction is left-to-right for recovery and rightward continuation; downward movement means loss or suppression.
- The call tag is the primary subject until booking; the work-order card becomes primary only at the booking consequence.
- Camera is locked in each shot. Framing changes occur only by cuts, except the single tag-preserving reality-to-system handoff.
- Proof lives at the right proof bench; it never becomes a dashboard or permanent world overview.

## Surface and motion

Paper has a sparse, static registration grid made from local CSS lines at 8% opacity. Navy has a 48 px drafting grid at 8% opacity. There is no grain, glass, glow, gradient, floating panel, or ambient movement. Motion starts only when a business verb starts and settles into readable holds.

## Explicit anti-rules

No slide-deck cards. No Prezi camera. No full-world inset. No dashboard chrome. No generated interface. No narration subtitles as design. No floating glass. No gradients. No constant ambient motion. No bounce or elastic ease. No decorative transitions. No noun-triggered layout reset. No meaningless camera drift. No fake evidence. No unlabeled synthetic content. No competing primary focal points. No polished opener disconnected from the operating model.
