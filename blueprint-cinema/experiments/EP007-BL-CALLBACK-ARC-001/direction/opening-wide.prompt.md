## Prompt

Create one continuous eight-second natural-speed observational film shot from the supplied first
frame to the supplied last frame. Preserve the exact woman, man, faces, ages, hair, reading glasses,
workwear, table, notebook, workshop geometry, practical daylight, focus, and owner-left/buyer-right
screen geography. Lock the chest-height 35mm camera completely.

The male buyer has just asked a serious but ordinary diligence question. He holds the owner's eyeline
with quiet professional attention; his hands and closed notebook remain still. The older woman owner
begins from easy practiced confidence. She starts a natural answer with one restrained hand gesture,
then the thought catches up with her. Her gesture lowers, her mouth closes before she continues, and
her eyes shift once a few degrees past the buyer toward the working shop. She does not resume.

The progression must read as a real thought interrupting a fluent answer, not two people posing. The
owner remains intelligent, capable, and equal. Use ordinary blinks and breaths. No generated audio,
audible dialogue, fifth word, repeated answer, large head turn, nod, sigh, frown, panic, shame, defeat,
confusion, tears, smile, body slump, melodrama, slow motion, camera movement, focus pull, identity drift,
face morphing, extra person, readable writing, logo, handshake, contract, money, sale, or celebration.
