When I built Grapevines for
myself, I already had all my
context. Getting that from
someone else was the big thing.
My first two companies,
I needed a dev team for the
software. Now I build it myself,
and that part's quick.
What takes the time is the
context. The model only has
what you give it,
so the prompt's only half the
job. Take a resume.
It feels like plenty of context,
but it only tells you what
someone did.
It doesn't tell you how they
thought, what mattered to them
or where they're trying to
go next. So Grapevines reads
the resume and then asks about
what it can't tell you.
It's the same if I'm building
an editorial partnership,
a storybook app for kids or
a hospitality marketing service.
When the context is thin,
the output comes out generic,
no matter how good the prompt
is. Think about the first conversation
with someone who's really good
at helping you,
like a good coach or a good
doctor. Most of what they ask
isn't on any form,
and some of it you wouldn't
think to write down.
That's the context your app
has to go get.
Tell me in the comments how
your app gets context from
people.
