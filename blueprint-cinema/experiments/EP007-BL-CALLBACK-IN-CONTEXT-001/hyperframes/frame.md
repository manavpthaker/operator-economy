# Boundary Ledger callback frame

The film carries the human context. Warm ledger paper carries the unresolved operating model. No
graphic overlay is placed on the generated scenes, and no type repeats the narration. The rough
model keeps short overlapping pencil strokes, broken contours, cross-hatching, uneven proportions,
and enough negative space to read at phone scale. Its owner-to-business dependency receives one
short oxide overtrace only after the full model has formed; the mark identifies the active thesis
path rather than decorating the frame.

The default transition is a hard cut. The visual energy comes from the owner's stopped answer, the
model's causal reveal, the buyer's physical binder action, and one motivated face cut-in—not camera
drift or decorative motion.
