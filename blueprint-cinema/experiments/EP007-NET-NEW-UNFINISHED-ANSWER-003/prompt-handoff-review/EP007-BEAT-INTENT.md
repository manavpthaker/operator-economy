## What the film is about

A capable operator is being asked to test preparation as a separate, fixed-fee job before a small-business
transaction exists. The business being inspected is the human situation carrying that argument,
not the protagonist business the viewer is being asked to build. The practice must make what can
be inspected explicit without promising a sale. The economics and demand remain unproven.

Three roles must never collapse into one generic person: the practice operator does preparation;
the client owner buys the engagement; the acquiring buyer inspects a business.
