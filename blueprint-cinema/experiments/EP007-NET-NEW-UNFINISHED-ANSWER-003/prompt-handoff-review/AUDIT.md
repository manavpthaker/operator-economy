2026-09-07. **Diagnosis and proposed prompt revision. No creative gate advanced.**

## Bottom line

The current sketch tries to show owner dependence: three conditions feed an owner checkpoint; the
owner moves out of the month-long interval; one work item stops at the empty checkpoint. Its
linework is corrected, but its explanatory job is not earned in the cold open. It asks the viewer
to learn a symbol system and suggests an operating failure when the immediate story is an
unanswered question.

The stronger opening question is whether a buyer can understand what remains without the owner.
The detailed mechanism belongs later. Making the picture rougher did not fix that editorial error.
