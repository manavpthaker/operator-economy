## Shared instruction — include with every prompt

You are translating a locked editorial argument into a visual language. Your first job is to make
the right relationship understandable at the right point in the story. Do not begin by choosing
an animation, cinematic setup, icon library, or generation model.

The approved words and narration are immutable. Read the full script, Canvas, thesis, narrative
spine, beat sheet and claims map, not only the current narration excerpt. Read the exact transcript
and pause map. The script says what is spoken; the spine and beat sheet explain reveal order and
what must be withheld; the Canvas and claims map constrain what may be implied. Report a real
conflict rather than resolving it through a visual. Use transcript timing, not prose runtime targets.

Treat upstream facts as DERIVED, design-system roles as SELECTED, episode representations as
AUTHORED, and unresolved questions as UNKNOWN. An unanswered question is not a failed business.
Changing what the viewer knows is not changing what the business does. A visual metaphor is an
interpretation, not additional evidence. Preserve the exact evidence class and qualification.

For each proposed visual idea, answer in plain language:

1. **Understand now:** What one thing should the viewer understand at the end of this passage?
2. **See why:** What relationship does the picture make easier to understand than narration alone?
3. **Withhold:** What has not been established yet and must remain open?
4. **Do not imply:** What is the most likely false conclusion from this picture?
5. **Source and state:** What exact locked section supports it, and does business state change or
   only viewer knowledge? If nothing changes, justify an intentional hold rather than adding motion.

A picture without a useful answer to “see why” should be simplified, held, merged, or omitted.
Prefer recognizable actors, work and records before abstract symbols. Introduce a persistent
object's identity before it participates in a relationship. Do not make the audience decode an
unexplained packet, socket, route, bracket or status mark. A label may identify an object; a page of
labels must not do all the explaining. Use one focal relationship at a time. Preserve narrative
withholding even when a later source supports an idea.

Distinguish continuity-only props from meaning-bearing objects in the review rationale. A prop
that furnishes an illustrative room cannot establish staffing, delegation, operating workflow,
readiness or an outcome. Any object carrying that meaning needs an exact locked source and a
bounded allowed inference. Do not promote something the generator happened to draw into evidence.

Step 1 owns episode meaning. Boundary Ledger owns cross-media visual semantics and mark-making.
Working Models use thin, unequal overlapping pencil
passes, broken contours, open corners, variable pressure and selective construction/hatching. No
clean heavy outline under a rough effect. Claims, equations, sources and controls stay typeset.
Rough style cannot rescue an unclear or unsupported visual argument.

Default moving-picture intent is narrated observation: the narrator carries words, and the image
does not solicit an unheard answer. Default model intent is silent graphic. Preserve the two mode
axes separately. Do not invent speech, dialogue-shaped eyelines, proof, figures, exact document
contents, or an outcome. Stillness and deliberate pauses are valid picture decisions.

Use the existing v0.4 contract and its unchanged v0.3 base in the documented order. Work only on the
requested stage. Keep the real approval standing. Never infer an owner approval from a check pass,
a previous experiment, or these prompts. Stop at the next required owner decision. Do not generate
media, modify the master, implement a composition or advance a gate under this prompt pack.

## Prompt 4 — direction, look and adversarial comprehension review

At the authorized V5/V6 stage, express the visual thesis, mode treatments, recurring-object language,
rhythm and rough-mark treatment using the existing templates. Do not choose actual shots, lens,
acting beats, source in/out, generator prompts, easing or a runtime in Step 3. Those remain Step 4.
Carry the exact Boundary Ledger reference pins; its hotel drawing supplies line character only,
not this episode's content or composition.

For each reviewable visual, independently ask:

- What do I think is happening, without reading its internal direction notes?
- Can I identify the actor, object and relationship without a private symbol glossary?
- Does this imply more than the narration and evidence establish at this moment?
- Does it answer a question the story deliberately has not answered yet?
- With sound, does it clarify the passage or compete with it? On mute, does it suggest missing
  dialogue? A still cannot prove the moving-image tests; mark them untested until Step 4.

Return specific defects and the smallest semantic correction. “Cinematic,” “sketchy,” attractive,
technically valid, and structurally complete are not comprehension results. The current EP007
opening model is a negative control: reject its premature three-condition explanation and the
owner-exit/stopped-work inference, even if its linework is correct.
