## Alternatives not selected

- Ties shorten, break or fade when she leaves: reads as customers lost. The script says nobody knows.
- Customers walk away or toward the business: resolves the uncertainty either way.
- A question mark over the customers: labels the uncertainty instead of letting the empty endpoint show it.
- Ties to the business with a detour through the owner: adds a route without clarifying the point.
- Presenter for this line: R47 just returned to the presenter; the dependence is spatial and shows better in the model.

## Limits

Customer count and loyalty are illustrative, not evidence. The picture must not imply customers leave. Technical checks do not establish comprehension or acceptance. No paid calls; no canonical gate change.
