## Risk

A handshake can read as a deal being agreed. His regretful expression, the narration (“or he walks”) and the rise straight after carry the meaning; review in context for that misreading. Kling misses timing cues in both earlier takes, so the handshake may land early or late; select the source offset after playback without speed changes.
