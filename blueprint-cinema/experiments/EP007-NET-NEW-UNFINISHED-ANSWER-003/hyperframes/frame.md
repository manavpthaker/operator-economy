# Frame direction — The Unfinished Answer, revision C

## Governing idea

This opening has two materials joined by one causal carrier:

1. **Observed reality:** a restrained, warm business interior organized around one work surface.
2. **The Working Model:** the same page plane and route mark become a rough, accountable explanation.

The cut between them is motivated. One ordinary work item routes back to the owner, her pencil stops at a post-authored checkpoint, and the viewer is carried into the page to see what that dependency means. The generated page remains free of semantic content; a nonlinguistic route mark is composited before the cut, and the authored model supplies the language after it.

## Observed-reality frame

- One woman owner and one buyer share a worktable in an established operating business.
- The space shows ordinary competence: maintained tools, stock, records, or work in progress. It must not look distressed, chaotic, luxurious, or staged as a corporate boardroom.
- The owner is the visual center of gravity without being isolated as a victim.
- The buyer is attentive and procedural, not predatory or performative.
- Continuity anchors are the same pale table, deep-mineral binder, blank warm sheet, pencil, keys, and one plain unprinted work ticket.
- Wardrobe and light stay understated and natural. Avoid glossy commercial polish, shallow-focus glamour, or overt “cinematic” haze.
- The generated ivory sheet and work ticket remain free of semantic content. One rough route mark is composited onto the sheet in post; the binder never exposes fabricated financial details.
- The observable demonstration is simple: one work ticket returns to the owner while the pencil follows as a pointer, then stops.

Generated plates are still animatic placeholders. Hold them as composed frames. Do not create motion by drifting, warping, or continuously pushing the stills. The only reframe is the bounded `00:26.80–00:27.92` approach used to cross media.

## Page-plane match

- The physical sheet and its post-authored route mark fill the frame on a finite Z-forward move.
- Boundary Ledger paper replaces it on one frame at `00:27.92`.
- Both page planes share the same center, dominant warm value, route mark, scale, and Z-forward direction at the cut.
- The incoming paper briefly preserves the motion sign, then decelerates and settles.
- No dissolve, flash, generic blur, texture wash, or faux film-burn bridges the two materials.
- The seam is allowed to be perceptible: observed reality becoming an authored model is the narrative event.

## Working Model frame

The model is a back-of-the-napkin operating sketch, not architectural drafting and not a polished infographic.

- Start with the equation, not unlabeled boxes.
- Give `= ?` enough blank paper to become the focal unresolved state.
- Arrange three distinct rough objects around the equation: an incomplete procedure sheet for `UNDOCUMENTED PROCESS`, an uneven person-to-person link for `OWNER-HELD RELATIONSHIPS`, and a closed or incomplete record stack for `RECORDS A BUYER CANNOT VERIFY`.
- Route each condition with perimeter steel so dependency is visible, not merely stated.
- Keep the three objects widely separated. They should read as different problems, not a dense card grid.
- Preserve those exact objects when the view widens into the business envelope.
- Build contours from short, individually placed strokes.
- Let neighboring strokes overlap, miss, and overshoot.
- Keep some corners open and some parallel lines visibly imperfect.
- Show one or two correction passes where the maker reconsidered a relationship.
- Use selective cross-hatching to indicate load, not uniform texture.
- Keep proportions uneven but recognizable.
- Never apply one global wobble or roughening filter to otherwise perfect geometry.
- Preserve blank paper around the model so the unresolved relationship has air.

The problem state contains the three owner-dependent conditions and perimeter-steel routes. Nothing collapses. The final state adds one incomplete core-oxide path and stops before resolution.

## Composition

- Primary content stays inside a 12% horizontal and 10% vertical safe area.
- One focal relationship per moment, even when three objects share the settled model.
- Human shots use negative space to make the operating sheet, single ticket, and one rough route mark legible.
- The equation should occupy a dominant region; the three conditions use the remaining field with clear separation and no mini-label system.
- Model passages use a broad horizontal field, with no dense dashboard, panel grid, repeated boxes, or four-column cadence.
- The accountable card or title may overlap only blank illustration margin; it may not conceal a meaningful stroke or human action.

## Motion

- Default edit is a hard cut.
- Within observed reality, motion comes from a physical event in eventual footage: reviewed page turns, binder shifts, sheet is revealed, one ticket routes, pencil stops.
- The page-plane approach is finite, begins after the spoken stop at `00:26.80`, and ends at the hard match at `00:27.92`.
- Within model passages, reveal one relationship at a time through finite draw-on or replacement actions, then settle.
- Draw-ons follow the rough source strokes; they do not trace a clean hidden master.
- No ambient loops, idle motion, perpetual line boil, breathing scale, generic camera push, automatic parallax, or kinetic type flourish.
- The decisive holds are the arrested pencil and the unresolved `?`. The rest of the frame must stay still enough for both to read.

## Sound relationship

- The locked narration carries language.
- Human images carry action and consequence, not dialogue.
- Graphics carry bounded explanation and must remain intelligible on mute.
- Preserve the `44.50–48.52` narration break. Do not fill it with generic music, explanatory copy, or an unapproved sting.
- Any future ambience or foley must name its visible cause and sit beneath the narration.

## Prohibited appearance

- smooth vector contours disguised as pencil;
- evenly weighted line systems;
- generic blueprint grids;
- fabricated readable documents, page contents, or figures;
- random gaze, lip motion, or conversational reaction coverage;
- source boxes, claim IDs, and LLM-style annotations;
- unexplained boxes, four-week/work-unit arithmetic, dollar math, valuation, price, multiple, or `= 0`;
- illustrative collapse, sale closing, or guaranteed outcome;
- a finished oxide solution before the episode has taught the practice.
