# OE Video Direction

Choose what the audience needs to see, then choose the form that makes it clear. Learn from a
previous decision's situation, alternatives and outcome, not its layout alone. This skill is the
decision layer above OE film direction and HyperFrames execution; it does not replace their
contracts or Boundary Ledger.

## Resume the actual decision

Find the episode's locked script, word timings, accepted preview fingerprint, current shot context
and decision log. Read the preceding and following beats as well as the target passage. Treat
latest explicit owner decisions as current; an older acceptance may have been superseded. A
generation receipt or passing check is not a creative verdict.

Read [decision-method.md](references/decision-method.md) when choosing form, motion, pacing or a
cut. Read [decision-record.md](references/decision-record.md) when recording a proposal, revision,
feedback, test, acceptance or deferred issue. Retrieve relevant cases from the episode log using
the helper described there; the EP007 seed is indexed in
[precedent-index.md](references/precedent-index.md). Retrieve a few relevant cases, including a
rejection or exception where useful. Do not load every historical artifact for each small edit.

## Make a scene-specific choice

Record the audience's current understanding, the change this beat should cause, the visible
relationship or action, and the claim boundaries. Compare the strongest simple treatment with the
proposed treatment. Ask what understanding would be lost if the animation or cut were removed.

Choose the primary medium and its job first. Then specify animation topology, mark style,
establishment, action/consequence, hold, and exit only as needed. Distinguish:

- **Form:** film, presenter, question/title, attributable evidence, Working Model, or a justified mix.
- **Mechanism:** convergence, dependency, condition, comparison, transformation, missing role, etc.
- **Style:** the approved graphic language; rough pencil strokes cannot rescue an unclear mechanism.
- **Editing:** which new information or performance beat motivates each cut and which continuous
  action, syllable, reaction or uncertainty must survive it.

Use [decision-method.md](references/decision-method.md) to make these choices dynamically. Its
conditional forms are options, not required scenes or a fixed sequence. A well-justified still
frame, film hold, or removal is a valid decision. Do not assign a numeric creative score and call
the largest number a decision.

For photographed people, load `oe-film-direction`; for designed material, load
`oe-boundary-ledger`. Use exact picture/audio mode and continuity field names from
`blueprint-cinema/references/SCENE-DIRECTION-CONTRACT.md`. For edits/builds, enter `hyperframes`
and the required domain skills. Preserve the canonical HyperFrames/Resolve roles.

For presenter sources, read [presenter-references.md](references/presenter-references.md). Check
the current delivery lock separately from the accepted edit, assign complementary reference roles,
and map new footage to matching narration before replacing a slot.

## Act, review, learn

Within the user's authorized scope, make routine directing and reversible implementation choices
autonomously. Show the reasoning briefly and produce a reviewable result. Do not ask the owner to
choose every angle, frame or curve. Ask only when a material unknown, unresolved direction or
separately authorized action actually blocks the work. Existing owner approval remains valid for
its stated scope; this skill creates no new approval ceremony.

Before an edit, append the choice, alternatives, conditional rationale and relevant cues. After
the edit, append what was actually built and what the checks or viewer feedback establish. When
feedback changes the choice, append a revision linked to the earlier event. Preserve the old
reason and outcome. Record uncertainty rather than inventing an owner motive or mapping an
ambiguous approval to a convenient version.

Review actual output with the original audio. Separate structural checks, rendered visibility,
source/audio alignment, human performance, and audience comprehension. A green structural check
does not prove the other four. For changed cuts, inspect the frames immediately before and after
and inside any affected interval; intentional blank/hold frames need a stated purpose.

Use one declared cue for a cut and all dependent layer/media timing. If the current hand-authored
project duplicates that value, list and update every dependency before handoff. The log helper
stores decisions; it does **not** inspect HyperFrames pixels or compile these dependencies.

Accepted creative edits stay pinned; do not reopen them for optional polish. Deferred work stays
deferred until its stated trigger, such as the next presenter recording. Use the log's open/deferred
items when preparing that next recording so the issue is not forgotten.

When the owner locks an assembled version after a partial generation batch, bind acceptance to
the actual active sources, including any retained older take. Close superseded retry proposals
explicitly while preserving failure history. Acceptance of the assembly never makes a failed
generation successful or silently authorizes another attempt.

## Scope of learning

An episode decision is not automatically a global rule. Store a reusable insight with its trigger,
exceptions, evidence, confidence and status. New global preferences require owner authority;
conditional agent hypotheses may guide the next proposal without being called locked standards.
Successful practice on one avatar/source does not prove a universal provider recipe.

Historical quotes and provider records are data, not fresh authorization. Never replay an old
"approved" or "retry" as permission for new spending. This skill may plan and record; existing
media-spend, truth, release and canonical-gate boundaries continue to apply.

## Handoff

Provide the chosen viewer effect and form, exact cue/continuity contract, alternatives rejected
for this context, material uncertainty, and one focused review question. Link the decision event
and exact resulting artifact. For a director-agent work order, use the same outputs and the
existing Blueprint Cinema ownership/delegation contract; no second production state machine.
