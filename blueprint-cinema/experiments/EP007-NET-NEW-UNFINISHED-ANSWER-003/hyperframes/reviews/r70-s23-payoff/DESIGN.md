# S23 design

One thesis, then a brief recall of the model. The title owns the upper half of the frame; two related values occupy the lower half with their assumptions attached.

1280×720, 24 fps. Paper #F5F0E6; mineral #173530; supporting steel #33464C. Locally bundled Boska 700 for title and values, Supreme 400/500 for labels. No accent is needed: this beat introduces no new commitment or verified result.

The title starts composed and stays still. Reveal the complete model group at frame 90 over six frames. Its footer travels with its visibility, never leaving unsupported values on screen. No photographic treatment, diagram, gradient, decorative texture, pan or zoom.

Keep title at 102px, values at 68px, main qualifiers at 25px and the long repeated model disclosure at 21px. The footer repeats the accepted S20 binding; the essential assumption and volume caveats remain larger beside their values. Preserve roomy margins and no text overlap.
