# Frame direction

Warm cream, ink navy, oxidized brass, and muted olive. Typography is restrained editorial grotesk with tabular timing where needed. Rules are 2–3 px at 1080p and never depend on hairlines. Labels sit in protected negative space, use opaque backing only as a narrow matte, and occupy less than one fifth of the frame. The key-tag match element is a worn brass rounded rectangle with one chamfered corner, one circular hole near the left edge, and one vertical scratch near the right third.
