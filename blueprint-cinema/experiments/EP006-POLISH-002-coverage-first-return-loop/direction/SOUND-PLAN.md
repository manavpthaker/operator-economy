# Sound plan

The locked mono narration remains sample-accurate on A1. The mix is built as an observational sound arc rather than corporate uplift.

- Checkout: low reception room tone, fabric movement, desk contact, restrained key/object clink.
- Permission: room tone narrows; paper and pen detail become legible without competing with words.
- Context/relevance: quiet daylight interior with page handling and distant operational movement.
- Suppression: ambience drops briefly and a short dry stop/catch sound makes the halt decisive, not alarming.
- Human review: paper placement and apron/fabric movement re-open the physical room.
- Return: door/room tone begins before picture, then footstep/luggage and desk contact resolve the bridge.

No music is selected by default because the intended emotional movement comes from location-specific ambience and performed foley; a bed will be added only if a local, rights-cleared source supports restraint without generic uplift. This is an authored finishing choice, not an inventory default.

Fairlight targets: dialogue remains natural and fully intelligible; approximately -14 to -16 LUFS integrated; true peak at or below -1 dBTP; no clipped samples, unintended silence, cut discontinuity, or constant whoosh layer.
