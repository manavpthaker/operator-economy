## What works

The decisive improvement is coverage. The review is predominantly live-action footage, uses hard cuts, and alternates a reception wide/medium world with hand, bell, paper, phone, and counter inserts. The labels are generally small and subordinate. The one full-frame system interruption is short and visually restrained. The guest and principal operator remain identifiable through the central permission/direct-action section. The neutral `FIRST STAY / OTA` treatment correctly avoids villainizing the acquisition platform.

Those strengths are not enough for a creative pass because the images repeatedly describe generic reception transactions rather than the specific beginning-to-end actions the narration requires. The cut's final five seconds are a hotel form being signed under a large `NEXT APPROPRIATE STAY / DIRECT` confirmation. The label supplies the interpretation; the action itself does not demonstrate a later return, recognition, a repaired direct booking, or receipt of the persistent key tag.

## Creative diagnosis

The cut has solved the previous format problem more than it has solved the story problem. It is no longer five opaque motion-graphic panels. It is now a 13-clip stock-footage sequence with modular graphics. But the selected footage comes from one staged reception shoot whose available actions are arrival, bell, phone, form, and desk interaction. Editorial labels have been asked to turn those generic actions into permission, memory, qualification, return, and direct booking.

The result fails at exactly the two objects/actions that should carry the causal loop:

1. The key tag is not photographed. It appears as an illustrative brass rectangle over a silver bell, then disappears instead of changing hands and returning at the end.
2. The later-stay outcome is not photographed. The guest never visibly leaves one stay, returns after a meaningful separation, re-enters, is recognized, receives the same key tag, or completes a privacy-safe direct-booking action. The final label asserts a direct confirmation over another form-signing shot.

The continuity is also the continuity of a generic contemporary reception rather than the specified independent inn: beige modern walls, hotel clocks, reception signage, fashion-forward guest, burgundy blazer, and COVID-era masks. It is coherent stock continuity, but it does not achieve the production-world target of warm wood, cream linen, worn brass, natural window light, dark-olive guest layer, and cream-shirt/navy-apron operator.

## Minimum correction required for another review

This version should not be promoted by polishing labels or adding more graphic explanation. A creative re-review needs replacement moving coverage for the causal spine:

- A readable checkout/key-tag handoff in the first four seconds, with the specified practical tag visible in a human hand.
- A genuinely voluntary permission action whose start and completion read without the word `PERMISSION`.
- A physical human-review choice that clearly selects or suppresses one neutral item after relevance/qualification.
- A later-return shot with an unmistakable entry/approach beat, not people already standing inside the same reception setup.
- A final welcome/key-tag/door/direct-confirmation action that demonstrates the outcome before any label appears.
- Wardrobe and environment coverage closer to the locked independent-inn world.

If those moving shots cannot be acquired under the current rights/capability limits, the truthful final status is `PARTIAL`, not a creative full-pass.
