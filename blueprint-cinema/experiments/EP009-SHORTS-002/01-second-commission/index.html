A year later the same

guest books the same room,

through the same website.

And she pays the site

the same commission she paid

the first time.

For a guest she already

knows by name.

There's a service a small

inn would pay for hiding

inside that second commission.

So what did that second

stay actually cost her, and

who gets paid to stop

it?
