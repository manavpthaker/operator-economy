# Design specification

## Viewer effect

The viewer should understand within two seconds that software reduced drafting cost without
removing the harder hospitality judgment. The following fourteen seconds prove the machine's work
inside a real native tool. The close withholds the human decision rules and points to EP009.

## Portrait system

- 1080 by 1920 at 24 fps.
- Full-height picture stages. Landscape source is deliberately reframed around the current proof;
  there is no centered 16:9 card or empty paper gutter.
- Dynamic orientation labels sit near the top. Verbatim 64 px phrase captions sit at y=1410,
  inside x=70..930.
- Mineral ink (`#204440`) supplies structure; oxide (`#b5482f`) marks only the unresolved human
  decision and the CTA.
- The truth strip persists: `FICTIONAL FIXTURE · AWAITING HUMAN REVIEW · NO SENDING AVAILABLE`.

## Motion and cuts

- Hard cuts follow the frame-snapped EDL.
- The hook cuts from the machine side to the human side on the sentence reversal.
- Tool crops use one restrained, deterministic focus move per shot. The native interface remains
  the subject; there is no invented cursor, vendor dashboard, send event, success result, or ambient
  decoration.
- The CTA reveals in two steps, then settles for reading.
