## Intent

Build one representative Boundary Ledger motion sequence against the locked EP007 narration. The
test must show the delivery work in one persistent world: baseline, interview, documentation,
record cleanup, customer ownership, owner dependency, transfer or documentation, package assembly,
and walkthrough.

This is test evidence, not a Step 3 gate pass or a production visual approval.

## Assets

- `.media/audio/voice/voice_001.wav` — 28-second mono excerpt of locked EP007 narration master v4,
  source time 699.50–727.50 seconds.
- `assets/fonts/` — local Boundary Ledger and Working Model fonts.
- `assets/vendor/gsap.min.js` — local deterministic motion runtime; no render-time network.

## Customizations

- Use a single continuous 16:9 ModelField; no cuts or camera motion.
- Preserve recognizable owner, business-part, checklist, record, customer, dependency, and package
  objects as the focal point moves through the model.
- Use rough, independently authored paths with open corners, overshoots, doubled marks, and variable
  pressure. Do not add wobble, jitter, or ambient motion after a mark lands.
- Keep labels, identity, and the system-gap notice typeset. Handwriting belongs only inside the
  Working Model.
- No music or sound effects. The locked voice is the only clock.
