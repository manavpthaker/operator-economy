# EP007 Boundary Ledger motion test — frame specification

## Semantic materials

- Paper / human context: `#F5F0E6`; raised paper: `#FBF8F1`; inset paper: `#EDE7D8`.
- Mineral / accountable records: `#204440`; deep mineral: `#173530`.
- Steel / existing dependency and current route: `#586D74`; dark steel: `#33464C`.
- Oxide / one active correction or owned path: `#B5482F`.
- Sage and risk are absent because this passage establishes neither a verified result nor a sourced
  negative exception.

## Registers

- Typeset authority: BL Supreme for identity and records; BL Zodiak for one closing statement only.
- Working Model: Caveat for sparse object notes; every basic relationship must remain legible without
  reading those notes.
- Rough paths are fixed authored geometry. Lines may stop short, overshoot, remain open, double back,
  and vary in width. No procedural wobble or post-landing movement.

## Composition

- 1920×1080, warm paper field, fixed camera.
- One thin identity docket at the top. It orients the internal test; it is not a persistent HUD.
- Owner and owner-held business parts occupy the left; the checklist and written support occupy the
  center; the review/package surface occupies the right.
- The same objects travel through the field. The focal point changes through contrast and causal
  movement, not through scene replacement.

## Motion law

The voice supplies every cue. Each action reveals a relationship or changes a state, then stops.
Maximum concurrency is one meaningful action plus its immediate consequence. This composition uses
the `svg-path-draw`, `control-target-sync`, and `nudge-curve` HyperFrames mechanisms under Boundary
Ledger timing and semantic constraints; package collection borrows the physical assembly pattern
from `grid-card-assemble` while remaining semantically marked `UNBOUND`.

No blur, glow, bounce, elastic ease, ambient drift, decorative pulse, or transition effect.
